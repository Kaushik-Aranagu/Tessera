# Receipts and bests: one copy pass across the sheets (Design item 62)

**Kaushik's principle, in one line:** Tess's line and the page never disagree, and every receipt names the thing first, then what happened.

Research 126 (`docs/research/copy-one-voice.md`, master) read every new line in sheets 41 to 57 against the voice and the house rules; Product decided the shape of a receipt, the word "best", the rest day and the planned session (`decisions.md` 3:15 AM, 3:05 AM, 2:53 AM PT; RF15 999df84). This sheet is Design's one answer: each line below is now the line, and each touched sheet carries a two-line addendum pointing here. Recordings keep their old words until a sheet is next re-cut; builders build from these lines, not from the clips.

## The rules the pass applies
1. **A receipt is the thing, a comma, then what happened.** "Call mom, done.", "Tacos, 2 added.", "Dune, finished.", "Rest on Sundays, kept.", "12 things, forgotten.", "Clean the bathroom, done." Never a verb first. UNDO under it reverts exactly what the line names.
2. **"Best", never "record"** (Review, the Day log, the lens, summaries): "a best, a first, the week's top count".
3. **One rest day, worded once.** Hero "Rest today", context "Legs A waits for tomorrow" (fixed days: "Legs A waits for Thursday", no "next"), Tess "Rest today. Legs A waits for tomorrow.", UNDO "Legs A, back on today." A planned rest day's context is "Push A tomorrow". Nothing else says rest in any other words.
4. **A planned session beats a rest day:** hero "Push A at 7 pm" with `START PUSH A`, never "Rest day" beside a START key.
5. **Digits** for counts everywhere, heroes included: "3 sessions", "4 hours free", "9 to get, for tacos and 2 more". Research updates tess-voice; no hero exception.
6. **Clock times** in sentences and heroes as "6 pm", "7:40 pm"; `6 PM` only in label case. The bare hour goes ("Tacos at 7 pm", "Push A at 6 pm"); a hero that no longer fits one line wraps to two in the fitting form.
7. **Dates** as "Oct 12", "Nov 1" on screen and in sheet prose.
8. **No "…", no " · " outside a pack size, no "Home" on screen** (the page is Today).
9. **Context lines never repeat a word the page already shows** (home-lines §2): no "the week so far" under "so far", no "morning" under a morning label, no "tonight" under "Tacos tonight".
10. **What leaves the phone is said one way:** "Titles and counts only. Never notes, places, health or money."
11. **Settings lines in Tess's colour speak as Tess:** first person. "I ask Claude when unsure, for Plan my day and Today.", "I stay on this phone."
