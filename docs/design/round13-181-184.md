# Items 181 to 184: Round 13, writing surface, hands-free cook, twins, Soon sweep (Oct 23)

Loop round 13 (10-minute cadence, power saver). Lane named per item. Each behind its own Review
switch, default Off. Grammar from 127 and 131.

## 181 The writing surface (lane: UI and UX, switch notesWriteV2, Builder B)
The note editor itself, not the page around it.
- A bare page: the title in the large role, the body in the reading role (Figtree), no toolbar.
  The keyboard accessory is plain words that appear only when useful: "Link" after "@", "Done".
- "@" opens a quiet list of things by name; the chosen word is violet (Notes accent) with a link
  underline in its own app's accent (A8). Private things offer only "Private".
- A line starting with "- " becomes a bullet that is a plain dash; no filled dots. Tab indents.
- Type "?" at the line start to find (158) without leaving the note. Reduce Motion: no animation.

## 182 Cook hands-free (lane: UI and UX with AI, switch cookHandsFreeV2, Builder A cook screens + Core)
Wet hands: the cook page works without touching small targets.
- The whole lower half of the screen is one "Next" area; a tap anywhere there goes on. The screen
  stays on while cooking. Steps stay in the large role, one at a time.
- Say "next", "back" or "how long": on-device speech only, nothing stored, never sent to Claude.
  Tess answers "How long" with the timer in words ("6 min left on the rice.").
- A timer ending is a single soft haptic and the word in ink, never an alarm sound unless the
  phone is not silent (existing KitchenTimerAlerts rules).

## 183 Twins (lane: data and architecture, switch twinsV2, Core with Builder B)
Two objects that look like the same thing get one quiet offer to join.
- Core finds twins by class, normalised name and shared links ("Black bean tacos" twice). Never
  across private/walled things, and never between a private and a non-private object.
- Offer in Review only, as one line: "2 look the same: Black bean tacos." with "Look" and "Not
  these". Look shows both side by side in plain lines; "Join" keeps the older, moves links and
  history across, and is undoable for 10 min (164). "Not these" is remembered.
- Never automatic. Never offered for money objects.

## 184 The Soon sweep (lane: UI and UX, switch soonSweepV2, Builder B)
Once a week, Tasks offers to sort Soon.
- One task at a time in the large role; swipe right = Today, left = Later, down = Done, up =
  stays Soon. Soft tap on each. The count above rolls down ("7 left").
- Finish: "Soon is sorted. 3 for today." Skipped the week: nothing is said.
- Tess is not the voice; the offer is one ink-2 line on the Tasks page on Sunday.
