# Item 128: Training Today, live-set words and the Stats sentence
From the Training brainstorm (Oct 22) and Kaushik's answer 08:16: "mostly at the gym or at
[a] light home gym". Character per sheet 127 (Training: hot accent, big number leads).

## 1. Today: Place knob and minutes word
- Place is a two-value knob on the title line, right side: "Gym" | "Home" (sheet 119 s12).
  Ink for the current value, the other at 30% ink. Swipe sideways or tap. Default Gym; the
  app remembers the last one. Home narrows the routine to the home equipment set; the Plan
  is never rewritten (switching back restores it). One line under the subtitle says what
  changed only when something was swapped: "3 swapped for home".
- Minutes is a value word inside the subtitle: "Push A, about 45 min". Tap or swipe the
  number to step it: 25, 35, 45, All. Rows that drop out fade to 30% ink in place (they do
  not vanish), so he sees what was cut. "All" is the default.
- No second dial on Today. No dots, no separators.
- Primary action: "Start" with the hot underline.

## 2. Live set: three quiet words
- Row under the big number: "Busy" (left), the ink action "Log 6" (centre-left, hot
  underline), "Short" (right). Busy and Short are ink-secondary words, no underline.
- Busy: the exercise moves to the end, the next one slides up; receipt "Bench Press, later."
  with Undo (10 s). At the end, if still busy, the page offers Similar.
- Short: steps the minutes the same way as Today, mid-session; receipt "Cut to 35 min."
- Next time: the hint line under the title reads "Next time 82.5 x 6. Last week 82.5 x 5."
  Plain words, ink-secondary, never a badge. Shown only when the phone has two or more
  sessions of that exercise. It is a suggestion; the big number stays what he types.
- Plates line: "kg, plates 20 + 10 + 2.5 each side" (no middle dot).

## 3. Stats sentence
- First line under the title, in the subtitle role, at most 10 words, two clauses max:
  "Stronger on every lift since July. Legs are behind."
- Computed on the phone from the ledger; never sent to Claude; names lifts and muscle
  groups only, never body weight, health or calories.
- Words: "stronger", "steady", "behind", "best"; never "record", never a percent.
- The one lit thing below it (sheet 127) is the item the sentence names ("Legs" tally in
  the hot colour).
