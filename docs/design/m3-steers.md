# M3 screen steers ("Make it mine")

One paragraph per Feature screen in `docs/product/ship-list-m3.md`, the same weight as the kitchen steers. Rough is fine; the UI bar is not optional: Inter Tight tiers, nothing under 16pt, no italics, no cards, pills or stock lists, painterly ground with hairline seams, directional entrances.

**1 · Body weight.** A light page, one screen tall. The hero is the **7-day average** as a numeral (80 Light, tabular) with "kg" at 21, and a context line above it: "78.4 this morning, steady for two weeks". Under it, the 90-day trend as one painted brushstroke (the exercise page's `PaintedTrend`, in sage, with a wet tip at today), and daily entries as small sage dabs along it rather than a chart with axes. Logging is the ghost row ("bw 78.4"), plus one ink key "Log today" that opens the weight dial (`WeightDial` at 0.1 kg detents, preset to yesterday's value) with hold to log. No goal line and no red; the copy states change in time, never judgement ("down 0.6 since September"). Living moment: today's dab drops onto the stroke and the stroke re-draws its last stretch.

**6 · Class inspector.** This is the "OS" screen, so it should feel like inspecting a part, not like Settings. The path in labels at the top ("THINGS › EXERCISE › BARBELL LIFT"), each segment a link up the tree. The hero is the class name. Below it, a context line in words: "42 objects, 3 subclasses, inherits from Exercise". **Fields** are a column of rows: name at 21/500, type in labels on the right ("MASS", "LINK → MUSCLE"). Inherited fields sit at 50% with a small "FROM EXERCISE" label, own fields at full ink, separated by a single `Seam` where inheritance changes. **Traits** are words in a line (not chips) with the painted carriage under none. **Subclasses** are names at 21 that zoom into their own inspector (z goes down the tree, back goes up). "Add a field" is the last row, in cobalt if Tess suggests one, else ink; tapping it slides in one row that is the form: a name field and a type word that cycles on tap, committed as a ghost row with Undo. Living moment: a new field writes itself into the column with the glyph reveal (`RevealText`), and the instance count above ticks.

**9 · What Tess did.** A receipt, read top to bottom like a day's ledger. Context line "Today, 6 things"; the hero is a sentence ("I changed six things today"). Each op is one row: a cobalt dab, what she did in plain words at 21 ("Moved the dentist to Friday"), the time in labels, and **Undo** in vermilion text on the right; an undone row gets the ink strike and stays struck. Ops that came from a capture show the phrase under them at 18 in context colour ("you said “dentist friday”"). Swipe x for yesterday (the day stroke's grammar). No icons, no grouping headers beyond time of day as Title words in the margin ("Morning", "Afternoon"), like aisle words. Living moment: undoing runs the strike, and the thing it undid briefly flashes its cobalt wash wherever it's visible (the soul's hand-off motion, reversed).

**12 · Settings.** Not a stock Form. One page, sections as Title words in the margin (the aisle-word pattern): **Units**, **Your gym**, **Rest**, **Motion**, **Your data**. Each setting is a sentence at 21 with the value as a tappable word in ink 600 that cycles or opens its instrument: "Weights in **kg**", "Bar weighs **20 kg**, smallest plate **1.25 kg**" (each opens the dial), "Rest **2:30** unless a lift says otherwise", "Motion **follows the system**" (cycles: follows the system / reduced / full). **Your data** holds "Export everything" (a hold key, 0.7s) and the last export time. No toggles: a two-state setting is a word that flips with a short crossfade. Living moment: changing a unit re-sets every number on screen with a tabular roll, like the portion drum.

  Recording: `recordings/settings-units.mp4` (source `studies/settings-units.html`). Values: every value is a row of tabular character columns, right-aligned so digits and unit letters line up. Each changed column rolls up (old out to −30pt, new in from +30pt, 440ms, house curve) and eases to its new width; columns stagger 26ms from the right, values 40ms down the page. Unchanged characters stay still. Converted values show what you would read on the kit (78.4 kg → 172.8 lb, 1.25 kg → 2.76 lb), stored values stay in kg. One `.selection` haptic on the tap, none per value. Word settings (Motion) cross-fade with a 3pt blur, 120ms out and 180ms in. Reduce Motion: values cross-fade in place, no roll.

## Home entry points for Notes, Review and Kitchen (for iOS Builder B)
- **Notes** has no tile. It's reached from Ask Tess ("note: …" lands a ghost row in Notes) and from Review; inside an object page, linked notes show as one line under the context line, "2 notes", which zooms into Notes filtered to that object.
- **Review** is Home's REVIEW sibling on x (already the left page). The full `ReviewView` opens by tapping its hero sentence (z).
- **Kitchen** is reached by its process and by words, not an icon grid:
  - Cooking and shopping become Now through the NowPicker, with the lit seam and Resume (the workout's language).
  - Later rows that link a recipe ("Carbonara, Thursday") zoom into the recipe page.
  - On the TRAINING page's sibling rail, add **KITCHEN** as a fourth word (REVIEW · TODAY · TRAINING · KITCHEN). Its page is the shelf (sheet 14) with Groceries one swipe further, so Home's x axis becomes the app switcher. (Confirmed by Kaushik, 2026-10-15.)
- Until a home for user-built apps exists (M3 deferred), don't add a launcher grid.
