## The stack
Ground (objects, classes, links, paths) -> Engine (landing sentences, process table, day
story, quiet interrupts, cook planner, pools, Measure) -> Walls (health, body, calories,
money never reach Claude, logs, backups, widgets) -> Tess (cobalt, the only cross-app
voice) -> Apps (six accents, leads, signature lines) -> Home (where they meet).

## 1. The day sentence (Home)
Home's lead = the apps' landing sentences in day order, key words in each app's accent.
Tap a word: it grows in place into that app's title (house spring; Reduce Motion crossfade).
Apps with nothing to say drop out. New: Core HomeSentence composer; word-to-title transition.

## 2. One pair of hands (whole-day scheduler)
The cook hands cap moves into the process table. Workout, cook, routine on one clock; a
collision gives one Tess offer with its consequence: "Cook first and eat at 6:50, then Push
A at 7:30?" Keep / Not now. Home "Tonight" draws the cook's time spine for all apps.

## 3. Words remember where they come from
A thing mentioned in another app keeps a hairline underline in its home app's accent; long
press = its side in place; tap = its page. Every page gets "Mentioned in", grouped by app.
NEEDS HIS YES: carve-out to 127 A ("no app uses another app's accent"): a link's underline
only, hairline, never the text. Private things: "Private", no colour.

## 4. Occasions
A dated thing (Trip, dinner, strength block) linking pieces of every app; its page asks each
linked app for its signature line (filling line, set marks, level line, total rule). Money
shows pool word and balance in ink only. New: Occasion class + assembled page.

## 5. Write anywhere
One write line routes to the app whose grammar takes it; line arrives wet cobalt, dries in
its app, receipt names where it went ("Money, Spending") with Undo. Ties: Tess asks once.
Money, health, body lines parse on the phone only, ties included.

## 6. Make a kind
Notes offers once ("You wrote about 4 books this month") to make Books a kind; the kind
page shows fields as words, old notes offered as objects. Then Books is a rail place in the
neutral colour. One offer a day, never for private notes.

## Order (Design recommendation)
1, then 3, then 5, then 2, then 4 and 6.
