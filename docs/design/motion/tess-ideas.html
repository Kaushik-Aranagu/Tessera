Tess · motion ideas

Twelve ways for her to feel more alive

You picked all eleven, plus a mouth. Each now has a sheet for the builders, in the order they'll be built: 1 and 2, then 3 and 4, then the mouth, then 5, 6 and 10, then 7, 8 and 9, and 11 last.

  1. 1

    Lights that follow the page

    Your note on the weak link. While she works, her reading lights hop to the words the ask actually touches, rest on each, and skip around, instead of sweeping every row in order. The lights at her corner drift on slow noise instead of a loop, so no two runs look the same.

    Calm cost: noneSame lights, same timing, no AI call. It is the first step toward the screen-aware Tess in your note, which is saved for later.

    Picked
  2. 2

    She wakes with you

    On your first open of the day, her eyes open from closed and she gives one slow stretch. It happens once a day and never again.

    Calm cost: noneAbout a second, once a day, no sound or haptic.

    Picked
  3. 3

    Stroke her

    Swipe a finger across her and a ripple runs through the paint, she blinks, and you feel one soft tick. Tap and hold keep doing what they do now.

    Calm cost: lowOnly happens when you reach for her. The swipe needs a clear rule so it never starts a drag by accident.

    Picked
  4. 4

    She wears the day

    Each thing you finish leaves her a little richer in colour. By evening she is a deeper Tess. At 4 AM she starts plain again. There is no number anywhere.

    Calm cost: noneNo motion of its own; the colour builds through the moments you already have. It is capped so a big day never gets loud.

    Picked
  5. 5

    She sleeps at night

    From 11 PM to 7 AM her eyes are closed, her colour is muted and she breathes slowly. Open the app late and one eye opens first.

    Calm cost: noneShe is calmer at night than she is today.

    Picked
  6. 6

    She watches what you hold

    While you lift a row to move it, her eyes follow the row. When you drop it, she looks back and blinks.

    Calm cost: noneEyes only, only while your finger is on a row.

    Picked
  7. 7

    A nudge before the next thing

    Ten minutes before something with a time, she leans toward the list once and glances at it, then settles.

    Calm cost: lowOnce per timed thing, Home only. It sits close to a reminder, so it never repeats.

    Picked
  8. 8

    She hears you type

    When you type to her, each finished word gives her one tiny pulse, the way she breathes with your voice when you speak.

    Calm cost: lowNo glow and no haptic. Too strong and it reads as a typing indicator, so it stays tiny.

    Picked
  9. 9

    She peeks at the end of a list

    When you scroll to the bottom of a page, she rises a few points as if looking over the last row, then settles back.

    Calm cost: lowOnce per page visit, so a long scroll doesn't make her bob.

    Picked
  10. 10

    She watches the rest clock

    In the last seconds of a rest her eyes move to the timer. At Go the timer owns the moment and she looks back.

    Calm cost: lowProduct says yes if you pick it, as eyes only: her eyes rest on the clock, with no lean, colour, sound or haptic. The clip's small lean would be cut.

    Picked
  11. 11

    She sits under the glass

    Tilt the phone and she shifts a point or two, her outer lights a little more, as if she sits just under the screen.

    Calm cost: someAlways moving while you hold the phone, and it uses the motion sensors. Off in Reduce Motion and Low Power.

    Picked
  12. 12

    A mouth, just a line

    Your addition. One short line under her eyes that turns with the moment: nearly flat at rest, a small soft o while she listens, one corner up while she thinks, a small smile with her answer. It never frowns at you. The box in the clip magnifies her, because at Home size it is a pinprick, like the eyes.

    Calm cost: noneIt only changes when her mode changes, and it comes with the eyes setting, so with eyes off there is no face.

    Picked
How these were made

Each clip is the motion lab's prototype of Tess at 60 frames a second on Obsidian, 6 seconds or less. The values are first guesses. Nothing here changes the app until you pick it and it goes through Design and a builder.
