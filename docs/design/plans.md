# Plans: week and month

Training's plan, one level below the TRAINING page. Two sibling pages on x, WEEK and MONTH. Light painterly ground; one screen tall; dabs, not cards or calendars-in-boxes. Mockup: `mockups/TrainingPlans.dc.html` (swipe between the two). Rules follow Product's `workouts.md` (plans roll on; weekly sets per muscle with fractional counting; below range is neutral, never red).

## The rail
At y 58: "WEEK" and "MONTH" in labels, with a painted vermilion carriage (50×4) under the active one that follows the swipe continuously.

## WEEK
- **Context line** (y 112): "this week, rolling on".
- **Hero** (y 142): "3 of 6 sessions" (two lines).
- **Days across** (y 282): seven columns, M to S. Each has the day letter in labels, a 34×30 paint dab, and the routine's name under it at 16.
  - Done: filled with the routine's colour (Push vermilion, Pull cobalt, Legs cadmium).
  - Today: an empty dab with a 2.5pt ring in its routine's colour, and the letter in ink. It links into live mode.
  - Planned: a 1.5pt ink ring at 25%.
  - Rest: no dab; "Rest" in muted text.
- **Sets per muscle** (title at y 412, window at 462, 270pt, scrolls): one 50pt row per muscle: the name at 21; a painted fill stroke (8pt) along a hairline, drawing in toward this week's count; a soft green target band (sage at 16%) behind it showing the muscle's range; and the count on the right ("6½"). Primary sets count 1, secondary ½. Under the band is simply short of it, never red.

## MONTH
- **Context line:** "October". **Hero:** "Session 11 of 26".
- **Heatmap** (y 282): weekday letters, then the month as dabs in a 7-column grid, one per day (46pt rows). A trained day is a dab in its routine's colour; its size (26 to 42pt) and opacity show volume against usual. Today has a 2.5pt vermilion ring. Future days are muted numerals. Each dab links to that workout.
- **Key** (y 610, 18): a small faint dab "lighter" and a big strong one "heavier than usual". Nothing else explains it.
- **Tess line** (y 660, cobalt): one forward-looking sentence, e.g. "Three more this week keeps October level with September." Never counts misses.

## Motion
- Pages swipe with the house paging; the carriage follows the scroll.
- On arrival, fill strokes draw in with a 60ms stagger; month dabs fade up row by row.
