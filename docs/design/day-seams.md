# A full day, sheet to sheet: the seams (Design item 22)

One recording runs a whole day through the current sheets and the build, in day order, Porcelain by day and Obsidian at night, so any place two sheets disagree shows up as a jump between them.
- Recording: `recordings/full-day.mp4` (450×930, 60fps, 96s). The order is: Plan my day (sheet), Speak on Home (sheet), the workout (**the build**, master after #278), Review, Settings, Habits, Upkeep, Money's first spend, Shutdown and the closed day in Obsidian, then the Day log turnover. Each clip is captioned with its time, look and source.
- The positions below were measured from each study's DOM and from a frame of the build (`docs/recordings/28-ink-line.mp4`, 2s).

**Where a sheet and the build differ on something already built, the build wins**, and the sheet is amended here. Where neither is built, this sheet decides. It wins over the older sheets it names.
