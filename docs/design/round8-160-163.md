# Items 160 to 163: Round 8, money month, pantry levels, routines day, empty pages (Oct 23)

Loop round 8 (10-minute cadence, power saver). Each sits behind its own Review switch,
default Off. Grammar from 127 and 131, HouseNumber from 138, finishing from 139, pools from 133.
Privacy: money values never reach Claude, backups, logs or widgets. Phone only.

## 160 Money month (switch moneyMonthV2, Builder B; Core money)
The Money page opens on the month as one sentence in plain words.
- Line one: "May, spent 1,240 US and 310 EUR, about 1,560 in all." Each currency is exact in
  Outfit numbers; the single "about" total is ink 2.
- Under it, pools as plain lines: "US checking, 2,100." "Euro card, 540." Each is a link
  with a Money-green underline (A8). Tap a pool to see its moves: sent, fee, arrived, rate.
- A level line per pool shows the month's spend against last month, ink on a 12% track.
- Scrub the month with a horizontal drag; digits roll (numberRules), one tick at each week.
- Never a total balance on Home. Occasion pool balance only on the Occasion page.

## 161 Pantry levels, to get inline (switch pantryLevelsV2, Builder B)
One list: what's in, then "To get" in the same page (Groceries folded in).
- Each item is a word and its level line: in = full, low = half, out = none. Unknown items
  carry no line. Level fill is ink on a 12% track; at AX sizes only the fill shows.
- Swipe right on a low item moves it to "To get" with a soft tap. Swipe left sets out.
- "Covers N dinners" at the top excludes recipes with anything open on To get.
- Finishing a shop (155) lifts every got item back to in, one rise, one haptic.

## 162 Routines day (switch routinesDayV2, Builder A)
Routines landing shows today as a short sentence, then the list.
- "Morning, 3 of 5 done. Evening is open." Words, no rings. Marked habits only show a
  small count ("4 days"); unmarked ones show nothing.
- Each routine is a plain line with its step in ink 2. Tap runs it as a process (appears
  in Now). Swipe right marks done, a strike follows the finger.
- "Pause" and "Busy" sit side by side at the foot, both plain underlined words, ink.
- Miss a day and the count simply restarts; no streak talk, no red.

## 163 Empty pages (switch emptyStatesV2, Builder B)
Every page that can be empty says one sentence and offers one action. No art, no mascot.
- Training: "Nothing logged yet. Start a session." Kitchen: "No dinners planned. Pick one."
- Money: "No spending this month." Notes: "A blank page. Write anything." Lists: "No lists."
- The action is the page's accent underline (2 pt, offset 6). Tess is never the voice.
- Reduce Motion: the sentence appears with a 150 ms crossfade, no other motion.
