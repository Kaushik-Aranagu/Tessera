# Items 189 to 192: Round 15, units, notifications, Spotlight and Siri, Tess's voice (Oct 23)

Loop round 15 (10-minute cadence, power saver). Lane named per item. Each behind its own Review
switch, default Off (192 is a rule sheet). Grammar from 127 and 131.

## 189 Units, once (lane: data and architecture, switch unitsV2, Core with Builder B)
Settings > Units: one Measure everywhere (no hard-coded kg).
- Plain rows: "Weight, kg", "Distance, km", "Food, grams" with the other choice one tap away.
  Changing it rewrites every shown number through Measure; stored values never change.
- Money currency stays per pool (133), not here. Temperature follows the phone.
- A sample line under each row shows the change: "Bench 82.5 kg is 182 lb." (digits roll).

## 190 Notifications, in words (lane: UI and UX, switch notifyWordsV2, Builder A + Builder B)
How every notification reads on the Lock Screen.
- One sentence, sentence case, no emoji, no exclamation, no count of what he missed.
  Examples: "Morning is next, usually 6:40 am." "Tacos, start at 6:05 pm." "Rice, 2 min left."
- A private thing reads "Private routine", "Private workout", "Private bill"; money only as due
  words ("Rent, Friday"), never an amount. Quiet (167) and Focus hold notices; the digest is
  one line when they end.
- Actions are plain words: "Done", "Not now". No badges; the app icon badge is always off.

## 191 Spotlight and Siri (lane: data and architecture with UI, switch spotlightV2, Core + Builder A)
Find and speak to Tessera from outside the app (App Intents, Core Spotlight).
- Spotlight indexes titles only: recipes, notes, lists, tasks, routines. Never private or walled
  things, never money objects, never health, body or calories. Removal is immediate.
- Siri phrases: "Add a task", "Log a set", "Start Morning", "What's next". Siri never sends
  anything to Claude and never reads private names aloud ("Private routine").
- Results open the object page in its app's accent as usual. The Review page lists exactly
  what is indexed, as a count ("212 titles"), never the titles.

## 192 Tess's voice (lane: AI, rule sheet, Core + all builders)
One voice for every line Tess says (cobalt), so the lines never drift.
- Short: one sentence, two at most. Plain words, no exclamation, no emoji, no "I think".
- Offers, not orders: "Want this on Saturday?" never "You should". No guilt, no streaks, no
  counts of what he missed, no comparing him to other days.
- She names the thing, not herself: "Limes are low." not "I noticed limes are low."
- Money, health, body and calories: "That stays on this phone." and nothing else.
- Every Tess line has Undo or "Not now" when it changed or offered something.
- Reduce Motion: text appears, no typing effect. Tess is never faded or blurred.
