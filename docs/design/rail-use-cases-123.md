# Item 123: the rail, app by app (how it is used)

Kaushik, Oct 22: "for each app think of how we use it and different scenarios". Each app: pinned, knob, dial, the situations, and where it opens.

## Rules
- Pinned = the one thing used most; it opens by tap and can be in a colour.
- Knob = a filter, range or kind, 2 to 6 values, always has a default, remembered per screen.
- Dial = the places, 3 to 8, loops. A place never holds a filter and a knob never holds places.
- Swipe right on a row is its own action (done, bought, swap); left removes or skips; the page itself never moves on a sideways swipe over a row.
- The page always opens on what he would most likely want in the first 3 seconds, not on where the page started.
- Writing something new is never in the rail: the write word or field stays where it is.

## Training Plan
Pinned: Today. Knob: Routine (Push A, Pull A, Legs, Upper). Dial: muscle groups. Rows: exercises with last weight.
- At the gym, about to start: it opens on Today's routine with START as the one ink word. Nothing to pick.
- Sunday planning the week: swipe the knob through the routines; the dial shows what each one trains.
- Rest day: Today reads "Rest" and the knob opens on the next routine, so it is never an empty page.
- Mid-workout the exercise isn't free: swipe right on a row to swap it, left to skip it today.
Opens: Opens on Today, dial on the first muscle group not yet done.

## Training Log
Pinned: Best. Knob: Range (Month, 3 months, Year). Dial: exercises. Rows: sessions, weight x reps.
- During a set: "what did I do last time?" One tap from the live set opens this on that exercise, the latest session on top.
- Monthly check, am I progressing: knob to 3 months, the dial through the lifts, a single plain trend line under the title.
- Showing a best: Best pinned lists each exercise's best set, worded "best", never "record".
Opens: Opens on the exercise you were just doing, range 3 months.

## Pantry
Pinned: To get. Knob: Show (All, Low, Soon). Dial: Fridge, Freezer, Shelf, Counter. Rows: items with amount.
- Planning the shop: pinned To get is the list; Low on the knob shows only what is running out.
- At the fridge, do we have eggs? The dial is the fridge; "Low" marks eggs in the hot colour.
- Back from the shop: swipe right on a To get row = bought, it goes to its place.
- Cooking: an item used up goes back to To get with a swipe right.
Opens: Opens on the place you used last; To get if anything is low.

## Groceries
Pinned: Now. Knob: Shop (the shops he uses). Dial: aisles in the order he walks the shop. Rows: items, quantity.
- In the shop with one hand: big type, screen kept on, the dial moves to the next aisle that still has items and skips empty ones.
- At home adding a thing: one write field stays at the top; the item lands in its aisle by itself.
- A recipe sent its ingredients: they arrive grouped by aisle under Now, with the recipe named once.
- A ticked row leaves with a 10 s UNDO, so a wrong tap costs nothing.
Opens: Opens on Now; the aisle order is learned from his ticking order.

## Recipes
Pinned: Tonight. Knob: Show (All, Can cook, Quick). Dial: Saved, Cooked, To try. Rows: recipe, time.
- 5 pm, what's for dinner: Tonight pinned names the planned cook with START.
- Nothing planned: knob to Can cook (from the Pantry), then Quick (under 30 min).
- Trying something new: To try on the dial; a cooked one moves itself to Cooked.
- Plain text only, no photos in the rows.
Opens: Opens on Tonight after 4 pm, otherwise on Saved.

## Money
Pinned: Due. Knob: Month. Dial: Spending, Bills, Saving, Cash. Rows: amounts. Money never goes to Claude.
- Did I pay the rent? Due pinned lists what is due and when, the nearest first.
- Logging a spend in the shop: the Log word stays at the bottom, the rail does not get in the way.
- End of month, where did it go: knob to last month, Spending on the dial, week sections, one plain total.
- All amounts in dollars, "about" only on converted ones.
Opens: Opens on this month, Due if something is due within 3 days.

## Notes
Pinned: Today. Knob: Kind (Notes, Lists, Links). Dial: topics (from the @links, so @Bench Press files under Training). Rows: title and first line.
- Capturing: the write field stays at the very top, always; the rail is for finding.
- What did I write about the trip? The dial goes to Trip, the titles are the page.
- Today pinned lists what I wrote today, so a day never gets lost.
Opens: Opens on Today.

## Habits
Pinned: Today. Knob: Range (Week, Month). Dial: habits. Rows: the days, done or not.
- Morning or evening check-in: Today pinned lists only the habits still open, tapping one ticks it.
- Is it working: knob to Month, the dial through the habits, the days as a plain row of marks.
- A streak is worded "best" and never shown as a loss.
Opens: Opens on Today.

## Reading
Pinned: Now reading. Knob: Year. Dial: Reading, Next, Done. Rows: books, pages.
- Bedtime: Now reading pinned with the page; one swipe up logs pages.
- Choosing the next one: Next on the dial, order set by swiping a row up.
- A year in books: the knob to a past year, Done on the dial.
Opens: Opens on Now reading.

## Body
Pinned: Latest. Knob: Range (Month, 3 months, Year). Dial: Weight, Waist, Sleep. Rows: values. Never leaves the phone.
- Morning weigh-in: Latest pinned with one entry word; it logs and closes.
- Trend: one plain line of words ("down 0.8 kg over 3 months"), no chart on the rail.
Opens: Opens on Latest.

## People
Pinned: Reach out. Knob: none. Dial: circles (Family, Friends, Work). Rows: name and last contact.
- Whom have I lost touch with: Reach out pinned lists people past their own interval, longest first.
- Before a call: the circle on the dial, the name's last note one tap down.
Opens: Opens on Reach out.

## Not a rail
- Today / Now: one list, nothing to turn through
- Live workout and cook mode: one task, one page
- Review: already tabs
- Settings: a plain list
