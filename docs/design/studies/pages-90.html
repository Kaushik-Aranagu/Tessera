THE SWIPE, THING BY THING

Whose swipe it is

The thing under the finger decides.

A number that steps (a weight, reps, a quantity): up adds one step, down takes one; the page does not turn. A dial or a slider: its own value. A row that opens or removes: keeps its horizontal swipe from sheets 75 and 86; vertical does nothing special there, so it turns the page. Plain text, a section word, the empty page: turns the page (and on D a slow drag follows the finger). The writing line or a field: never turns the page while it has focus. Rule for builders: a screen declares its page slots (N rows) and, per element, an optional vertical action; the page-turn recogniser only starts if no such action claims the touch.
