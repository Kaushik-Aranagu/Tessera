THE RULES

Never blurred (that is Tess). Size 160 pt or less, one per page, in the largest empty corner, 24 pt clear of any word; if a bigger text size would reach it, it is not drawn. Strength 18% (Obsidian 16%); the figure is information and runs full strength. Never at the top, on Home, on Groceries, a live workout, a widget, a Private thing (plain ground), or a progress photo. Arrival: a photo or drawing fades up over 380 ms with no blur; an ingredient drawing also draws its line once. Reduce Motion: 150 ms crossfade. VoiceOver: hidden, except the figure (read as one element) and a photo.
