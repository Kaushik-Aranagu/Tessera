91. Pockets

Kaushik on sheet 88 (Oct 17, 12:31 PM PT): no band or banner at the top. Very faded images in small sections that make a screen look good, especially where it doesn't feel cluttered. The rule: a picture is a pocket, a small feathered patch, 160 pt or less across, at 14% to 18% strength, that sits in the emptiest part of the page, behind nothing and touching no word. It belongs to a page, so when the page turns (sheet 90) its pocket turns with it. It never sits at the top.

WITH PAGES (90)

A page carries its own pocket. Groceries read aisle by aisle: Produce, Dairy and Frozen each leave the foot of their page empty, so each gets one patch in its own colour. When the page turns, the old pocket dissolves in 260 ms and the new one dries in over 380 ms, the same beat as the rows. It never moves with the rows.

GROCERIES, PAGE BY PAGE

The recording turns Produce, Dairy, Pantry and Frozen.

THE RULES, IN ONE PLACE

Where a pocket may sit, and where it may not

Size: at most 160 × 160 pt, 5% of the screen. Strength: 14% to 18% in Porcelain, 12% to 16% in Obsidian; the figure 22%. Edge: feathered on every side, never a line, never a card, never a circle. Place: the largest empty rectangle the page has, bottom right by default, at least 24 pt from any word; if a longer title or a bigger text size would touch it, it is simply not drawn. Never: at the top, behind a title or a number, on Home, on a live workout, on a Private thing (plain ground), on a widget, or as a progress photo. Loading: a photo, if there is one, dries in over a colour patch in 380 ms (blur 5 to 0); no spinner, no grey box, no "add a photo". Reduce Motion: 150 ms crossfade. VoiceOver: hidden, except a photo, read once as "Photo of Sheet-pan paprika chicken". Budget: one pocket per page, and no pocket on a page that already has a lead edge marker and two section words.
