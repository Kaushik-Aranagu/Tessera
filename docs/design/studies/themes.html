Switching

In Settings, "Look" holds one sentence: "Porcelain by day, Obsidian by night". Each name is a word that cycles through the light or dark themes, and the app follows the system's appearance between them. Saying "Obsidian always" pins one. The change paints in from Tess's soul as a flood of the new ground, over 700 ms. Live Activities and widgets follow on their next update.
