One swarm, five dots
They drift on their own like the thinking dots. While you lift, they count: one ripple per rep, so you can count the reps. When the set lands they settle into a shape that says how it went, and a small copy of that shape stays on the row.
The shapes
Ring on target.
Open ring short: the gap is the missing reps.
Ring with a satellite more than planned: the extra rep orbits out.
Star a personal best: a burst, then the dots join.
Heavier sets draw bigger dots. Rest: they shrink to a small ring and breathe, then brighten for the next set.
Dots rest in ink; warmth shows only while they move.
