## Seams found, and fixed here
1. **The "1 / 5" count** at the top right goes. The strip says which exercise this is; 41 keeps counts off the page unless they are the data (the strokes are).
2. **The next exercise no longer peeks** cut off at the right edge (an "Overhead" with no "press" was a cut line). The strip's unlit segments say what's ahead, a sideways swipe brings it, and the **rest line names it when its time comes**: "Rest, then Overhead press" on the last set of an exercise.
3. **The context line sits above the name,** in `context`, as everywhere (41 rule 1); the detail line under the strokes is `inkSecondary`. In the old cover the "set 1 of 4, last was 82.5 × 8" line sat under the strokes and the name had no context line.
4. **The reps are set-block.md's one row**, 56 Light on the weight's line, neighbours only while the finger is down. The three-row drum at the right (in the day reel's live cover) was the old design and is retired there too.
5. **The long name's fitting form** (Title 44 → 34 → two lines of 34) is 41's hero rule applied to the page heading; "Incline dumbbell press" sits at 34.
6. **Speak in the gym:** his heard words sit **right-aligned above the soul at Field 18** `inkSecondary` (the `calm-capture.md` addendum: his words one step under her voice), not Body 21 and not voice-gym.md's Title 34 at y 560. `speak.md` is amended with this line.
