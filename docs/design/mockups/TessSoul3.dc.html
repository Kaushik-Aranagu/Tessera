Beyond moving: colour and shape

Eight directions to pick from. Any of them can layer onto a state.
A · FOLLOWS THE DAY
Dawn rose and peach, noon’s three primaries, dusk magenta, night indigo. Sped up here; really it shifts over hours.
B · COLOUR CHASE
The colours pass from dab to dab, so the mixed places keep moving. A candidate for thinking.
C · BORROWS THE ROOM
trainingtasksnight
Tess takes on the palette of where she is: training, tasks, or night.
D · DROPS TO STROKES
The dabs pull out into brushstrokes and fall back into drops. A candidate for hand-off.
E · POINTILLISM
She breaks into points of colour, like Seurat, then gathers again. A candidate for searching.
F · WET ON WET
Like paint dropped in water: rings of each colour spread and mingle. A quiet way to say she got it.
G · CATCHES THE LIGHT
Mosaic’s glint, crossing the wet paint every few seconds. For a fresh note or a new best.
H · ONE-COLOUR MOODS
focuscelebrate
All one hue for a mode: cobalt to stay out of the way in focus, cadmium for a celebration.
