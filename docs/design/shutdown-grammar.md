## Rules kept
1. **One base** per look; the Obsidian phones are the same frame.
2. **No dots, no seams.** The carry-over and TOMORROW, FIRST are label words; the only colour at rest is the clock.
3. **Nothing cut.** Row names wrap; the hero steps down its forms.
4. **The value is the state.** TOMORROW or LATER at the right of a row, nothing drawn through it.
5. **Haptics,** one per moment: `.selection` on a row's flip, the hold's `.success`, nothing for the handoff.
6. **Reduce Motion:** crossfades only; no lift, no bead, no keyboard ride (the column hides and the line shows above the keys in place).
7. **VoiceOver:** "Shutdown, 9:04 pm", then each row as "Book the physio, tomorrow" or "Clean the oven, later" (hint "Tap to keep"), "Tomorrow, first: Reply to the landlord", "Close the day, hold".
