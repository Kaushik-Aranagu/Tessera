# 85. Habits: the front and its side (E5)

**Kaushik's principle, in one line:** a habit is a name, a count and seven strokes; a day without a mark is only a day without a mark, and nothing on the page or the side ever says what he did not do.

## Hard rules held
No streak count or run length, no "missed", no miss mark, no red, no percentage, no chart, no sentence about a gap. Unmarked days and days to come are the same dashed guide. Cobalt only in Tess's lines. No "at" before a time, no "...", digits for counts, "best" never "record".

## The states
1. **Empty, first use.** Context line as usual, the writing line, then "No habits yet." at 66% where the first habit goes. No art, no count. Tess: `Say "new habit read 5 a week" and I'll keep it.`
2. **Made by speaking.** Hold the soul anywhere (speak.md): the listening bloom, "Listening" in cobalt, his words at Title 34 and 55% ink as heard. Letting go sends it; nothing is saved until it lands. Typing the same words on the writing line is the same grammar (D10).
3. **One habit.** Read rises in (10 pt, 360 ms, one `.impact(soft, 0.6)`, the made haptic) with seven guides and "5 a week". Tess: "Read added, 5 times a week." with UNDO 4 s.
4. **A new week, after a gap (streak-free).** Monday: every stroke a guide, every count its target, Tess silent. Nothing carries over, nothing says how long it has been.
5. **Three habits, one done today.** Read has Mon, Wed, today (3 of 5); Train has Tuesday from a workout in its own pigment (1 of 3); Meditate has no mark and nothing is drawn for that. Tess: "Read, 3 of 5 this week." with UNDO.
6. **A long list.** Nine habits, creation order, one sheet scrolling under the pinned back word, rows melting into Tess's band at 736 (40 pt fade). Pull down at the top opens the index. Tess names two: "Read and Stretch are done for the week." The "and N more" rule is for names in a sentence: three named "Read, Stretch and Walk", from four "Read, Stretch and 2 more". A row never lists names.

## The check-in: one gesture, one haptic, the fleck
- **Swipe the habit's line right** past 140 pt (the task gesture; `.selection` when armed) or **tap a stroke** (any day this week, as 59). Release writes today.
- The stroke falls 26 pt and lands wet at x1.25 with a 2 pt blur, then dries to the rest tint over 2 s. **The fleck stays:** a 12 pt fleck of the paint leaves the stroke and arcs to the soul (520 ms), which brightens once. The count rolls. Tess's line with UNDO 4 s.
- **One haptic:** the done pattern (task-reward.md), at release. The fleck arrives silent.
- Swiping a habit already marked today does nothing and Tess says "Read is already in for today." with no haptic. Tapping a done stroke clears it (reverse drop, 10 s UNDO).
- A left swipe on a row is not the row's, so the middle swipe left opens the side (76). Rows have only the right swipe. The hold on a line and the Shutdown ask (DONE, NOT TODAY) of 59's addendum stand.
- Reduce Motion: no fall, no fleck; the stroke fades in place (150 ms), the count crossfades, a joined stroke appears whole. The done haptic still plays.

## Differences from earlier sketches
1. **Count at zero** is "5 a week", not "0 of 5" (59, H11): a zero reads as a shortfall.
2. **History moves to the side.** 59's and body-weight-and-habits' "Met in 1 of the last 3 weeks." goes: "met" is a score. Weeks appear only as plain days on LATELY. every-side.md says "only this week shows, with no history": this needs Product to allow history on the side (the front still shows only this week).
3. **The side's first page is new** (LATELY, BEFORE); every-side.md's HOW IT COUNTS (FACTS) stays as page 2. The front's side word is LATELY.
4. **Done haptic** is the done pattern (task-reward haptics map), not 59's `.impact(medium, 0.8)`.
5. **The fleck** is added to the habit check-in (59 had only the drop); it lands silent, unlike a task's soft reach tap, so there is one haptic.
6. **Swipe right on the line** also checks in (59: tap the stroke only).
7. **VoiceOver:** a habit line is one stop with actions, not seven toggles; the toggles live on the habit page.

## Rows for acceptance
1. Empty shows "No habits yet." and Tess's speak line only; speaking lands a habit with UNDO.
2. A zero habit reads "N a week"; no "0 of" anywhere; no streak, "missed", red, ring or percentage anywhere including the side.
3. Check-in: swipe or tap, one done-pattern haptic, fleck to the soul, count rolls, UNDO; a repeat does nothing.
4. Long list scrolls under a pinned back word and fades into the band; Tess names two.
5. The side opens by right edge, middle or LATELY; pages by middle or facet word; empty side never dead.
6. Reduce Motion crossfades 150 ms; Dynamic Type one column.
