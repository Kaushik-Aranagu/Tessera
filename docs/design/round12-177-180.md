# Items 177 to 180: Round 12, controls, speak to write, what stays here, bring things in (Oct 23)

switch, default Off. Grammar from 127 and 131. Privacy rulings hold on every item.

## 177 Controls (lane: UI and UX, switch controlsV2, Builder A)
Control Center and Lock Screen controls, iOS 27 controls API, words only (no pictures of food or bodies).
- "Log a set" (opens the live workout, only while one runs), "Start Morning" (the next routine),
  "Add a task" (opens the write line), "Quiet" (toggle, 167).
- A control shows a plain word and, for Quiet, "On" or "Off". Never a number from money, health
  or body; a private routine reads "Private routine". No accent hue beyond ink on the Lock Screen.

## 178 Speak to write (lane: AI, switch voiceWriteV2, Core with Builder B)
A hold on the write line records speech and fills the line; it is the same write line (writeAnywhere).
- Speech recognition on device only. Nothing is stored; audio is dropped after the words land.
- The line shows the words in ink, then Tess's route in cobalt ("Lemons, to Groceries") with
  Undo (164). Spoken money is routed as words, never an amount on Home.
- Never reaches Claude. Reduce Motion: the words appear, no waveform. One soft haptic on land.

## 179 What stays on this phone (lane: data and architecture, switch walledPageV2, Core with Builder B)
Settings > Privacy becomes one page of plain sentences that tell the truth from the store.
- "4 private things. 2 routines, 1 workout, 1 note. They show as Private everywhere." The count
  is read live from ObjectPrivacy; names are never listed on this page.
- "Never leaves this phone: health, calendar, body, calories, money amounts." and
  "Tess asks Claude: Off" (or On, with the one line of what it sends: titles and counts only).
- A final line: "Last backup tonight, 9:14 pm, without private things." (169)
- Nothing on the page can turn a wall off. Changing Tess asks Claude is the only control, as now.

## 180 Bring things in (lane: data and architecture, switch importV2, Core with Builder B)
Paste or share a plain list or CSV into Tessera; it becomes objects, on the phone.
- "Bring in" in Settings and the share sheet. A pasted list becomes a List; lines with a date and
  a number can become spends or sets after one question ("These look like spends. Add them?").
- A preview in plain sentences first ("12 lines. 9 look like tasks, 3 like spends."), then
  "Bring in" with 10 min Undo (164). Nothing is sent anywhere; no accounts.
- Bad lines are skipped and counted once at the end, never one by one.
