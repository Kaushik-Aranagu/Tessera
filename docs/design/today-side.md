## THE DAY
- **The top stays:** the name TODAY at 52, "Friday" at 138, and the context line at 186 ("6 done, 1 on now", with NOW at its right). The hero is what's left: "4 to go".
- **Four strands,** one per area: TASKS, TRAINING, KITCHEN, HABITS. Each is a 2 pt line at x 78, 154, 230 and 306, from 7 am at 330 to 11 pm at 700. Above the now line, the strand is its area's colour at 46%; below it, 16%. It's the same "ink that fills" as the titles (72).
  - The header is Label 12/700 in the area's colour, with the count under it at 13 ("3 of 5", or "on now" while something runs).
- **Marks:** each thing is a 6 pt stroke from its start to its end, at least 16 pt long. These are strokes, never dots.
  - **Done:** solid (72% mix), with its 13 pt name struck through at 50% ink.
  - **Running:** solid down to the now line with a soft halo, the rest tinted, and its name at 600 in the area's colour.
  - **Ahead:** tinted (34%), with its name at 86% ink.
  - Names wrap within 66 pt and keep "7 pm" together.
- **Now:** a 1.5 pt `tess` line across all four strands, with the time at its left ("6:10").
  - Hour words ("9 am", "noon", "3 pm", "9 pm") sit at the left in 13 pt at 45% ink.
  - Nothing is red for late, and a thing that ran over just draws longer.
- **Tap a mark** to open its thing (the zoom, as from the Day log).
- **Motion:** the page moves as in 71 (the body goes 44 pt left; the side page comes in from 60 pt, 40 ms behind, 320 ms on the house curve). The strands then draw down the day to now over 1.1 s, ease-out, and what's ahead tints in last. The running stroke breathes as the soul does.
- **Reduce Motion:** a 200 ms crossfade, with the strands already drawn.
- **VoiceOver:** each strand is one element: "Tasks, 3 of 5 done. Next, Stretch at 8:30 pm."
