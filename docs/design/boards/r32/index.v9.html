Rounds 210, 217 and 218, Oct 23

The Training room

Where the doorway lands. The Home mosaic comes through with you and becomes the room's lintel, a strip of pixels across the top that shows this app's own life.

The lintel. Twelve weeks of your best for one lift, as a pixel ridge: a landscape, not a chart. The high ground is this app's red, the valley fades to the page in steps, and the bottom edge blends the same way Home's mosaic does. The week of your best glints once when you arrive.

Tap a lift. The ridge re-forms for it, column by column from left to right, and the line above it says that lift's best in plain words. Tap the same lift again to go back to the session's lead lift.

Set marks. Each set is an ink ring under its lift. Done sets are drawn in red; sets still to do are pale. Nothing is filled.

Start. The ridge flattens into a level line, the live-workout pulse, and the sentence becomes the first set. The lintel stays the doorway home: tap Home and the pixels fold back into the mosaic.

Every room is its own place. The lintel is what they share: a strip of pixels that comes through the doorway from Home. Each lintel has its own height and its own way of arriving: Training's ridge rises column by column, firmly; Kitchen's buildings settle in one after another, with weight; Notes write themselves in, drifting; Lists' line steps in from the left. Under the lintel, each room keeps its approved landing (sheet 131 v2), so no two rooms are laid out alike.

Touch the lintel to read it. Hold and slide along any lintel and the line under it reads what is under your finger: the week and its best, the day and its dinner, which line of the note and what else it is on, how many were left that day or how many will be at this pace. Let go and it settles back after a moment.

Training. The best is the lintel's one numeral, top right, and the week it came from glints once; how often you set a new best is in the line under it. Under it, the approved landing: the session in words, Start with the place word, the week as set marks, today's lifts with their sets, and the doors.

Kitchen. Each dinner's building has a window for every ingredient still to get, so tonight's tacos show four gaps until they are in the pantry. Under it, Pantry and Recipes as two large doors, Pantry with its level lines, Recipes with the week.

Notes. A line that is also something elsewhere carries that app's colour in the margin: Packing's olive, Bench press's red, Tacos' ochre. Under it, the search-and-write line, the things you write about sized by how much, and the margin sentences. No numbers anywhere in Notes.

Lists. How many are left is the lintel's one numeral, top right; ahead of today a few pale whole tiles step down toward zero, and the day it gets there is in the sentence. Under it, the lead list's next things as large words (tap one when it's packed and a tile drops from the line), then every other list as a name that fills with ink by how much is done.

Routines. The day is a tide line, from 5 am to 11 pm. Each routine is a swell at its usual time, as wide as it usually runs. What has passed is water in the room's rose, fading to the page in steps; what is ahead is only its surface, in pale whole tiles. Now is one ink stitch standing on the water, and the routine that is running breathes. Tap a routine and its swell glints. Under it, the approved landing: the step in large words, minutes on this step, Pick up, one line per routine, and Browse. On a quiet day the top reads Nothing is running with three Start words.

Private workouts show "Private workout" and no lifts on the Lock Screen and in widgets; inside the room they read normally. Reduce Motion: the ridge swaps plainly, no glint.
