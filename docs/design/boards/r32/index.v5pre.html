The Training room

Where the doorway lands. The Home mosaic comes through with you and becomes the room's lintel, a strip of pixels across the top that shows this app's own life.

The lintel. Twelve weeks of your best for one lift, as a pixel ridge: a landscape, not a chart. The high ground is this app's red, the valley fades to the page in steps, and the bottom edge blends the same way Home's mosaic does. The week of your best glints once when you arrive.

Tap a lift. The ridge re-forms for it, column by column from left to right, and the line above it says that lift's best in plain words. Tap the same lift again to go back to the session's lead lift.

Set marks. Each set is an ink ring under its lift. Done sets are drawn in red; sets still to do are pale. Nothing is filled.

Start. The ridge flattens into a level line, the live-workout pulse, and the sentence becomes the first set. The lintel stays the doorway home: tap Home and the pixels fold back into the mosaic.

The Kitchen room. The week's seven dinners stand side by side as one pixel skyline: a low dome for the soup pot, a narrow tower for leftovers, a flat roof for the stir fry, a peaked roof for pasta, a rounded arch for tacos. Tonight's building glints once when you arrive. Tap a day and its building rises two tiles, and the line above says what is for dinner. Start cooking lets a little steam drift up from tonight's building.

The Notes room. The lintel is the latest page, written as rows of pixels: each line of the note becomes a row of tiles, with a gap for every space, so you can see the note's shape without reading it. When you arrive, the newest line writes itself in, tile by tile, and a single pale tile waits at its end where the next word goes. Tap another note and the page turns: the rows lift away and the new note writes in from the top.

The Lists room. The lintel is what is left, as a falling line. It starts high on the left, where the list began, and steps down every time something gets done, out to today. Ahead of today it continues as a quiet line of pale tiles toward the day it should reach zero. Tap the ring beside an item and that step drops: one tile breaks off the line and falls away into the page.

Every app gets a lintel. Kitchen shows the week's cooking as a skyline of dishes, Routines the day's hours as a tide line, Notes the latest page as written rows, Lists what is left as a falling line. Money's lintel shows only shapes, never amounts.

Private workouts show "Private workout" and no lifts on the Lock Screen and in widgets; inside the room they read normally. Reduce Motion: the ridge swaps plainly, no glint.
