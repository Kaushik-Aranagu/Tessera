Fold the loose areas into the apps

Body weight, Habits, Reading, People and Upkeep sit outside the seven apps today, behind the area index. Seven is about the limit before the colours start to look alike. So each area would become a place inside the app it belongs to, on that app's rail, with nothing lost.

Body weighttoTraining, place "Body". It stays walled as now.
HabitstoRoutines, place "Habits". Small counts only for marked habits.
ReadingtoNotes, as its first kind, "Books".
PeopletoNotes, place "People". "Call mom" links to her.
UpkeeptoTasks, as repeating tasks, place "Upkeep".

Builders wait for your yes on this one only.

Tess, the orb as a screen (a sketch)

You said you'd like Tess to be like the Sphere, an orb whose skin becomes content. Here is a first sketch. She stays whole, never faded, and only in her own paints. Nothing gets built until you ask.

Listening: one eye with a slow iris.
