Pantry, place by place

The Pantry page from the Kitchen landing. One place at a time on a three-place dial: To get, Fridge, Freezer, Shelf, Counter. Each place lists its things in short pages that turn up and down. Swipe a thing right when it's used up and it flies onto To get.

Pantry

How it works

1The dial. The place you're in sits large in the Kitchen colour, with its count. The place above and below sit small. Drag the dial up or down, or tap a small place, and it snaps one place at a time. It loops. 2The brief says what matters in this place, in two lines: what runs low, what wants using. 3Things, five to a page. Low things say low in the Kitchen colour; others show how much. Drag the list up for the next page. Tap a thing to switch it between in and low. 4Swipe right: used up. The row slides off and its name arcs up to "to get", which counts up. On To get, swipe right means you got it, and it goes back to its place. Undo for 10 s. 5Swipe left: not now. A low thing stops asking for today. Hold a thing to pin it to the top of its place (three pins at most).
