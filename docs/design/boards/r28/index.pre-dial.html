Departure mosaic

The three lines are a departure board: when the day turns, each letter clicks forward through the alphabet to its new word. Under it the mosaic, the page stays a page. Only the mosaic is pixels, and its edge has no line. Its corners are softly rounded, between a circle and a square, and every pixel stays full size. Around it, about twelve rows of pixels start in the page's own color and climb, a small step per pixel, into the mosaic's colors, carrying the scene's detail as they go, so a star stays a star. You can't point to where the page stops and the mosaic starts. Its shape has a life of its own: it breathes a little, and stretches or shrinks to fit each mood (wide and low for still water, tall for the night sky, narrow while something runs), carries the mood of the hour in its own colors. Its edges break up into scattered pixels so it sits on the page instead of in a box.

The apps list talks to the mosaic

Turn the apps list and the mosaic answers. Its color takes a light wash of the app you stop on, and that app's own little pixel sign appears: a barbell that lifts, a pot that steams, a clock hand going round, a check drawing itself, a line being written, list ticks, three pools filling. Tap the middle app and the mosaic opens the door: its pixels run out across the whole page in the app's color, flip over, and become the app's page. Home, at the top, runs it backward.

Next, by hand

Swipe a Next line right and an ink strike follows your finger; let go past the mark and it's done. A tile leaves the end of the line, arcs up and is laid into today's mosaic, which sparkles once. Swipe left for not now: the line slides away and the rest close up. Either way one Undo waits under the list for five seconds.

Tess picks the mood

On the phone nobody taps these. Tess watches the day on the phone and moves the mosaic: the clock and sun times, something running, a plan for rest, days away, everything finished, and later the weather. All of it is read on the phone, so nothing about you leaves it.

Each mood picks its own changeover unless you choose one here. Touch the mosaic to lean the pixels; tap for a ripple. On the phone the mood follows the day by itself.
