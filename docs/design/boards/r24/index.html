Your day, laid in tiles

1

Tessera means a mosaic tile. Home now has one: today, morning on the left to midnight on the right. Each column is a stretch of the day, coloured like the sky at that hour, dawn peach to noon cream to dusk rose to night indigo. The day so far is set; what is ahead is still light and loose. The column you are in now breathes.

2

Touch it. Tiles turn over in a ripple from your finger, with a soft tick. Drag across and it tells you the time and what is there.

3

Finish something. Swipe a Next line right: a tile leaves the line and is laid into the mosaic at this hour, glazed a deeper shade of that hour's colour. By night the mosaic is a picture of what you did. The tile already in the morning is the gym.

4

Every so often a tile turns on its own, so the page is never quite still. Reduce Motion keeps it still.

The apps list is off. You can add it back in Settings if you want it.
