Food from outside is logged as a meal with an estimate, and by default it goes into the fridge.

Try it

1Done cooking asks what's left. The servings drum starts at what's likely left (4 made, 1 eaten). Drag it, or tap a neighbour. The day to eat it by starts 3 days out for the fridge, 2 months for the freezer; tap the day to roll it. Tess reminds you that morning at 10, never between 11 PM and 7 AM. "Put in fridge" files it under Leftovers. 2The fridge opens on Leftovers. Each one says how much is left and when to eat it. The day before, the words turn the Kitchen colour; on the day it reads "throw out today". Swipe right is always one serving eaten, logged as a meal, with Undo. Tap a leftover to change its servings or day; on its last day that page's foot reads "Throw out". 3Sections packed in two columns. Small sections stack in one column when they fit whole, and two sections always take both columns. Section names are set in small capitals in slate, with a hairline under them, so they never read as things. Swipe sideways on a section name, the space under the last thing, or tap the "Next" line, and the columns move on. Changing place on the dial brings the columns in from that direction.4Four colors on the page. Kitchen amber for the place and the action word, slate for section names, wine for what needs you (low, eat by tomorrow, throw out today), pine for leftover days that are fine. 5Food from outside. Type what it was, add a photo or a menu line, and an estimate appears. It's made on the phone unless you've let Claude help, and you can tap the number to type your own. "You ate" starts at none yet, so by default it all goes in the fridge, and every serving you eat later is logged. 6Calories stay in the room. Not on Home, widgets, notices, the Live Activity, logs or backups. The reminder names the dish, or "Your leftovers" when it's private.
