Round 214, Oct 23

The mosaic's gallery

Scenes instead of stripes. Each one is alive in a small way and blends into the page at its edge. Tess picks one for the hour, the weather and the kind of place you are in.

How Tess picks. On the phone only: the hour (dawn, day, golden hour, night), today's weather, and the kind of place you are in, coast, city, mountains, desert, woods or plains, read once from a coarse location and never stored, sent or paired with anything of yours. A city is never named; it is just "a city". With no location she follows the hour and the weather.

Never still, never loud. Every scene has one small life: windows lighting, waves rolling in, a ripple on the lake, fireflies, heat shimmer, raindrops sliding, stars turning, the sun clearing the furrows. The one-thing signs and moments still borrow pixels from whichever scene is showing.

More later. The gallery grows: you can ask Tess for a different scene, and later she can make new ones from the same pieces.
