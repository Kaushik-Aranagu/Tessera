Round 246, Oct 24

Notes landing: wall and shelf

His 1:27 AM: the quarter band, Figtree with a little something (sharper corners), no gaps and never unevenly spaced letters (filler or repeated words are fine), and words that stay live with effects. His 1:28 AM: the rest of the page holds the doors into Notes. Below it, plain sections: recent notes and the doors.

Moments
Font

Paper.

Pure black.

The page. Room name and one line at the top. The word wall is a quarter of the screen (220 pt), centred on the Home mosaic's slot (180 to 400 pt), so the mosaic hands its place to the words on the way in. Under it, the shelf of doors (430 to 680 pt), one plain line, then the action word.

Figtree, sharpened. Figtree 900 in capitals, with a hairline outline of its own colour drawn behind each letter with mitred joins (3.5% of the size). It adds a touch of weight and squares off the soft joins, so corners read crisper. Plus Jakarta Sans and Urbanist are on the toggles as sharper relatives.

No gaps, no uneven letters. Every word fits its brick by its own size, never by spacing. The notch beside a stacked name is filled by one to three small words stacked to its exact height; repeats of any Notes word are allowed when the fresh ones run out. Two small words side by side at the name's own size can fill it too. Repeats are only of words not already on the wall. A word may stretch by 8% at most, glyphs and spacing together, so letters always stay evenly spaced. If nothing fits, that name goes on one line and the wall is packed again; a row that can't reach both edges is thrown out, never squeezed. The wall is packed at 36 slightly different row heights and the one that meets the band's bottom edge closest wins, so rows never open up.

Moments (live). Every few seconds one word does something: it flips over top to bottom, its letters jumble and settle like the Home departure board, or it dissolves into the page and comes back. Every 30 s the whole wall does one: a diagonal flip wave, a dissolve into the page that rebuilds with new words, or the pile-and-tidy. One word swaps every 6 s. Reduce Motion: no moments, swaps cross-fade.

The lower half (his 1:40 AM: cleaner, regular). Plain sections under the wall, nothing vertical. Recent: the last two notes as 44 pt rows, title in ink 600, when it was written in ink 2 at the right. Doors: two columns of 44 pt rows, name on the left, count in Outfit on the right: All notes, Ideas, From Tess (Tess blue), Voice memos, Pinned (gold), Mentions, Private (slate), Archive (ink 2). Section names in ink 2, 13 pt. No lines, no cards. The spine shelf is kept for another place.
