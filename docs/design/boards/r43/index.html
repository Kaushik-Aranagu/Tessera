Round 228, Oct 23

Landing elements, the other four rooms

Notes, Lists, Routines and Money, on the approved 226 bands, with the same rules as 227: type and fine lines only, lines keep a fixed length and change only colour or weight, nothing fades, Reduce Motion lands each change in one step.

1 Notes, the pull quote

Apartment, just now

The first words of your last note, set large. It fills three lines at most and is cut where a word ends, never with "...". Type a note and save it to see the quote change.

Fit30 pt Figtree 500 on 34 pt lines, three lines fixed. The phone lays the words out and keeps whole words that fit; the last kept word ends the quote, stepping back past a small word such as and, the, of or to so it never ends on one. A one-line note stays one line, with the space below left empty. MarkAn opening quote mark in the accent, 600, set as the first character. ChangeThe old quote is clipped away upward (0.24 s). The new one is clipped open from the baseline line by line, 60 ms apart. PrivateA private or walled note shows its shown label ("Private note") in ink 2, never its words: the headline is a standing display, not a reading view. Open notes quote normally while "Show the last note on top" is on. The app switcher snapshot masks the headline. ShortA one-word or small-word note shows in full; the quote is never empty.

2 Lists, the tally

6 of 14
Packing, for Sunday
Next: socks, the book.

One stroke per item, in groups of five. Packing an item turns its stroke to ink. The fifth in a group gets the strike across, drawn in once. Strokes never grow or move.

Strokes26 pt tall, 1.6 pt, round caps, 5 pt apart, groups 30 pt apart. Not packed: edge. Packed: ink. Up to 20 items; past that the tally hides and the number carries it. StrikeWhen a group fills, a 2 pt accent stroke is drawn across it from lower left to upper right by a clip (0.3 s), with a light tick. NumberThe count changes in place. At the end it reads "14 of 14", the tally steps away, and the brief says what's next (226). TouchA finger on the tally reads the nearest stroke's item in the brief ("Adapter, not yet"); that stroke goes to 2.6 pt while held (weight only), holding 1.2 s after release. Undo10 s, from the op log. The stroke goes back to edge; a strike undraws.

3 Routines, the tide line

8pm, stretch
Morning and lunch are done.
Night starts at 10:30.

One swell per routine across the day, drawn once and never moved. Passed water is ink, ahead is edge, and the next routine's swell is drawn heavier in the accent. Touch along the line to read it.

Time
Line6 am to midnight across the width. Each routine is a swell 18 to 40 pt wide (its length), 10 to 26 pt high (its size); a flat line between. 1.6 pt. ColourPassed: ink. Ahead: edge. The next routine: accent at 2.4 pt. As time passes, the ink runs forward by colour only; the line itself never changes shape. TouchA finger on the line reads the nearest routine in the brief slot ("Pills, 9:15 pm"), holding 1.2 s after release. The headline doesn't move. PrivateInside the room routines read by name ("Pills, 9:15 pm"). Outside it (notices, widgets, backups) every routine is "A routine"; lab-mode Claude calls may carry the name. A swell that would start before 6 am or end after midnight is clamped to the line's ends. NowNow is a colour cursor only: where ink meets edge. No ring, no tick.

4 Money, the month line

October so far
$2,418.60
Rent is due Friday.
Payday was the 15th.

The month as one line, a day per step. Passed days are ink, days ahead are edge, and today is where the ink stops. A bill or payday is its day's step drawn heavier. No ticks or marks off the line. Touch to read a day.

LineFull width, 1.6 pt. Each day is 1/31 of it (or 1/30, 1/28). It never changes length; the ink runs forward a day at a time at midnight (colour only). TodayWhere ink meets edge. Nothing else marks it. BillsThe bill's day drawn at 4 pt with round caps: ink once paid, accent while due in the next 7 days, ink 3 later. Payday is a 4 pt ink day too. TouchReads the day in the brief: "Oct 28. Phone, due." Amounts show inside the room only, never on the doorway or Home. UndoReversing a spend reads "Reversed" (engine inverse), 10 s.
