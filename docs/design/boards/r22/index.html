What changed

1

Colour is rationed. The day, the Next lines and the list are ink. Colour is only on the app words in the sentence and on the one app picked in the list.

2

The day is a picture again, but a quiet one. The line rises with daylight and falls at dusk. The dark part is the day already gone, the open ring is now, and things stand on the line with a name. Slide a finger along it: it snaps to things and says what, when, how long, and how long you are free.

3

Spacing is tighter between lines and sections, and the three sentence lines sit close, like one sentence.

4

Still here: tap an app word, tap a Next line for its reason, scroll the apps, press and hold the pick to choose Words, Number, Picture or None, tap the knob.
