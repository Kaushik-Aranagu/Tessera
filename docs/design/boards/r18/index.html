What to try

1

Slide a finger along the day. A cursor follows you and snaps to things: it says what it is, when, and for how long, in its own colour. Between things it says how long you are free and what comes next. Lift on a thing and it opens. The coloured words in the sentence open them too.

2

Swipe a Next line. Right strikes it in ink, left is not now. Tess says it, with Undo.

3

Press and slide down the apps. A lens follows your finger: the app under it swells with its drawing, its neighbours lean in, the rest go quiet. Let go and its colour opens out from your finger across the whole page. Choose Arc above the apps for a quarter circle round your thumb, with the picked app\'s drawing and fact opening beside it.

4

Read the little drawings. Training is the week, a tall stroke for each session. Kitchen is the three places and how full each is. Money is the month with Friday marked, no amounts. Notes is your last lines, the new ones coloured. Lists is what is left against what is done. Routines is the day in three parts.

5

Tap the knob by Settings. It turns, and Settings opens from the corner with Review in it. It gives a small turn on its own when the page opens so you can see it is a control. The two little arcs on the day line are sunrise and sunset; slide onto them. Tap the mic and say something.

6

Type "lemons". Tess shows where it will go, in blue, before you send it.
