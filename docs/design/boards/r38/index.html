Pantry

Round 221, Oct 23

The Pantry room

Kitchen's Pantry door opens onto its own room, built from the layout you picked on Oct 22 (places, sheet 118 C) and the dial rail you asked to feel nice when dragging through sections.

The lintel is the cupboard. Each place is one mound of whole pixel tiles, in dial order. How stocked a place is sets how dense its mound is: a full fridge is solid, a half-empty one goes airy, tile by tile. The place in focus stands in ochre; the others wait at half strength. Use something up and its place thins a little. Slide along it and the line under it says what is out or low there.

The dial. Places sit on a looping dial down the left edge, three at a time in fixed 64 pt slots: the focus large and in ink, one above and one below smaller and quieter, bowed slightly like a wheel. Drag it up or down and it follows your finger 1:1, ticks once per place and springs to rest; tap a place to bring it in. The rows on the right slip sideways as the place changes. The list itself never travels up and down.

The pin and the knob. To get sits above the dial in ochre with its count; tap it and it grows, the dial dims, and the rows become the shopping list by section. The knob on the title line (All, Low, Soon) filters every place; swipe it sideways or tap it.

Rows. Name on the left, the level line and the amount on the right (two columns stay, as decided). Swipe a row right and it's used up: a strike follows your finger, and past 90 pt the name lifts and flies into To get while its column in the lintel drains. On To get, swipe right means bought, and it flies home to its place. Drag an amount up or down to change it. Undo stays for 10 seconds.

Pinned things. Things you always keep, like milk and eggs, can be pinned: hold a row and choose Pin. They stay at the top of their place on every page, under one small word, in weight 600.

Pages, not a long scroll. A place shows six things at a time. When there are more, the last line says how many, and a swipe up on the rows turns the page: the rows lift away one after another and the next six come in.

No food drawings: the cupboard is pixels and lines only. Reduce Motion: the dial steps plainly, rows swap, nothing flies. VoiceOver: the dial is an adjustable element, one place per swipe.
