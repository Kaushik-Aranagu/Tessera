Tess in her corner

She sits bottom right and stays quiet. Everything she does starts from your finger on her: a tap to ask, a hold to talk, a drag to ask about something, a swipe up to see what she has been doing.

Try it on her, or here

Look

Her own layer: one conversation. A tap or a hold brings her into focus. Home blurs under her (blur is hers alone), and she rises to the middle of the bottom. The layer is the running conversation between you and her: what you said earlier today stays above, smaller and quieter, and she opens with one line about what matters now. Below it are at most three short replies you can tap, or you can type or hold to talk. Each answer keeps the conversation going. Only the newest exchange is large. Tap the blurred Home or swipe down to put her back.

Tap: talk with her. The layer opens on her line about now, with the keyboard. As you type, her eyes follow along. The reply you pick turns her cobalt for a moment as she takes it.

Hold: talk. The layer opens at once while she stays under your finger, swelling with your voice; the paint inside her turns faster. Let go to send. Slide left to drop it. Speech stays on the phone.

She is alive. She never sits still the same way twice. Her eyes glance in quick small moves and then hold: around her, at something on the page, or back at you. Often she blinks as she looks away, and now and then she blinks twice. Her body leans after her eyes, and she breathes on an uneven rhythm with a slow float. Bring your finger near her and she watches it. Touch her and her eyes widen for a moment. She takes a small breath before she speaks, and when she has done something for you her eyes smile.

Her colors. Soft and glowing, with no ring around her. Vermilion is at her middle, and cobalt and gold drift around it as wide soft fields that overlap gently, so there are three colors, not a rainbow. Parts of her reach out in different directions and come back, each on its own slow clock. She breathes in a smaller range than before.

Drag her onto anything. She leaves her corner and follows your finger, sitting a little above it so you can see her. The word under her thickens its underline. Drop her and she answers about that thing, then floats home. Anything with a name can be asked about: a notice, the date, an app, the one thing.

Swipe up: her room. Her room opens from her corner as a circle. It shows what she is watching and what she did today, each with its time and its Undo. Swipe down or tap Back to close it.

Double tap: quiet for an hour. She closes her eyes and breathes slower, and the top lines stop rolling. Tap once to wake her: she blinks twice.

When she speaks first. Only when something changes: one line beside her for 6 s, every name in it a door. No standing line, ever.
