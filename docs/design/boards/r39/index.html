Round 222, Oct 23

The element kit

Eight ways to touch things, each with its own feel, and where each one belongs. Every one of them is ink and type only: no cards, pills, bars or filled dots. Try each one in its frame; the table at the end says which apps and moments use it.

The drum

Words on a turning cylinder. Drag and they roll past a reading line, the one on the line in ink, Outfit 300, the rest tipping away. It springs to the nearest value. For choosing one from a known run.

Uses: a routine's start time, servings, rest between sets, a repeat rule.

The thumb arc

A quarter circle that comes out of the bottom-left corner only while you set a value, so it never reads as a progress ring and never meets Tess in her corner. Drag around it and the number steps in fives with a tick. One-handed, no reaching. The arc is a hairline; only the part you have set is in the app's colour.

Uses: cook timers, focus minutes, the live set's weight when the phone is in one hand.

The lens

A long list read through a magnifier at one third of the height. The line under the lens grows to 1.38x and reads in ink; lines further away settle smaller and quieter. It snaps a line into the lens when you let go.

Uses: Recipes, the exercise Library, Notes in a thing, Runs.

The page turn

Long lists come as pages of six, not an endless scroll (your Oct 17 ask). Swipe up and the lines lift away one after another while the next six arrive; the last line says how many are left.

Uses: Pantry places, a long packing list, the Log, Spending.

The fold

One more dimension, by pinching. Twelve weeks of tiles, one per day. Pinch in (or tap the word) and the days fly together into weeks, then months; pinch out and they spread back into days. The same tiles, never a chart.

Uses: the Training log, habits, the Routines tide (day to week), Money's shapes.

The word strip

A sideways run of words with the middle one large, 21 pt and 600, and its neighbours 16 pt in ink 2. Drag or flick and it settles a word in the middle. No blur, no underline bar.

Uses: the days of Plan, a knob with several values, Recipes' week, a month in Money.

The thread

Daily's signature, made physical. Steps hang from one line down the left; the step you're on has one hot stitch. Pull down anywhere and the thread stretches, then lets go and the next step comes up. The finished step is struck through and rises away.

Uses: a running routine, cook mode's steps, the sets of a live workout.

The pressed number

Numbers are instruments (kept from your Oct 22 approval). Hold the top half and it climbs, faster the longer you hold; hold the bottom half and it falls; a flick steps it once. "was 185" stays under it for two seconds, and the change goes on the one Undo for 10 seconds. With Reduce Motion each press is one step.

Uses: set weight and reps, amounts in Pantry, a bill's due day.
