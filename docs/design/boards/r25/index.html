The living mosaic

1

Smaller tiles, so it can draw. More than 800 of them now. At rest it is today's sky, morning to midnight, with a slow swell passing through like wind on water, the hour you are in breathing, and tiles popping here and there.

2

It morphs. When Home opens, the tiles show the time, then melt back into the day. The words under it are moments it can become: the time, the sky at sunset with the sun sitting on the horizon, how long until the next thing, and what you have done today. Each change rolls across the tiles as a wave from where it starts. On the phone these come at the right moment by themselves; here you can tap them.

3

Touch it. Tiles lean away from your finger and spring back, and a tap sends a ripple. On the day, sliding reads the time.

4

Finish a Next line and its tile spins into the hour you are in.
