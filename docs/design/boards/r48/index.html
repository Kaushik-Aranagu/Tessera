Recipes, by what you can cook

The Recipes page from the Kitchen landing. The same three-place dial as the Pantry, but its places are ways of looking: Ready now, One short, Planned, All. Every recipe you can make has Cook now; one that's a thing short offers to put that thing on To get.

How it works

1The dial picks how you look. Ready now (everything's here), One short, Planned, All. The focus is large in the Kitchen colour with its count. Drag it, flick it, or tap a small one. It loops. 2The brief picks out the useful one: the fastest, or the one that uses what's about to go. 3A timetable, five to a page. Minutes run down the left, quickest first, like a departures board. Each recipe says what goes in it, with anything that wants using first in the Kitchen colour (spinach, lemons). Cook in 30 min steps 15, 20, 30, 45, any time; recipes that won't fit go lighter. Drag the list up for the next page. 4Tap a recipe and it opens in place with its one action: Cook now (the Kitchen landing becomes the cooking), or Get it when one thing is short. Tap the name again for the full recipe; tap the row to close it. Hold serves 2 to step it. Only one row is open at a time, and the rows below slide under the edge and come back. 5Hold a recipe to pin it to the top (three at most). New recipe: tell Tess, paste a link, or type it in.
