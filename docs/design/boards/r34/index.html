Round 213, Oct 23

One thing at a time

Under the mosaic, one thing fills the space: something big, something smaller, something smaller still. Swipe across it, or across the mosaic, for the next. No card, no frame: just the words and a little pixel sign, and the mosaic turns to match.

Look

Each kind has its own shape. A session is a countdown. Groceries is a count and the things themselves, which fall in one by one. A birthday is the day, in its colour, with pixels of confetti. A task writes itself in, and you hold it to strike it through. A list shows how far along it is. Weather is the sky and the temperature. A routine is the hour, with a clock hand sweeping.

The swipe. Nothing moves under your finger. Swipe past 50 pt and everything changes where it stands: the words fold away in place and the next ones reveal in the same spot, each with its own entrance. One light haptic per page. Reduce Motion: a plain swap.

Done. Swiping across turns the page here, so to finish the thing on screen, press and hold its big line: an ink strike draws through it, and letting go finishes it with the one Undo line. Hold works anywhere the thing is shown.

The mosaic lends its pixels. When a page arrives, its little sign is made of tiles that fly out of the mosaic: each leaves a brief gap that fills back in, so the mosaic keeps its own life. When you swipe, the sign stays where it is: it collapses in place, and the mosaic feeds in the pixels for the next one. Each page also washes the mosaic lightly in that thing's colour.

Tess reads what is under her. She measures the ground beneath her and sets how she blends and how strong her paint is, so she is as warm on white as on paper, and as bright on pure black. On light grounds she lays clean paint and mixes only lightly, so her colors never turn brown. Each look has its own tuned colours. Swiping the mosaic turns these same pages, so there is one row of things, not two.

Order: Tess's ranking on the phone, most important first, at most seven. Private things read by their label. No money amounts, no health or calendar text leave the phone.
