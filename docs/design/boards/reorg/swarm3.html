Bench press
4 x 6-8, on the Training page
Ink drops that draw each set
Five tiny ink drops drift on their own, with tails and a little life. While you lift they hop, one hop per rep, and the last hard reps slow and tremble. When the set lands they draw it as one brush circle, a bit of calligraphy that tells how the set went. A small copy stays on the row.
The shapes
Closed circle on target.
Open circle short: the gap is the missing reps.
Circle that overlaps itself more than planned: the extra rep runs past the start.
Circle with a flare a personal best: rays flick out.
A heavier set draws a thicker stroke. The wet ink starts warm and dries to ink. Rest: the drops swirl together slowly.
