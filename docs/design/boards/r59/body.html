1 What it is

A basic Weather room, the first new room built on the weather words. Data: Open-Meteo (free, on by default, 0.1° rounding, 30 min cache): current, hourly 48 h, daily 7 days, feels-like, gusts, UV; air quality from Open-Meteo’s free air-quality API. Core’s WeatherCondition cases name every word. No city is ever shown; the place is just “here”.

2 The landing

Top: “Weather” in sky amber, the time on the right. Now: the temperature in Outfit 200 at 104 pt, coloured by the sky right now (cloud grey here), with feels-like and the day’s high and low. The sentence: two lines of what’s coming, each weather word acting out its weather (the 236 words). Next hours: one column per hour (time, temperature, one word in its colour), dragged across or up and down, snapping three hours at a time. The week: a single sentence, “Saturday is Sunny, …”, where each day’s weather word is a door to that day. Air: one line. Foot: Rain alert (tap: a receipt with Undo 10 s; you hear 30 minutes before rain starts).

3 A day

Tap a week word: the day slides in. Its name, the high large and the low in ink 3, its own sentence with its weather word, the hours for that day, sunrise and sunset. Back: “Weather” at the top, or the edge swipe (0 to 24 pt).

4 From Home

Tapping a weather word on Home’s board (Rain, Snow, Windy, …) opens this room through the doorway, with that word’s sentence first. The same words, colours and effects as Home (sheet 236 v2.8). The landing is a first cut: it will follow whatever landing system he picks.
