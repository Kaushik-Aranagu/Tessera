Room landings, layout first

No pixel art outside the Home mosaic. Every room lands on the same four bands, so the app feels like one place. What goes in each band is the room's own, which keeps the rooms from looking alike: a big headline drawn in type and fine lines, a brief, then a body laid out in one, two or three columns.

The four bands

HeadlineThe one thing that matters in this room right now, as large type: a number in Outfit ExtraLight or a word in Figtree. Beside it, one fine-line drawing that belongs only to this room. It replaces the lintel. No tiles, no fills. BriefTwo lines, right aligned, fixed. What's next and what's off ("Tacos tonight. Lemon is short."). Tess shortens it; it never wraps a third line. BodyLines on a shared 24 pt grid, so every column's baselines meet across the page. Column titles sit on one baseline. Numbers align on the right of their column. No rules between columns; a 22 pt gutter does the work. FootOne action word bottom left, Tess bottom right. Nothing else. Swipe from the left edge to go home.

Columns by room: one where order matters (Training's sets, Routines' evening), two where two things sit side by side (Kitchen's Pantry and Recipes, Lists, Notes), three where the room is a ledger (Money). Each column holds at most six lines, then pages.
