Living landings

His 12:07 to 12:09 AM: none of the 240 pages, no drawings, and not the mosaic either. Every phone is live: pluck, strike, stir, swipe, fling, and tap anything named to open it.

Touch

Each element answers the finger physically, then settles. A tap on anything named opens it with the usual slide. Doors are at least 44 pt. Reduce Motion: the element holds one calm frame, physics off, every door still works.

My pick
