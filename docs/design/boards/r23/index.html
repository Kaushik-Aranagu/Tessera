What to try

1

Swipe the widgets sideways. Weather, Day, Training, Kitchen, Money. The words above them follow, one is ink and the rest grey, with a tick when it changes. A piece of the next one peeks in so you know there is more. Tap a word to jump.

2

Press and hold on a chart, then slide. The reading changes under your finger: temperature through the day, the day's things, the bench week by week. A plain swipe still turns the page.

3

Colour is rationed: only the app words in the sentence and the one picked app. Everything else is ink.

4
