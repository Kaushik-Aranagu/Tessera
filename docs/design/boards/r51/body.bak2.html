Lists, one at a time

The Lists page from the Lists landing (226, 217). A dial of ways to look, your lists on the depth wheel with the front one opened a little, and a list on its own page where things get checked off and added. Three colors plus ink: Lists olive for the room and its actions, slate for labels, wine for what's due.

Lists

How it works

0Kinds across the top. All lists, Shopping, Trips, Home, People, Watch and read, each with its count. The one you're on sits first in ink. Swipe the row (or swipe across the dial, or tap a kind) and the dial's words, the lists and the action word all change with it: Shopping looks by To get, Due soon, Got it all; Watch and read by Up next, Halfway, Finished. The same row works in any room with more than one way to sort things. 1The dial, tighter. Ways to look, now 46 pt apart (was 64), so the wheel below gains a list and a half. The one you're on sits large in olive with its count; the neighbors step back and blur a little. Drag it or tap a neighbor; it snaps and loops. 2The depth wheel. The front list is open a little: its name in olive (26 pt, down to 21 to fit), then what's left and when it's due, then the next few things. The rest step back, smaller and softly out of focus. A small "Your lists" title sits above the first. Tap the front list to open it. 3A list. Things still to do first, then a "Done" label and what you've checked. Tap a thing (or swipe it right) to check it: a thin olive line draws through it and it stays put for 1.5 s (longer while you keep checking), then the run drops to Done together. Tap a struck thing before it drops to take it back. Swipe left for Not now: it moves to the end. Tap a done thing to bring it back. Long lists turn page by page. 4Adding. Tap Add: a new line opens at the top of what's left, ready to type; Return adds it and opens the next. Esc or tapping away ends and keeps what you typed. 5The action word takes gestures. On a list, drag Add left to Clear done, right to Uncheck all (packing again for the next trip), each with Undo for 10 s. Clearing says "Cleared 5 done", with Undo; cleared things stay in the op log for 7 days. On the landing the word names the kind: Start fresh, Start a run, Pack a bag, Start a project, Start a call list, Save a pick. Drag it right for a Private list. 6Due is the one wine touch: "Fri" beside a thing, and "due Friday" on the list. Labels are slate small capitals. A private list reads normally here and as "Private list" everywhere off the phone.
