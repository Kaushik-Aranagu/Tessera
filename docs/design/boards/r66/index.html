Round 283, version 6, Oct 25

Every landing, one layout.

Every landing now opens like Bridge and keeps Bridge’s lower half: the widget in the middle and the doors dial below it. The top holds the title, a text-only switcher and the room’s needs. Tap a room in Bridge’s apps dial (or a face, or a highlighted word) to watch the flip. Swipe from the left edge, or tap “Bridge”, to go back.

New in version 6 (his 4:47 and 4:48 PM notes). Back no longer moves the page. Pull from the left edge and the screen stays put: the content loosens and blurs a little and the nav word grows; let go past a short pull and every place changes in place, short of it everything settles back. The door summary beside the dial now changes in each room’s own way as you turn the dial. Log types its lines and backspaces the old ones. Lookout’s lines condense out of fog. The Deck chalks them on and wipes them off with a puff of dust. The Galley prints them like a ticket and tears the old one off. The Watch grows them from the ground and the old ones fall like leaves. The Purse keeps its words still and rolls only the numbers.

New in version 5 (his 4:21 and 4:22 PM notes). The screen never changes now. The places stay where they are and only what is inside them changes. Open a door and nothing slides in: the masthead flaps to the door’s name, the keystone stays (the Galley’s rail keeps its tickets), the widget and the doors leave and the door’s list settles in from the top. Back runs the same moves the other way and puts every place back as you left it. In Layout mode, tap the Dial bubble twice (or press the down arrow) to open a door: Switcher and Needs melt into one Door title, and the widget, glyph, dial and summary melt into one Page. Tap the Nav word twice (or the up arrow) and they split apart again.

New in version 4 (his 4:11 PM note). The whole screen is mapped below, with empty places kept and marked. Moving between Bridge and a room now goes place by place: the masthead flaps, the mosaic flips, the widget swipes up, the dial spins too fast to read and slows onto the new doors, the summary slides off right and back in, and the action word streams. Open a room from Bridge’s dial to watch it, and go back to see it reverse.

Layout mode. Settings, then Layout mode, shows the app as its places only: soft bubbles in each room’s colour, empty places dashed. Swipe across (or use the arrow keys) to walk Bridge and every room, and the bubbles move the way their places do. Tap a bubble to read what it holds, how you touch it and how it moves. It is read-only for now and can become editable later.

New in this version (his 3:57 PM notes). Deck now writes in a condensed gym face (Barlow Condensed), on the board and in the widget. Galley’s tickets are printed now, in IBM Plex Sans Condensed, and Caveat is gone.

The action word is a verb. The verb sits in the room’s colour with its object small under it (“Start” over “Push A, about 45 min”). Tap does it. Swipe across for the room’s other actions: the verb’s letters stream out and the next one’s stream in. Starting a workout asks for a hold: the verb fills with colour from left to right in half a second.

The glyph follows the widget. A library of 21 small drawings is picked by rules from the item’s words, and Tess picks later. The drawing never cuts. It collapses into a point, the point pulses once, and the new drawing forms out of it. When nothing fits, the glyph is built from the item’s own words, set like the wall.

The keystone gives the widget its item. The Log’s word lifts off the wall and lands as the big line, and the widget’s words drop in from the top. In the Galley the ticket tears off the rail. The Deck sheds chalk dust from the board, and the Lookout drips rain from the glass. In the Watch a bird flies down from the woods, and the Purse’s dot slides off the index.

Same lower half as Bridge, the text-only switcher at the top, and the flip are unchanged from version 2. Lookout’s mosaic blows apart like cloud.
