Every landing, one layout.

You asked for every landing to open like Bridge, with the depth-blur dial on the left, room summaries on the right, a switcher to scroll other things, and the mosaic flipping into each room’s keystone. Tap a room in Bridge’s apps dial (or a face, or a highlighted word) to watch the flip. Swipe from the left edge, or tap “Bridge”, to go back.

Five sections, the same place in every room. The top holds the title, “Bridge” to go back, and two summary lines where Bridge has its departure board. The keystone sits in the 354 by 182 slot at 196. Up next is at 422. The doors dial is on the left at 540, and the room’s own summary is on the right at 556. The action word is bottom left and Tess is bottom right.

The dial is the 246 one. The focus is sharp and big in its color. The neighbours above and below are smaller, gray and blurred. Bridge’s apps use it too, with one line on the right. In the rooms the right side shows more: a list, the next hours, the lifts, the tickets’ contents, the indices.

Up next is the switcher. It shows one thing, with the ones before and after small and blurred. Swipe it up or down to scroll through the room’s next things. Swipe right for Done, left for Not now (Undo 10 s). Tap to open it. Bridge has none, since its one thing already does this.

Opening. Every section rises 10 pt out of a 3 pt blur at 95% size, 80 ms apart. It is revealed by a clip, not faded in. Reduce Motion gets a 150 ms cross-fade.

The flip. Bridge’s mosaic is cut into 16 by 8 tiles. Each tile turns away over the new keystone, and each room turns its own way: the Log row by row like type, the Lookout from the middle out, the Deck in a sweep left to right like the towel, the Galley column by column like tickets, the Watch from the ground up, and the Purse left to right like a line drawing. Going back, paper tiles open on the mosaic. All of it finishes in under 1 s.
