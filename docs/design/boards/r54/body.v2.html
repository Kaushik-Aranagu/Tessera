Routines, one step at a time

The Routines page from the Routines landing (226, 219). Today's routines on the depth wheel, the next one opened a little, and a routine on its own page, where you work through its steps one at a time. Rhythm shows as weekday letters and stroke marks, never bars or rings. Three colors plus ink: Routines plum for the room and its actions, slate for labels, and wine only for something late.

How it works

1The week, as letters. Under the title, Monday to Sunday as single letters. A day you kept every routine is plum and heavier, a day with a gap is plain ink2, and today is a size larger in ink. To the right, the rhythm in words: "four nights of stretch". No bars, rings or counters. 2Today on the depth wheel. A small-caps "Today" header row sits above the first routine. The routine in front is open a little: its name in plum (26 pt, stepping down 1 pt at a time to 21, then cut at a whole word), then how many steps, how long and when, then its first steps. The rest step back smaller, with up to 1 pt of blur (never on the one in front; off under Reduce Motion or with Depth off). Done routines read in ink2 with the time you finished. Rows are 44 pt apart, so every one is a full tap target. A late one shows its time in wine, the page's one warning color. 3A routine on its own page. Its title, the rhythm in words ("Fourth night in a row"), and the last 14 days as stroke marks, all the same length: plum and heavier for a day you did it, light ink3 for a day you didn't. No count. Then its steps. The step you're on is plum, with one line on how to do it. 4Checking a step off. Tap a step, or swipe it right: a thin plum line draws through it, it waits 1.5 s, then it drops under Done and the next step opens. Swipe left for Not now: it moves to the end of the routine. Everything has Undo for 10 s. 5The action word takes gestures. On a routine, tap Start (later Next step). Drag it left for Skip today ("Skipped today.", recorded as skipped, never late) or right for Restart ("Evening stretch, restarted."; Undo puts the checked steps back), each with a haptic tick when it arms and Undo for 10 s. On today's page, Start opens the routine in front and starts it; drag left for Skip today, right for Later, 9 pm. 6Privacy. A private routine reads normally here and as "Private routine" everywhere off the phone (Lock Screen, widgets, Tess and Claude lines, backups, notices).
