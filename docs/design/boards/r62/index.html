Round 245, Oct 24

Word wall, as a band

His 1:13 AM: capitals, a Figtree or Bricolage feel packed tighter, two-word names stacked into one brick (same colour, same size) so the wall gets Tetris L and J shapes, and a band the size of the Home mosaic in the mosaic's own place. His 1:13 AM pile: inside the band, words tilted at angles, never on top of each other, still packed, then they tidy into the wall.

Font
Band

Paper. Deep tones, every brick its own colour; a stacked name keeps one colour across both lines.

Pure black. Light tints of the same twelve.

Where it sits. The band takes the Home mosaic's slot (the mosaic's middle, 140 to 440 pt, a third of the screen), so going from Home into a room the mosaic hands its place to the words. Above it the room name and one line; below it two plain lines and the action word.

Capitals and type. Every word in capitals, so each line box is only the cap height: no descender space, rows sit 3 pt apart and read as solid bricks. Each word is fitted to its brick exactly (the last few points go into letter-spacing). The pick is Bricolage narrowed to 84% width at 800: it keeps Bricolage's shapes and packs about 12% more. Figtree 900, plain Bricolage, Inter Tight and Mona Sans are on the toggles. Nothing under 13 pt.

Stacked names. A two-word name can stand as one brick on two lines in one colour and one size. The shorter line leaves a notch, and a small word in another colour drops into it, so the brick reads as a Tetris L or J; when no small word fits, the shorter line is spaced out to the full width.

The pile (the clever part). Words drop one at a time into the band, biggest first. Each one tries a few tilts (flat, slight, steep, upright) and many spots across the band, falls straight down until it touches the floor or another word, and keeps whichever spot lets it settle lowest. Words never overlap (tilted boxes are tested edge against edge with a 3 pt margin). If a word can't fit, every word shrinks a little and the drop runs again, so the pile always fills the band and stays packed. Then each word glides, turns and grows into its wall place, one after another.

Liveness. Every 6 s one word changes and the wall re-packs (spring 0.6 s). Tap a word to open it. Reduce Motion: no pile, just a cross-fade.
