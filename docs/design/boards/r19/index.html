Long lists

Fixed-size rows that change as you scroll or swipe. The rows never grow or jump; what is inside them leans, swells or follows your finger. Three ways to scroll, a stepped swipe, and a sideways slide between lists. Everything plays.

What to try

1

Scroll in Focus. A line sits a third of the way down. The row on it swells and goes full ink, its neighbours lean away and soften. Rows keep one height, so nothing jumps.

2

Scroll in Wheel. The list bends away from you like a drum, the middle row flat and readable.

3

Scroll fast in Wake. Rows trail your finger sideways, the ones further down later, then settle. Slow scrolling is still.

4

Swipe a row right. The strike follows your finger. At the first step it says Done, further on Done, and again tomorrow. A tick at each step; let go where you want. Swipe left for Not now, then Next week.

5

Slide along the three words at the top, or tap one. The rows slide out one after another and the next list slides in from the other side. The count sits next to each word.
