How it works

1The dial. Ways to look: This week, Pinned, Private, All. The one you're on sits large in Notes violet with its count. Drag it or tap a neighbour; it snaps one place at a time and loops. 2The wheel. The top note is open a little: its title in violet at 26 pt (down to 21 to fit, then whole words), then how old it is and how long, then its first words. Every other note is a title and its age, a step back: smaller, and softly out of focus (1 pt at most; none with Reduce Motion). Scroll and the next note comes up into the top slot; the one that was there goes round to the back. Tap another note to bring it up, tap the top one to open it. 3Fresh today is the one sienna touch: an age under a day ("2 h") and "today" in the detail line. Labels are slate small capitals. Everything else is ink. 6The action word takes gestures. On the list, tap Write for a new note, drag it right for a Private note. On a note, drag Write left to Delete note (Undo 10 s) or right for a New note; while typing, drag Done left to Put back the words as they were. The word leans with your finger and rolls to the option past 56 pt; a tick as it rolls, a firm tap when it commits. 4The note page. The title in violet, shrinking to fit (28 to 21 pt) before any short form. The words in Newsreader, the way his writing reads, in pages that turn up and down. Tap "Write" to type; the foot becomes "Done". "Keep at the top" and "Private" roll on a tap, with Undo. 5Private. Inside the room a private note reads normally, real title and words. Off the phone, in the landing headline, notices, widgets, Siri and backups, it reads "Private note". The page says so under the switch.
