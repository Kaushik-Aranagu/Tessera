Mosaic moods

The mosaic changes its whole temper with the day you're having, not just the hour. The three lines turn over letter by letter when the mood changes, so the words and the picture move together.

Each mood comes from what Next already knows: something running, a planned rest day, coming back after three or more days away, or the late evening. Nothing new leaves the phone. Tap a mood to see the change roll across; touch the mosaic to lean the tiles and send a ripple.
