Round 244, Oct 24

Word wall, packed tight

His 1:05 AM: a band filled like a wall of bricks, each brick a word, different sizes, colours and weights, packed tight with no gaps. Every row is justified to the full width by its own type size, small words stack two or three high inside one brick, and gaps between bricks are 3 pt. Tap Toss to throw them into a pile; they tidy themselves back. Every few seconds one brick changes and the wall re-packs.

Paper. The biggest bricks take their kind's colour, mid bricks ink, the smallest stacks ink 2.

Pure black. The same wall with lighter tints, so nothing glows.

How it packs. Words are sorted by weight (pinned, recent, due, how often opened). Rows are built greedily: a row takes words at a target size until it's full, then the row's type size is solved so the words plus 3 pt gaps meet both edges exactly (between 0.8 and 1.3 times the target size; any small remainder goes into letter-spacing). Low-weight words pair or triple into a stacked brick, each line solved to the brick's width. Row height is the cap-and-descender box (0.86 of the size), so rows sit 3 pt apart. Type: Archivo condensed (75% width) packs most and stays legible: 900 on rows set above 60 px, 800 below, 700 in stacks; nothing under 13 px; stacked words are paired by length so no line shrinks to nothing. Figtree and Bricolage are on the toggles to compare. Colours (his 1:05 AM: as many as it likes): every brick its own colour from a twelve-colour set, stepped so neighbours never match; deep tones on paper, light tints on black. Tap a word to open it. Liveness: every 6 s a word changes and every brick glides to its new place (spring 0.6 s). Toss: bricks fall into a heap at the bottom, tilted, then tidy into the wall. Reduce Motion: no drift or toss, just a cross-fade.
