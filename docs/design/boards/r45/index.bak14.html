The landing

1The day's plan, not a workout. The big word is today's session, then the week as day letters (done days in ink, today in blue). The brief says how it starts: 8 min on the rower, in the green. 2The plan is a depth wheel. The one you're on sits in front, large and in blue (green for the warm-up); the rest sit further back, smaller and softly out of focus. Before you start, the first is in front; mid-workout, the lift you're on. Drag or scroll to bring another forward; tap the one in front to open its page. 3Change the day. Tap 45 to step 25, 35, 45, All; tap Gym or Home. The plan shortens or swaps, and the count of lifts follows. 4The action word takes gestures. Tap Start Push A to begin. Drag it left: Skip today. Mid-workout (left edge or Esc from the workout comes back here) it reads Resume Push A: drag left to End workout, right to Start over. The word leans with your finger and rolls to the option past 56 pt; let go there to do it, with Undo for 10 s. A tick on the phone as it rolls, a firm tap when it commits.
1The warm-up. Cardio leads, in the green, with its own time. Done warming up goes to the first lift. 2The lift you're on owns the top. Its name in blue (tap it for the exercise page), which set you're on, the weight, and the bar. 3The session is a wheel. At the bottom, the lift you're on sits in focus, large and in blue. Lifts around it get smaller and lighter the further they are. Drag or scroll it; it snaps one row at a time. Tap a lift in focus to open its page, where Do it now swaps it in (Undo for 10 s). Leave it alone and it springs back to now. 4Change the weight two ways. Drag the number, or drag the bar itself (sideways or up and down). The plates reload either way. 5Change the unit. Tap or swipe the unit word: lb, kg. The number converts to what the bar actually holds and the plates reload. Every number with a unit in the app works the same way. 7Log set takes gestures too. Drag left: Skip this lift (on the last lift, End workout); during the warm-up, Skip warm up. Drag right after a set: Same again logs the last count. Left edge or Esc goes back to the landing; the workout keeps running. 8A set logs whole. Log set takes the planned reps. For 5 s the brief confirms it, "Set 1, 5 reps." The count scrolls up or down to set it right, and a tap opens a dial; each change restarts the 5 s, then it's kept as is. 6Sets as reps. The strokes for a set drop in one by one on a spring. Log set inks them left to right, each with a small hop, and a green strike draws across. The strokes then gather into the number, "5", which arcs over to the trail at the right and lands with a small bounce. "Set 1" rolls to "Set 2" and the rest runs in green. To log fewer, drag the strokes first.
