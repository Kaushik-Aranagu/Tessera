What to try

1

Slide a finger along the day. The ruler under the sentence is today, 6 am to midnight, painted in ink up to now. Where your finger is, the ticks swell and the line above tells you what is there.

2

Swipe right on a Next line. The ink strike follows your finger and the line closes up. Tess says what happened, with Undo. Swipe left for not now.

3

Pull up from the six lines at the bottom. Your apps rise one after another, each with where it stands. Slide to scrub, a tick for each. Let go on one and it opens; let go below them and nothing happens. Tap a line to open that app straight away.

4

Type "lemons" in the write line. Tess shows where it will go, in blue, before you send it.
