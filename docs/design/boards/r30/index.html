Settings overhaul

Everything you've approved is simply on. Settings is now a short page of plain lines about how you like things, not a wall of switches.

The rules

  1. On by default. Anything you approve, ask for, or I recommend ships turned on. There are no Review switches on the main page any more.
  2. One line per preference, written as a sentence, with its value at the right. Tap a line and its choices open in place, as words; the chosen one is underlined.
  3. Conflicts are a choice, not two switches. Where two things do the same job, like the two ways pages turn, you pick one.
  4. Developer mode is for turning things off. Hold the build line at the bottom to open it. Every feature is listed by app, on, with one tap to turn it off and one to turn it back on. It never adds anything new.
  5. Easier to find. Settings sits at the top right of every page, beside a small knob made of the screen's own pixels, and it turns when you press. The page arrives with the pixel flip.

What stays on the main page

You, Home, Tess, Training, Kitchen, Privacy, Look, Safe keeping, About: about twenty lines in all, down from 65 switches. Your Claude key keeps its own page and is never shown here.
