Living ideas

More ideas like the mosaic: something on Home with a life of its own that also tells you about your day. Each one plays. Pick the ones you want; any of them can sit where the mosaic sits, or take turns with it.

Departure board

The day sentence on a split-flap board, like an old station. When Home opens, every letter clacks through the alphabet and lands, left to right, with a soft tick per line. Tap it and it flips to the next thing worth saying: what is next, the weather, a best you just set.

Feels like: arriving. Tells you: the day, one sentence at a time.

Run a finger down across the threads.

Loom

Your day as threads stretched across the page, one per part of the day. Run a finger through them and they twang: a wave runs along each one and settles, with a tick as you cross. Each thing you finish is woven in as a short stitch across the threads at its hour, so the cloth gets denser as the day goes.

Feels like: a harp. Tells you: how full the day was, where.

Drag to tip it.

Hourglass

What is left of today as grains of sand. They trickle down through the evening; at midnight the glass turns. Drag sideways and the sand slides and piles. Each thing you finish drops in as a darker stone that stays.

Feels like: holding time. Tells you: how much day is left, how much got done.

Stir it with a finger.

Pinwheels

The mosaic's cousin: every tile is a small blade that can turn. The day's colours run across them; a finger stirs a swirl that spreads and slowly unwinds. Through the day the blades change shape, square at dawn, diamond at noon, slim at night.

Feels like: wind in a field. Tells you: the hour by its shape and colour.
