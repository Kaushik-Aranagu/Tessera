Notes: the pages behind the doors

His 1:28 AM: after the landing, the deeper pages behind each door finish Notes. Each door page keeps the landing's grammar: a slim word wall of that door's notes on top, the dial under it, and the focused note opened a little beneath. Going in, the landing's words that belong to the door stay and re-pack; the rest dissolve.

Paper.

Pure black.

Going in. Tap the dial's focus on the landing (or a door above). The words on the wall that live behind that door stay, glide and grow into a slimmer wall (150 to 300 pt); every other word dissolves into the page. The title changes to the door's name in its colour, and the doors dial gives way to the notes dial. Coming back (edge swipe, or the "Notes" word top-left) runs it in reverse: the door's words shrink back and the others rebuild around them.

His 2:08 AM (supersedes the dial on door pages). The dial belongs to landings only. The door wall is smaller, about 55% of the landing's (at most 124 pt), and never opens a gap: when the packing comes up short, the band shrinks to fit instead. Below it, a different list: the ledger (pick) or pages. Ledger: a left column (84 pt) names the group, written only where a group starts, in room colour small caps: Today, Yesterday, This week, Earlier (Mentions groups by kind: Exercise, Recipe, Person, Place, Routine). The right column holds each note as its title with when, and one short line under it. Tap a note to open it in place: its first lines and meta appear and the rest move down; tap the open one to open the note. Pages: four notes a page, each title big with two lines under it; turn sideways (or tap Older) to the next four, no counters. A long ledger turns pages the same way, never scrolls.

Private (his 2:08 AM). Off the private page, private notes are counted, never listed: "3 private notes", not "Private note" three times. Inside, after Face ID, the titles show.

Every door page (first cut; its dial is superseded). Door name (Figtree 700, 34 pt, its colour) and one line. A wall of the door's own notes, packed by the same rules (capitals, no gaps, even letters; repeats fill a small door). The notes dial (3 places, 48 pt): the focus in ink at 1.32 times with when it was written at the right; neighbours smaller in ink 2 with depth blur. Under it, the focused note opened a little: its first three lines and one meta line (mentions, list items, length). Tap a wall word and the dial turns to that note; tap the focus to open the note. The action word bottom-left belongs to the door.

What each door adds. All notes: everything, newest first. Ideas: notes Tess tagged as ideas; action "New idea". From Tess: notes Tess wrote for you, previewed in her own voice (Newsreader, Tess blue); action "Ask Tess". Voice memos: the focus shows its length in Outfit and the start of the transcript; action "Record". Pinned: only a few, so the dial holds them all. Mentions: the dial turns through the things your notes mention (an exercise, a recipe, a person) with how many notes mention each; the preview lists those notes. Private: locked until Face ID; the wall and dial appear only on the phone and lock again when you leave; off the phone they read "Private note". Archive: older notes by date; action "Restore".

Moments. The same as the landing, on the slimmer wall: one word every 5 s, re-pack every 40 s, a whole-wall moment every 2 minutes. Reduce Motion: none, and going in cross-fades.
