Kitchen, at a glance

The Kitchen landing says what you could cook tonight and how the pantry stands, in a few numbers and two short lines. Cook leads to the recipes you can make, and every recipe has Cook now. While you cook, the top of the landing becomes the cooking: the step you're on and its timers.

The landing

1Tonight, in one name. The big word is something you can cook right now with what's here. Tap another (or swipe the name up) and it rolls to the next one, like a reel. Tap the name to open the recipe. 2The pantry in three numbers. On hand, low, to get. They count up when you arrive. Low is in the Kitchen colour; the brief names what runs out first and what wants using. 3Recipes in two lines. How many you can make now and what's planned. The Pantry and Recipes pages come next, on their own. 4Cook opens the recipes you can make, each with Cook now, and New recipe at the foot.
1Cooking owns the top. The dish, the step you're on, and the running timer, large. The brief says what comes next. 2Timers stack as plain lines under the brief, soonest first. A timer that finishes turns into words: "Pasta's ready." 3The rest of the Kitchen stays, shorter: one line for the pantry, one for what's planned. 4Next step moves on. The last step reads Done cooking, and the pantry takes what you used, with Undo for 10 s.
