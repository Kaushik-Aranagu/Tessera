Tessera, instruments

The pages kept turning into sentences. This round is the parts you actually touch: a drum, a ruler, a crown, a lens and a scroll with a spine. Drag each one. They are the numbers and lists on every page, not new pages.

The drum

Every weight, rep count and amount is a drum you spin. The number in the middle is full size and the rest roll away from you. The decimal hangs off the side so the whole number never moves. Last time and your best sit on the drum itself.

Training sets, Kitchen amounts, Money, anywhere a number is typed today. Drag, flick, or turn the scroll wheel. A tick plays at every step: 0 so far.

The ruler

Time and length are a ruler under your thumb. The fine ticks are 5 seconds, a taller one is 15, the tallest is a minute. The mark in rose is where you are. What you chose last time is marked on the ruler too.

Rest between sets, timers, cooking steps, quiet hours. Left and right, or the scroll wheel.

The crown

For a stretch of time, turn the crown. Ink fills clockwise from the top, a tick every 5 minutes, and the page tells you when it ends, in your clock.

Focus, routines, anything with a length. Go around the dial with one finger.

The lens

A long list does not need a search first. Put a finger on it and the line under it grows and opens its detail while its neighbours make room. Lift and it settles. No outline, no pop-up: the list itself is the magnifier.

Exercises, recipes, pantry, lists, the rail. Touch and slide up and down.

The spine

Scrolling a long history shows you where you are. The strip at the edge is the whole record as a barcode: a long ink tick for every day you trained, a short one for a rest day. Drag the strip to jump.

Training log, Review, Money, Notes. The date above follows you.
