What to try

1

Turn the ring. It is today, 6 in the morning at the bottom left, round the top to midnight at the bottom right. Drag your finger round it: the number and the words beside it follow, and snap to what is there. The thin outer line is daylight. The thick coloured stretches are your things, the dark line is the part already gone.

2

Tap a coloured word in the sentence to open that thing. Swipe a Next line: right strikes it, left is not now.

3

Scroll the apps. One line high, the one in the middle is the pick: it swells in its colour and shows its drawing and what is happening. A tick each time it changes. Tap it to open; tap another to bring it to the middle. The little ticks at the right show where you are in the list.

4

Tap the knob by Settings. It turns and Settings opens from the corner, with Review in it.
