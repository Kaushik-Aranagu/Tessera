# Items 155 to 159: Round 7, five run modes and rituals (Oct 23)

Loop round 7 (5-minute cadence, Kaushik 5:26 PM PDT). Each sits behind its own Review switch,
default Off. Engine pieces already exist: Shopping, FocusBlock, DayPlan, NowPicker,
QuietInterrupts. Grammar from 127 and 131, HouseNumber from 138, finishing from 139.

## 155 Shop run (switch shopV2, Builder B)
Opens from Pantry's "To get" or "Shop" on the Kitchen landing.
- The page leads with the next item in large words. Under it, the rest of the same aisle as
  plain lines. The aisle word ("Produce") is a label that rolls to the next aisle.
- Swipe right on an item: struck, gone. The strike follows the finger and gives a soft tap.
- A typed price ("4.50") lands as an amount on the item. The basket total is "about {n}" in
  ink 2 at the foot, phone only, never sent to Claude.
- Finish ("Done shopping", ochre underline). 139 style moment: "12 got, 2 left for next
  time." Then "Put away" walks each item to its Pantry place with one tap ("Fridge").
- A running shop is a process: it appears in Now and on Home's day sentence ("Shopping,
  6 left. Go on").

## 156 Focus (switch focusV2, Builder B)
From any task's "Do it" (140) or a long press on a Next row: "Focus for 25 min".
- The page: the task in large words, the first step under it in ink 2. Time left is a
  level line that empties left to right, ink on a 12% track. No digits, unless he taps the
  line, which shows "14 min left" for 3 s.
- Quiet: QuietInterrupts holds notices, so the Live Activity reads "Quiet: 2 waiting".
- Halfway: Tess's one check-in line, existing FocusBlock.
- End: "25 min on {task}." and the held digest as one line ("3 things waited: ...").
  Then "Done" (strike) or "Keep going" (10 min more).

## 157 Plan my day, v2 (switch planV2, Builder B; Core DayPlan)
Each morning, the first time he opens Home after 6 AM, Home shows the proposed day as a
sentence, before the usual day sentence: "Gym at 7, then the report until 11. Lunch, then
calls. Tacos at 6:05." Each clause is a word group he can:
- drag to reorder (a selection tick at each slot), with times re-flowing as digits roll
- swipe left to move to tomorrow ("Not today")
- tap to open.
Keep (cobalt underline, Tess's offer) or "My usual day". Every failure falls back to
"Here's your usual plan." as today. Calendar events are plain words, never moved.

## 158 Find (switch findV2, Builder B + Core)
In the write line, starting with "?" or "find" turns it into search. Matches show live under
the line as words grouped by app: the app name as a label, then up to 3 titles, each an
.opens word with its app's link underline (127 A8). Private things match only by count
("2 private notes"). Nothing leaves the phone. Return opens the first match.
Core: Find.query(text) -> [(app, [ObjectRef])], built on ObjectStore titles and fields,
walled fields excluded.

## 159 Sunday reset (switch weekReset, Builder B; Core facts)
Sunday after 4 PM (or "Plan the week" on Review): one page, next week in sentences, one per
app with a key word in its accent:
"Training: 4 sessions, Push Pull Legs, rolls on." / "Kitchen: 3 dinners planned, 2 open."
/ "Money: rent Friday, card the 28th." / "Lists: Trip packing, 6 left." / "Tasks: 9 for
the week, 2 carried."
Tap a sentence: that app's plan view (the rail on its week place). The foot word "This
looks right" (ink underline) logs it as done. No scores, and nothing about last week's
misses.
