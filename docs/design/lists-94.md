# 94. Lists shelf and a list page, paged

Product ask Oct 17. Sheet 90's rules applied to Lists. Spec only.

## The shelf (all lists)
- A page holds six lists or fewer; the page word is the group: "Pinned", "Reusable", "Others" (empty groups are skipped; a group of more than six splits at the nearest even break, odd splits larger first).
- A row is the list's name (Body 21/500) with its open count at the right as a number ("7"). A reusable list shows "reusable" in the context line only on its page, never on the shelf.
- Groceries is the first row of page 1 and cannot be copied; Private lists read "Private list".
- The add field is pinned at the foot of every page.
- Sideways is the side swipe (sheet 75): on the shelf it shows the lists' run history.

## A list page
- Open items first, six rows or fewer a page, in their sections: a section word is one page's word; a section of more than six continues on the next page under the same word.
- A done item folds out of the page into the fold, which is pinned and never paged (it is the foot).
- A swipe on a row's quantity steps it; on the words it turns the page (sheet 90b).
- Pick-one lists keep their pick moment: the page dims to 14% (sheet 87), no paging while it is up.
- The rail and peek word as in sheet 90; a list of six rows or fewer is one page and draws neither.
