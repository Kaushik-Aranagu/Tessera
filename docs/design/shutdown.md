## "Tomorrow, first," typed
- Tap the writing line. The keyboard rises (system, 340ms) and the line rides up with its label to sit just above the keys (y 468, label at 444). The opener, carry-over, rows and Shutdown complete fade back (blur 3, 420ms). The tabs, day label, stroke and context line stay.
- The caret is cobalt. He types in Body 21 `ink`. Return reads **done**. Done (or a tap above the keys) lowers the keyboard, the line rides back to 618, and the column returns. Tess says nothing: the line on the page is the answer.
- Nothing is stored yet. The line is written when Shutdown completes.

## "Tomorrow, first," said
- **Hold the soul** (speak.md's fast path, anywhere). It blooms in its corner and listens while held.
- While Shutdown is open, the heard words stream **into the writing line itself**, in `inkSecondary`, each one rising out of a 6pt blur.
- Let go. Tidying squeezes out the lead phrase (width to 0, 320ms), and the rest crossfades to wet cobalt, breathing at 0.5 to 0.66, which dries to `ink` over 3s (voice dry time). Tess says "Tomorrow, first: reply to the landlord." beside the soul. There's no UNDO, because nothing is written yet; tapping the line edits it.
- **What counts as the lead phrase:** "tomorrow first", "tomorrow, first", "first thing tomorrow", and "first tomorrow", spoken or typed, at the start. A phrase without it while Shutdown is open goes to Tess's capture as usual, not the line.
- **When Shutdown isn't open:**
  - Before 21:00, it's stored as tomorrow's line, Tess says the same line with **UNDO** under it ("Tomorrow line taken back."), and Shutdown opens at 21:00 with the line already written.
  - On the closed day, until 04:00, it replaces the closed-day hero (crossfade) with the same Tess line and UNDO.
- Typed into Tess's Write, the same phrases do the same thing.

## The hold and the closed day
- **The hold** (0.7s): the ink line empties to a 0.9pt trace at 35%, repaints, then the bead. `.success` plays (§7's one exception; 21:00 is before quiet hours).
- **The handoff:**
  1. The day stroke paints the rest of its length to its vermilion end (700ms, house curve), and the dashed guide fades.
  2. Each kept row's TOMORROW lifts toward the stroke's end (620ms, 80ms stagger), shrinking and blurring. LATER rows don't move.
  3. The column folds away (blur 3). The soul plays `done`.
- **The closed day** settles at once, with no "Done for today." beat (Tess's line says it):
  - The day label crossfades to "Thursday night", and the context line to "tomorrow, first".
  - **The hero** is his line in Hero 56 `inkSecondary`, up to 2 lines ("Reply to the landlord"), with "THEN PULL A · 07:30" in labels under it. With no line, the hero is tomorrow's first timed item ("Pull A") with "07:30" under it.
  - The soul goes to `resting`.
  - **Tess says the close** (slot E, or F3/F4 late at night): "Closed. Your line is waiting at 08:00." with **UNDO** under it for 10s.
- **What the hold writes:** kept rows move to tomorrow, LATER rows move to Later, the tomorrow line is stored, and the day's Day log line (slot A) is frozen.
- **UNDO**, within 10s: Tess says "Shutdown taken back." All of it reverses. Shutdown comes back as it was, with his line still written and each row's TOMORROW or LATER as he left it. The stroke unpaints to now, the guide returns, the day label and context go back to "Thursday evening" and "this evening", and the soul wakes. No `.success`.
- **From 22:00 without a hold**, the day closes by itself as `evening-on-now.md` §4 says: a crossfade, no Tess line, no UNDO, and CLOSE THE DAY brings Shutdown back until 04:00.

## Reduce Motion and VoiceOver
- Reduce Motion: crossfades only. No lift-off, no stroke paint, no bead, no keyboard ride (the column hides and the line shows above the keys in place).
- VoiceOver reads the three lines in order, then each row as "Book the physio, tomorrow" or "Clean the oven, later", then "Tomorrow, first: Reply to the landlord", then "Shutdown complete, hold to close the day".

## Never
An evening wash or night ground, dots, a count of what didn't happen, "left over", "overdue", red, a sound, a haptic after 23:00 except under his finger, an UNDO on the 22:00 close.
