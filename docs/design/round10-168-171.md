# Items 168 to 171: Round 10, week in a thread, backups, how often Tess speaks, big text (Oct 23)

Loop round 10 (10-minute cadence, power saver). Lane named per item. Each behind its own Review
switch, default Off (171 is a rule sheet, no switch). Grammar from 127 and 131.

## 168 The week in a thread (lane: UI and UX, switch weekThreadV2, Builder A)
Routines page, below the day: the Thread signature drawn across seven days.
- One rose line (A4505F/D79AA6) runs left to right through the days he did the routine, with a
  small loop on each day done; a missed day is a gap in the line, no mark, no red, no count.
- Under it one sentence: "Morning, 5 of 7 days." Tap a day to see that day's run in plain words.
- Drag along the line to scrub weeks; digits roll, a tick per week. Reduce Motion: no draw-in.

## 169 Backups, in words (lane: data and architecture, switch backupsV2, Core with Builder B)
Settings > Backups becomes a page of sentences.
- "Last backup tonight, 9:14 pm. 4,210 things." The count is a Measure-style number (Outfit).
- "Back up now" (plain underline). "Restore" opens a list of the last 7 backups as dates; a
  restore always shows what it would change in one sentence first, and offers Undo for 10 min.
- Walls stay: health, calendar, body, calories and walled titles are never in a backup and the
  page says so in one line: "Not included: health, body and private things."
- Money amounts: counted in the total, never listed on this page.

## 170 How often Tess speaks (lane: AI, switch tessQuietV2, Core)
Settings > Tess gets one control: Tess speaks "Rarely", "Sometimes" or "Often".
- A budget of lines per day: Rarely 1, Sometimes 3, Often 6. Counts offers, nudges and
  noticed lines; never answers to something he asked, never a safety or timer notice.
- Never during Focus (156) or Quiet (167). When the budget is spent, Tess is silent until
  tomorrow and the page says "Tess has said her 3 for today."
- Phone only; the counter is not synced, logged or sent to Claude. Cobalt stays Tess's colour.

## 171 Big text rules (lane: UI and UX, rule sheet, all builders)
How every v2 page holds at Accessibility sizes (AX1 to AX5). Checked in each PR's stills.
- Two columns collapse to one: a label and its value stack, label above in ink 2.
- Level lines drop their numbers and keep only the fill under the word (pantry rule).
- Fans, edges and sheets scroll; nothing clips; no "..." anywhere.
- Underlines stay one text line wide, not full width. Acts keep a 44 pt target.
- Outfit numbers scale with the text; HouseNumber hangs decimals at every size.
- A PR for a v2 page needs one AX5 still with its Design verdict.
