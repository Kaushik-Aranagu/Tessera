1. Library > Workouts: the Library knob (Exercises | Workouts | Programs) on its own line
   under the subtitle. Dial = kind of session (Push, Pull, Legs, Short, Full body); pinned
   "Tried" (hot). Rows = name left, time right ("25 min"). A workout that needs equipment the
   current place lacks stays at 30% ink with one line saying why ("needs a cable; Home has
   none"); never hidden.

   A section "Instead of today's Push A" says what happens: "Push A stays on the Plan; it
   moves to Thursday." (rolling plan rule; the Plan is never rewritten). Actions: "Try
   today" (ink, hot underline) and "Save" (quiet; copies it into his routines).
3. Today after Try today: subtitle names the workout; the big number is the minutes; one
   line "Trying Upper in 25 today. Push A on Thursday." Actions "Start" and "Undo" (10 s,
   then the word leaves).
4. Similar: from the exercise page, or from Busy on a live set. Groups in words: "Same
   muscles, same pattern", then "Near". Each row shows his last weight ("last 30") or
   "new". A row that the place lacks or that is marked busy stays at 30%. Swap starts from
   his last weight, or a light guess marked "about". Actions: "Swap in" (changes the
   routine, with Undo) and "Just today" (this session only). No muscle figure.
Words: sentence case everywhere; no middle dots; "best" never "record". AX5: rows wrap,
right values drop under the name.
