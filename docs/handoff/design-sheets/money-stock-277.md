## The dictionary (what every part means)
| Stock chart | Here | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| Ticker | YOU · OCT | your month is the instrument |
| Open | payday balance (4,200 on 30 Sep) | where the month started |
| Price line (solid, sage) | still yours, day by day | every payment a tick down, income a tick up |
| Last price | still yours now (big number) | the thing you most want |
| Change vs | ▲/▼ n VS LAST MONTH at today | ahead or behind yourself |
| After-hours (dashed, ink) | forecast at your pace, with known bills placed as drops on their dates | where you are heading |
| Fan behind the forecast | your calmest pace to your heaviest pace this month | how sure the forecast is |
| Close | the forecast on the 31st | what you end the month with; the number Tess talks about |
| Support line (dotted clay) | PROMISED TO BILLS: the sum still owed this month | everything above it is spendable |
| Spendable | still yours minus promised | the honest number for "can I buy this" |
| Prior close line (faint) | last month's line | the comparison without reading |
| Volume bars | what went out each day, stacked by kind colour | heavy days, quiet weeks, where it went |
| Earnings date (▲ at 31) | next payday | the jump that resets the chart |
| Watchlist | JAPAN (savings) and PORTFOLIO sparklines, with their monthly figures | the longer game, small |
| Headline | Tess reads the chart in one sentence, cycling every 4.5 s | "Food is running 37% over last month." |
| Scrub | drag: any day, past or future, with its events | the whole ledger is in the line |

Pace = average of non-fixed spending per day (fixed = home, subs, saving, gym), so one rent payment does not pollute
the forecast. No red or green: sage for yours, ink for forecast, kinds in their colours.

## Alive
The line draws itself on open; the today-dot breathes; a payment flashes a ring and grows today's bar; the forecast
and the close re-draw instantly; the portfolio sparkline ticks during market hours; the headline cycles.

## Ideas to extract more (next)
- Candles: per day open/close/high/low of still-yours (intraday spend and refunds), one tap away.
- Annotations: tap a point on the past line to see that day's receipts; long-press a future drop to defer a bill and see
  the close move.
- "What if": drag a phantom purchase onto the chart and watch the close before you buy.
- Splits: drag a day range to see the kinds for just that range.
- Dividends: investment income as small ▲ ticks on the portfolio spark; a goal reaching target as a flag.
- Quarter and year views by pinch: the month lines overlaid, the close of each month as a dot.

## v3: the next layer (his go-ahead, Oct 25)
- Two levels: the slot shows the compact chart (fewer labels: no ticker name, pace line or volume caption); tap opens
  the full chart. The sandbox on the board is the full chart.
- Scrub a past day: its receipts list by the hairline (name and amount, in kind colour); a future day lists the bills
  due and says "hold to defer".
- Hold a drop ahead (520 ms): the bill is deferred to next month; a faint dotted stub marks where it was ("PHONE → NOV");
  the promised floor and the close move at once.
- What if: a slider handle right of the chart (sandbox) or a preset; a slate ghost forecast shows the month if you spent
  that today, labelled "IF 600 TODAY · closes at -421". Release clears it. Nothing is logged.
- Range: drag under the chart across days; the days shade, a stacked bar across the top of the volume shows the range
  by kind, with the total.
- Year (pinch out in the app; a toggle on the board): every month payday to payday as a faint line, this month in sage,
  and the twelve closes as dots along the bottom with month initials; Tess's line reads the year.
- Fixes: the close label sits inside the chart's right edge; last month's label mid-line; what-if label on its line.
