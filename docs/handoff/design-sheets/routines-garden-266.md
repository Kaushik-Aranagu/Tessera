## A. The windowsill (recommended)
Your sill at home in the standard slot. The window IS the Weather skypiece's sky (same real plates, same hour and
weather: rain in Weather rains on the garden). A real wood sill. Routines are real potted plants: a daily one is
thirsty (thyme), a weekly one is slow (succulent), an empty pot is a routine not planted yet. Doing a routine waters
its plant (a gold mark on the sill); a skipped day droops it; days of neglect grey and brown it (the ivy here, "dry
since Saturday"); a planned rest is a pot moved to the shade. Steps are leaves. Tess's one line on the glass. Tap a
pot to open; behind a door the pots of that door; on a routine's page its one plant, large.
Material: CC0 photos (rawpixel, Flickr CC0) keyed out; Poly Haven wood_table_001; our sky plates. For the build:
plants shot/generated at five growth states + a droop, same camera, so growth is plates changing, never a drawing.

## B. The woodland
The grove on a real plate (Poly Haven misty_pines, tonemapped). Routines are trees; done = gold mark at the base,
skipped = hollow, next = the accent ring, neglected = the tree's patch of the photo desaturating and browning (a
soft mask over the plate, not a drawn tree). Weekly routines stand further back in the mist. Night = the plate
darkened and cooled; dusk = warmed. Tess's line at the top.
