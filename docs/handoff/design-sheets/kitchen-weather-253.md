## Kitchen: pull apart is the theme
- The landing keeps the brief sentence at the top ("Tacos tonight, 25 min, you have all 8 things. Eggs and milk run
  out first."), then five section words in Figtree 900 52 pt ink, 66 pt apart, each with its count in Outfit 300
  18 pt ink2: Tonight, Pantry (31), Recipes (4 now), Groceries (4), Leftovers (2).
- Tap a word: its letters spread from the middle (about 30 pt per step), lift 10 pt and shrink to 40 pt in Kitchen
  ochre, over 0.6 s on a spring; the other words slide to make room (0.55 s) and dim to 40% with 0.6 pt blur; the
  section's first screen appears in the gap, in place, with no new page. Tap the word again to close.
- Inside: Tonight = the dish, time, its ingredients with missing ones in wine. Pantry = the five places with counts
  (two columns). Recipes = what you can make now, with times. Groceries = what to get, by aisle. Leftovers = what goes
  out when. The foot word follows the open section (Cook, Add a thing, Cook, Got it, Eat it).
- The same move opens a dish into its ingredients on Recipes and a place into its things on Pantry.
- Open: his "something about the refrigerator that could help". Recommend: Leftovers and Pantry open from a Fridge
  word and show what goes out when; not a keystone.

## Weather: the page is the sky
- The whole page is a live sky painted from the hour and the condition. Sun on its real arc (rise 7:25, set 6:06),
  brightness and sky gradient from its altitude (night slate, dawn gold, day blue to pale, dusk amber). Moon on its
  own arc (today 3:48 pm to 2:12 am) in its real phase (waxing gibbous), lit disc with an offset shadow disc. Stars
  come out as the sun goes. Clouds: soft layered discs drifting, cover by condition. Rain and storm: streaks the
  whole height; a storm flashes every 2.5 to 6.5 s with a bolt. Fog: moving haze bands. Snow: drifting flakes. A low
  darker band at the foot gives the page a floor.
- Text color is computed from the sky's brightness (ink on bright skies, warm white otherwise), so every line reads.
- The slot (196 to 378) holds plain numbers: temperature Outfit 200 118 pt with the degree at 48, feels like and
  high/low at the right, and four small facts along the bottom (wind, humidity, UV, sunset) in Outfit 300 24 pt
  with small-caps labels.
- Under it: one 24 pt line on what is coming ("Cloudy now, rain from 7 PM."), the next hours strip (59 pt columns),
  then CARE: nudges from the forecast and the other rooms (take the umbrella at 7; close the windows before the
  storm; water the balcony plants tonight, no rain till Thursday; sunscreen at noon, UV 7; frost tonight, bring the
  basil in). Rows of 46 pt, swipe right or tap = done (strike), Undo 10 s. Foot: Rain alert, Tess.
- Board controls under the phone: hour (now, 6 am to 3 am) and sky (clear, cloudy, rain, storm, fog, snow).
- Data: Open-Meteo for sunrise and sunset; the moon's rise, set and phase computed on the phone.

## Verdicts (Kaushik, Oct 25)
- KITCHEN: pull apart is right, but NOT the landing. It is the move for level 1 and 2 pages (opening a section, a
  dish, a place). Make it dramatic: what opens is its own layer on top of the page, and everything behind blurs out
  strongly. The Kitchen landing still needs a keystone in the same slot and layout as Home, Notes and Routines, for
  consistency across every landing. More landing ideas wanted.
- WEATHER: the living sky is the idea, but it must look REAL: real-looking (photographic) skies, a sun that looks
  real and moves, real clouds and rain, adjusting to light and dark looks. Build a library of seemingly real pieces.
- WEATHER KEYSTONE: in the same slot as every landing, an EMBOSSED GLASS slab lifted off the real background so it
  never hides it, with frosted or refractive edges, and live, dynamic information inside that has some life. His
  ask, so an exception to the no-glass rule (like the Notes areas pane).
