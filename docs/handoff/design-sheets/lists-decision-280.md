# 280 Lists: shared tool or its own keystone (decision sheet, Oct 25; awaiting his pick)

## What is already decided about Lists
- Lists is a room (217, 226, 237 v3): a kinds row (Shopping, Trips, Home, People, Watch and read), a dial of lenses per
  kind, a depth wheel of lists, a list page with check / not now / add / clear done / uncheck all, private lists, and
  the glass lens pattern that every horizontal swipe in the app now uses. None of this is in question.
- Groceries are not a list: they are Pantry "To get" in Kitchen (237 ruling; the Kitchen pass rail of 259 carries them).
- The keystone rule (Oct 25): every landing has one centrepiece in the same 354 x 182 slot, live through the app.
- Lists is the only room without a keystone decision. Its kinds cut across rooms: Shopping belongs with Kitchen, Trips
  and Home with Routines or Home, People with Notes, Watch and read with Notes.

## The two ways
**A. Shared tool.** A list is an object any room can hold and open in place: Kitchen's To get is a list; a trip's
packing list opens from the trip; a reading list from Notes; a call list from People. The Lists room stays as the
place where every list is gathered (and the only place private lists show their names), but it does not hunt for a
scene of its own: its slot shows the Sheaf (249, approved in Routines), every list as a stack of paper edges, pull one
to fan it. Same 354 x 182, same rules, nothing new to invent. Cost: one shared `ListView` surface and the Sheaf, which
is built.
- For: lists have no live state to animate (a list does nothing while you are away; a woodland grows, money moves, the
  sky changes), so a scene for them would be decoration, which is the thing the keystone rule exists to prevent. The
  Sheaf is already the approved form for "many lists, one glance". Every other room gets lists for free. One fewer
  concept round.
- Against: the Lists landing is quieter than the others; someone opening Lists for its own sake meets a tool, not a
  world.

**B. Its own keystone.** Lists keeps a full landing and gets a centrepiece hunt like Routines and Money had. The
honest candidates all come from what a list is: a pile of paper (the Sheaf, scaled up and lit), a pinboard, a drawer,
a string of things to carry. The function it could carry: what is due this week across every list, and what you are
in the middle of (half-packed, half-read).
- For: parity; a world for people who live in lists.
- Against: it repeats the hardest part of this session for the room with the least to animate; the Oct 23 reviews
  already rejected door grids and "sections of lists" for Routines, and Lists would face the same.

## Recommendation
A, with one addition: the Sheaf in Lists' slot is live in the one way lists are: the edges of the stacks are the
things, and a due thing's edge is wine; a stack with something due this week sits a little proud of the others. That
is the keystone, and it costs nothing new. If he wants more world than that later, it can be added without undoing A.

## If A
- Record: Lists = shared tool + the Sheaf as its slot. Builder B owns `ListView` (open in place from any room) and the
  Sheaf in Lists.
- Doors under the slot: the kinds (Shopping, Trips, Home, People, Watch and read), as in 237.
- Rooms that embed lists show them with their own accent (Kitchen ochre, Notes violet); in the Lists room, olive.

## If B
- Next round: three candidates drawn plainly, in the slot, with the due-this-week function; the Sheaf as the control.

## Verdict (Kaushik, Oct 25): neither A nor B. Lists fold into Notes.
His words: "Integrate lists into Notes. Notes could also have lists; lists are a special kind of note which can be
checked off or completed. A shopping list can be part of the list, shown in Notes as well. Maybe name Notes something
else so it makes more sense to include lists. We don't need a separate app."
Consequences:
- No Lists app, no Lists keystone. The Lists room (217, 226, 237) becomes the list kind inside the renamed Notes room:
  a list is a note whose lines can be checked. Everything 237 decided about a list page (check, not now, add, clear
  done, uncheck all, private, due in wine, the glass lens) carries over as the behaviour of a checkable note.
- The Notes word wall stays the keystone; list titles and open items are words on it too (an item due this week in
  wine, a checked item struck).
- Kinds row in the renamed room: Notes · Lists (Shopping, Trips, Home, People, Watch and read as sub-kinds) · the
  existing Notes kinds. The Lists olive 5E6B1F / B5BE7A becomes the colour of the list kind inside the room; the room
  accent stays Notes violet (the Ember-distance fix for violet is still open from 241).
- Shopping: Kitchen's "To get" remains the live grocery list on the pass rail; the same list shows in the renamed room
  as a list note (one object, two rooms), in Kitchen ochre there and in olive here.
- Home mosaic: the Lists face merges into the Notes face; Builder A updates the mosaic faces (236).
- Name: open, see below. The word wall, Tess's lines and the Home tile take the new name.

## The name (proposals, Oct 25; his pick pending)
What it must cover: notes and lists, private things, the word wall. What it must avoid: Apple's own app names
(Notes, Pages, Reminders, Journal), and anything that reads as a to-do product.
1. **Notebook** (recommended). Plain and honest: a notebook holds notes and lists on the same pages. "Private
   notebook" reads naturally; "a page in your notebook" is Tess's line. Door words: Pages · Lists · Private.
2. **Jotter**. Quicker, more personal, British. Jot a note, jot a list. Slight risk of reading as small.
3. **Paper**. Fits the app's paper look and the word wall; a list is "a paper". Collides with the theme name "Paper"
   inside Tessera (the paper/black looks), so the look would need renaming.
4. **Desk**. The room where you write things down; holds notes, lists and later clippings. More place than object.
5. **Scraps**. Warm, true to how notes start; wrong for a careful packing list.
