Tessera / Money / sheet 273

Three ways to see in and out.

Same month, same buttons, three different elements for the Money keystone. Each is drawn at the standard slot's shape. Press the buttons and watch all three answer: is more coming in or going out, where is it going, what is left.

1

Threads

Every 50 that came in is one fine thread entering from the left. Where money went, the threads peel away into a bundle for each kind; what is still yours runs straight on. A payment lifts one thread out of the straight run and lays it into its bundle. Thick bundles are where it went; the straight run is what is left.

2

Liquid

What is still yours is one soft sage body. A payment pinches a droplet off it; the droplet travels and melts into the body for its kind. Pay pours new liquid in. The sizes are the amounts, so you see at once what is left against what went.

3

Two lines

Across the month, what came in (sage) and what went out (ink) are two lines drawing themselves. The space between them is still yours. Every step on the out line takes its kind's colour. The day out crosses above in, you are spending more than you earn, and the space turns to a hatch. Last month's out line is dashed.
