Tessera / Money / sheet 275

Five ways to feel in against out.

Not a literal balance. Five other ways to show, at a glance, whether more is coming in or going out and where it went, all in type, all moving. Same month, same buttons; press a few and watch all five answer.

1

Afloat

What is still yours floats on a waterline. What came in hangs above it as balloons, pulling it up; where it went hangs below as weights, pulling it down, each word sized by its amount. Spend and it settles lower; spend more than came in and it goes under.

2

Tug of war

One rope. What came in pulls from the left, where it went pulls from the right, each team a stack of words sized by amount. The knot drifts toward the stronger side and the centre mark is even.

3

Rising water

The month's income is one huge number. Spending is water rising over it, in layers by kind. What is still above the water is still yours; past what came in, it goes under completely.

4

Charge

The month's name, lit like battery cells. As money goes out the light drains from the right, letter by letter, from sage to grey. No red: the charge itself is the warning.

5

Push

IN and OUT share one line. Each word takes width by its amount, so spending literally pushes what came in aside. OUT is striped in the colours of where it went.
