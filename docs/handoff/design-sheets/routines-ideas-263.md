## His steer (Oct 25)
"Doesn't have to be real, doesn't have to be that crazy graphic. It's more about the cool idea, the creative idea.
Keep the rendering simple and easy; the idea itself out of the box or creative." So the two generated brass plates
(`routines-plates/`) are shelved and this round is hairlines, dots and type.

## The five
1. **Record player.** Routines are on repeat. Record turns once a day; each routine a groove band, each step a mark at
   its hour; tonearm = now; played marks shine, skipped dull (needle jitters past); weekly record behind, 7x slower;
   side A morning, side B evening; drag the platter to look ahead. Tess is the DJ. (Recommended.)
2. **Day map.** Routines are places: a hairline floor plan (bed, kitchen, desk, gym, door, a pool only on Thursdays);
   you are a dot moving on schedule or on check-ins; a room lights when its routine is due; automated ones light
   themselves; sunlight crosses with the real hour; behind a door you are inside that room. (Most out of the box.)
3. **Polyrhythm.** Each routine a pendulum, length = period (dailies short and fast, weeklies long and slow); crossing
   the line = due; done bobs glow; the pendulum wave falls into step on days everything coincides.
4. **Sundial.** Flat dial + gnomon, shadow driven by the real sun (shared with Weather); routines are marks at their
   hour, shadow over a mark = time; weekly ring outside; after sunset a thin moon shadow.
5. **No picture.** One sentence in Tess's voice, Newsreader italic, word by word, changing through the day.
