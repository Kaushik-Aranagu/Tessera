# 261 Routines keystone, round three: the cat (v1, Oct 25; not yet reviewed)

Board: https://claude.ai/artifact/GGgy9Fx8xBWp2721Y64jFf (file `routines-cat-261/board.html`). After rounds one (woodland), two (paths,
sampler, beads) and his steer (life, expressiveness, fun, uniqueness, a personal relation; Tess drives the reactions),
he said "the animal thing has some potential". Built: ONE animal, a cat, living in the standard slot; your routines
are its routine.

## The cat
- A silhouette with eyes (ink-colored on paper, pale on black), so it stays graphic, never a cartoon. Eyes in gold
  with slit pupils, almond when content, round when alert; blinks every 3 to 7 s with a double blink one in five.
  Ears turn toward a sound (an event, a tap). Whiskers as hairlines. A tail of seven joints on a spring that follows
  the body, lazy when calm, flicking when alert or annoyed. It breathes; purring is a fine fast tremor and three
  faint arcs by the head.
- Its room, in the slot: a floor line, a door frame at the right, a windowsill at the middle, a bowl at the left,
  and a sun patch that moves across the floor with the real hour (gone after sunset).
- Postures: sleep (curled, eyes closed, a paw twitch when dreaming), sit, loaf, stretch (long and low, paws
  forward), walk (legs stepping), door (sitting upright facing the door, tail flicking), pace (by the bowl), belly
  (on its back, paws in the air).

## Your routines are its routine (by hour on the board)
Morning: asleep in the sun patch until the morning things are done. Stretch time: the long stretch. Day: a loaf on
the sill. Walk time: waiting at the door, facing it. Dinner: pacing by the bowl. Evening: the loaf by your chair.
Night: curled and asleep. Routines without a time show as small habits (a yawn, a wash, a look at you).

## It reacts to what you do (events on the board)
Finish something: a purr (a haptic you feel) and a slow blink. Back from the walk: it walks from the door to the
middle and settles. Not now: an ear flick. Three days without a routine: it sits facing away and glances back. All
done today: it rolls onto its back in the sun. A quiet week: it curls up small and sleeps more. Tap it: it answers.
Stroke it (a drag): it leans into the finger and purrs.

## Tess writes its lines
One sentence under the slot, Newsreader italic, arriving a word at a time, in her voice as the cat's: "The sun is on
this side until ten. Water, then we both get up." "You said walk at six. It is six." "Fine. I will look at the wall."
"I am not looking at you. I am looking at the wall near you." "Everything. Come here then." The cat's state is a
small vocabulary (place, posture, mood, attention), and the AI layer writes to it; the life is hers to run.

## Through the app
Behind a door the cat comes along (Body: stretching; Mind: by the shelf; Home: on the sill). On a routine's page it
does that routine's thing, small, above the steps. The slot is the standard 354 x 182.

## The relation
If he has or had a cat, it can be that cat: name, color (the silhouette tints), one habit. If not, Tess names it and
it becomes his over the weeks.

## Open
- Which animal: the cat recommended (a routine of its own that people recognize; indifferent as well as
  affectionate, so a missed day is a look, not a wound; unmistakable silhouette). A bird (parakeet on a perch) is the
  cheekier alternative; a dog reads as needy when things slip.
- How much face: eyes only (recommended). A mouth pushes it to cartoon.
- Rendering on the phone: SwiftUI Canvas with the same posture model, or a small rigged vector character; the
  silhouette approach keeps either cheap.

## Verdict (Kaushik, Oct 25): "I don't think this is working. We should try something else."
Three Routines rounds rejected: the woodland (scene with a caretaker), paths / sampler / beads (records of repetition),
the cat (a character). What he HAS liked across the app: the mosaic (pixels, a departure board, scenes), the word wall
(typography, color), the skypiece (a real sky, glass, weather getting in), the blackboard (chalk on slate), the pass
(paper tickets carrying live information, cooking run live). Pattern: real material + information written on it +
live state; characters and abstract records have not landed. Next: a different method (see HANDOFF, open items).
