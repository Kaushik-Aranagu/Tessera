Tessera / Money / sheet 270

Your money builds a town.

Pay arrives by train and is stacked as crates in your yard: that stack is what you have in hand. Every payment loads a crate onto a little cart that drives down the street, and it becomes a new floor on the building for that kind of spending. By the end of the month the skyline is your month: the tall ones are where the money went, and last month's outline stands faintly behind each building. Saving goes up the hill into a hot-air balloon that fills as you save and lifts off when you get there.

What it does

In arrives by trainPayday: a train pulls into the station and its crates hop into your yard one by one. A side job comes by bicycle.
Out builds the townA payment: a crate leaves the stack on a little cart, drives down the street, and lands on its building as a new floor with a puff of dust; the windows light.
The skyline is your monthMarket (food), gym (body), the tower block (home: rent, power, phone), the radio mast (subscriptions), the cinema (going out). Tall means that is where it went.
Last month, faintlyA dashed outline behind each building where last month ended. You see what grew and what didn't, and nothing is coloured good or bad.
Floors are the thingsEach floor is one thing (Groceries, Eating out). Tap a building and its floors are named.
Saving fills a balloonSaving goes up the hill and fills the Japan balloon. At the goal it slips its rope and floats up.
