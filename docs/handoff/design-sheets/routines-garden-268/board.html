What it does

Hold to waterHold a tree: a little rain falls on it for about a second and a half, it straightens, its flowers open. That is "done". No button anywhere.
Buds are stepsA five-step routine carries five buds. Tap the tree as you go and they open one by one. All open = done.
Missed days showThree days dry and the tree yellows, sags and drops leaves on the grass. No counter, no red.
Rake to let goSwipe along the grass under it: the leaves blow away. That is the "I know". The tree stays thirsty until you water it.
Plant in the clearingTap open ground to plant a routine. The caretaker walks over and sets a seed; three days kept and it is a small plant.
Rest under a lampA planned rest hangs a small lamp in the tree. It stops asking and keeps its leaves.
It grows with youSprout, small plant at 3 days, young tree at 7, tree at 28, fruit at 35. Weeklies are the slow pines at the back.
The world gets inThe real hour sets the light; Weather's rain, snow and wind arrive here; a bird visits a tree the moment you water it; fireflies at night.
Tess keeps the restThe caretaker by the lake tends the automated ones (Backup waters itself at 11) and Tess writes the one line under the slot.
