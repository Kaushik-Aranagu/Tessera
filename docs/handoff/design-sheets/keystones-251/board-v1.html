A centerpiece for every app.

Home has the mosaic, Notes has the word wall, Routines has the woodland. Each is made of the app's own material and comes alive with what you do. Here are two or three ideas for each of the other apps, drawn live in the mosaic's slot. Press the words under a sketch to make things happen. Tapping is optional: some could open a part of the app, but the point is the life in them.

What every keystone does (your rules)

Made of the app's own stuff.Pixels for Home, words for Notes, plants for habits. Not a picture of the app, the app's material.
Built from real data, and it scales.Ten things or two hundred: no drawing per item that breaks at volume.
Reacts, and lives on its own.Something happens when you log, add, finish or spend; something also drifts, decays or grows while you are away.
Alive first, a door second.Above all it has life: fun, expressive, showing something true about the app. Tapping into the app is a bonus, never the point.
Three to five seconds.Every reaction is over within five seconds. Reduce Motion gets a still frame.
No chart furniture.No bars, rings as gauges, axes or percentages. Pixels stay on Home only.
