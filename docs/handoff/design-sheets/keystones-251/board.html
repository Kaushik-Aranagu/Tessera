Tessera / keystones / sheet 251, round two

Ten small worlds.

Round one was charts in costume. The three keystones that work are each a little world with its own physics, made of the app's real material: pixels, words, plants. So this round is scenes. Each has a life of its own while you watch, and something happens when you do something. Press the words under a scene. Nothing here needs a tap to be worth looking at.

Kept from round one: Pull apart, as an opening move

Not a keystone. The move itself: tap a name and its letters come apart to reveal what is inside, then the screen opens through the gap. Below, the same move on three names. Tap each one. I would reserve it for one app so it stays special; Kitchen fits best (a dish pulls apart into its ingredients), Lists second (a list pulls apart into its things).
