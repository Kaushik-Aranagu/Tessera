How it works

The pass is permanent.The rail lives in the standard keystone slot (354 x 182 at top 196), the same slot as the Home mosaic and every other app; it never grows. Below 412 is the page: the landing’s doors, or Tonight, Pantry, Recipes, Groceries, Leftovers. Back is the edge swipe or the back word; the rail never leaves.
Live to the page.The rail shows the tickets that belong to what is below. Landing: the kitchen’s asks (dish, get, going out, dishes). Tonight: one big ticket for the dish. Recipes: two medium tickets for the dishes you can make. Groceries: one big list ticket, as many pages as it needs. Pantry: one ticket per place with its count. Leftovers: a ticket per thing with its day. Tickets that stay keep their place; the others slide away and the new ones slide in from the right, all in 0.55 s.
Dynamic size.Tickets share the rail width: four small, three, two, or one big. A big ticket holds about six lines, a small one three; what does not fit goes to the next page, never to a taller rail. Type is the kitchen hand (Caveat here; Reenie Beanie is the thinner option).
Pages.Swipe a ticket up and it flips over on its clip (rotateX, 0.65 s) to show the next page on its back; swipe down and it flips back. A page number sits bottom right. Flipping is only within one ticket; the rail’s set does not change.
Cook mode.Cook: the rail becomes one big ticket of steps with the running tally bottom left (“2 of 5” in hand). Tap or swipe right on a step, on the ticket or in the page below, and it is struck on the ticket as it happens; the ticket flips to its next page of steps by itself when the last visible step is done; the timer ticket beside it counts down and announces (“Rice, 2 min”). Finishing spikes the recipe ticket and writes a Dishes ticket with the new count.
Spike and finish.A fast swipe up spikes a ticket (done); the rest reflow. Open to decide: your “some other gesture” for finishing. Candidates: a hold (0.5 s) that lets the ticket tear off the clip, or a drag to the spike at the right. Recommendation: the hold-and-tear, since swipe up is taken by pages.
Life.Tickets flutter in the hood’s draft (slow sine, harder while cooking). New tickets slide in with a clip. Tess writes them all, in one hand.

Finishing gesture

Hold a ticket half a second and it loosens from the clip; let go and it tears off onto the spike. Swipe up stays for pages, swipe down for back. Recommendation: build this; it feels like paper.

How many tickets at most

Four small is the comfortable limit at 354 pt. Beyond that the rail pages: swipe the rail itself sideways to see the next four. Recommendation: cap at four and let the rail page.
