## The element
A needle-thin beam on a point and two bodies of liquid resting on it (metaballs, rendered per pixel at device
resolution, soft gloss, no outlines). Left, sage: what is still yours. Right: what went out, one fused body in which
every kind keeps its colour, side by side (Home clay, Japan gold, Food ochre, Going out slate, Body blue,
Subscriptions teal; a new kind such as a laptop arrives in its own colour). The beam leans toward the heavier body
on a damped spring: level when what is left equals what went out; tipped right once you have spent more than you
have left; when out exceeds in, the left is empty and a dashed hollow on the left is the shortfall, "OVER BY" in ink.
- A payment: a droplet pinches off the left, arcs over the point (colour shifting to its kind), joins the right; the
  beam swings. Pay: liquid falls into the left from above.
- Hold the right body: it pulls apart into one drop per kind, each named with its amount; let go and they flow back.
- Small numbers only: IN and OUT under the ends, STILL YOURS / OVER BY under the point. Dashed beam = last month.
- No words as weights (that was Notes); colour lives in the liquid and in the few small labels.

## Open
- Performance on device: the field is per pixel (CPU here); in the app it is a Metal shader (SDF metaballs), trivial.
- Haptics: a soft drip when a droplet lands; a firmer tick when the beam passes level.
- Inner pages: two lines (273) for In / Out over the month; tiles (271) for the month in time order.
