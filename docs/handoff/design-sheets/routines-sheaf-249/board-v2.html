What changed in round two (Oct 25)

No more menu line.The small-caps line with three words is gone. The sort is part of a sentence, "Sorted by grove", and the plum word rolls like a small dial: neighbors sit faintly above and below it, tap or drag turns it. The routine page uses the same roll for Steps, How it's going and About.
Sections look like sections.The open section's name is Outfit 200 at 40 pt in plum with its count, then one colored line on how it stands. Routines are Figtree 19 pt 500 rows with a second line for state and time. Two typefaces and two weights, so a row never reads as a heading.
Closed sections wait at the foot.Each is a quiet name (Outfit 300, 24 pt, ink2) with one short phrase at the right in its color ("two to tend"). The hairline edges are gone. The waiting names sit in a fixed place, so the open list never pushes them around.
Rows have room.Rows are 52 pt with the name on one line and its state and time on a second, smaller line. The section's first row starts 18 pt under its stand line.
Fold and deal.Opening a section is one move in 0.55 s: the old section's rows fold into its name from the bottom up and it sinks to the foot; the new name rises and grows; its rows deal out from under it one after another (35 ms apart) with blur clearing. Pulling a waiting name up follows your finger and dims the open rows as you go; let go past a third of the way and it takes over.
Regrouping keeps every routine.When the sort rolls, routines fly to their new sections; those that land in a waiting section fold into its name.
A routine leads with itself.Unchanged from round one: name, the whole life on one hairline, then Steps, How it's going and About, now on the roll word.
How it's going is a time ribbon.Unchanged: four weeks across, the day down, a stroke where and how long it happened. Pull it down to split it by step, or by weekday for a one-thing routine.

What goes on About?

Shown: when it happens and on which days, how long it takes, its grove, where it comes from, when it was planted, its size ceiling and who can see it. Recommendation: keep these seven, and make When, Days and Size editable in place with a wheel, so there is no separate edit page.

Does the ribbon replace the seven strokes everywhere?

The strokes still appear on the 248 wheel and in the Habits grammar. Recommendation: the ribbon lives only on the routine page; rows elsewhere show one state word and no strokes, so the sheaf stays calm.

Steps inside a step?

A routine can hold a routine (Evening stretch inside Wind down). Recommendation: allow one level only, shown as a step whose how-to line names its own steps, which opens that routine on tap.
