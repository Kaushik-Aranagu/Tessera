## His definition (Oct 25, kept close)
"Routines include all of these: my workout routine, my morning routine, evening routine, lunch routine, something
every afternoon, every week swimming or badminton. Whatever I'm trying to repeat: a repeatable action, a set of
actions or procedures which are looped. A habit or routine is basically the procedure itself. Where they happen:
physical, in the world, or digital, like journaling every night or things before sleep. Some could be automated;
whether a routine is automated or triggered by me is a design decision and a feature for later."
So: a routine is a LOOP with a PERIOD (daily, weekly, more) and a PROCEDURE (steps), some run by hand, some by
clockwork. None of rounds one to three showed a loop, or loops at different speeds. Mechanisms do.

## A. The orrery
A tabletop orrery in brass, seen from a chair (an ellipse). The center post and a small sun are NOW; a pointer at
the front marks it. Each routine is a body on its own arm: daily routines on the inner track turn once a day, weekly
ones (Swim Thursday, Badminton Saturday, Call family Sunday) on the outer track once a week; a monthly thing would
sit further out. A body comes to the front, under the pointer, when it is due. Done: it turns gold and polished for
the rest of its orbit. Passed and not done: it goes dark until it comes round again, and that is all; the next turn
is a fresh start. Steps are moons circling a body (a five-step routine has five). Automated routines wear a thin
brass ring and fire by clockwork with a chime (a ring of light); yours wait for your hand. The whole thing turns in
real time; drag to spin it ahead and see what is coming. Tap a body to open it. Names appear only on the bodies
at the front.
## B. The music box
A brass drum turning once a day, a comb of teeth beneath it, a winding key at the right, a slower weekly drum
behind. Each routine is a track along the drum with its name engraved; each step is a pin at its hour; the front
half of the drum is visible, with a hairline across it at now. A pin that reaches the comb plucks its tooth (the
tooth vibrates, a ring of light floats up) and is polished: done. A pin you skip passes silent and dull. A five-step
routine is a phrase of five notes; a one-thing routine, one note. Automated routines are pins the mechanism plays
by itself; yours wait, and you pluck them with a tap on the track. Tess winds the key. Drag to turn the drum ahead.

## Rules held
Standard slot; the mechanism stays through the app (behind a door only that loop or grove; on a routine's page its
one orbit or one track, large); no red, no streak counted aloud; a planned rest is a body or pin set aside with a
small ring; reactions under 5 s; the approved soft edge.

## Recommendation
The orrery if what Routines should put in your face is WHEN THINGS COME ROUND (daily and weekly loops side by side,
brass turning slowly, as calm as the skypiece). The music box if it is THE PROCEDURE (steps are literally pins and
playing them is the most satisfying way to do a routine). They can be one object: an orrery whose bodies, when they
come round, play their pins.
