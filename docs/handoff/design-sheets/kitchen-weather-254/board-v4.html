How it works

The glass, round four: a plateau.Subtler, and the edge flows into the screen. The flat top is the slot itself (sky at 1:1, 1% magnified, a faint sheen). The sides slope 26 pt outward, drawn as thirteen rings from the foot inward. Across the slope the bending follows a bell: nothing at the foot where it meets the screen, nothing at the top edge, up to 11% in the middle where the slope is steepest. Lighting follows the same bell: up to 20% white on the top-left, 20% dark on the bottom-right. A soft 1.5 pt crease where the top meets the slope. A shallow shadow (14 pt blur, 4 pt down) under the whole shape. No outer line, no caustic.
Live inside.Three numbers tick on their own: the wind (gusts within a range), the minutes until the next change (counts down), and the sun’s height in degrees (moves with the hour). The temperature rolls like an odometer when it changes.
The real library.A canvas drawing will always read as drawn. The real page needs plates: photographed or filmed skies, about 30 (clear, thin cloud, broken cloud, overcast, rain, storm, fog, snow, each at dawn, day, dusk and night), the sun and moon composited by position, particle rain and snow on top, a grade per look. I can start that library the moment a few sky photos land in the repo folder; this round only improves the stand-in: noise cloud fields lit from the sun, horizon haze, film grain, a quieter sun.
The pull-apart layer.Tap: the page scales to 97% and blurs 18 pt with a 52% page tint over 0.5 s. The word’s letters start where they were, then travel to the layer’s headline and spread 34 pt per step over 0.7 s on a spring. The body rises 24 pt and fades in, 0.3 s behind the letters. Close reverses it in 0.4 s. Reduce Motion: no blur, cross-fades only.
Where the move lives.Level 1 (a section's list: Recipes, Pantry places, Groceries) and level 2 (a dish, a place). Never on the landing, which keeps the keystone like every other room.
