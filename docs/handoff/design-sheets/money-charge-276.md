## v1
The month's name is the battery (OCTOBER = 7 cells; May 3, September 9). Lit part (sage, breathing, glow on black) =
still yours. Drained part = striped in the kinds' colours (Push folded in), oldest spend at the far right, newest next
to the light. A hairline under the word = the month; a mark = month left; the sage run on the line = money left.
Reading: STILL YOURS n · "49% left · 55% of the month left" · "lasts to the 27th at this pace" (burn rate).
Moves: payment drains with a flicker, the hit letter jolts, the name floats up; pay = a sweep and the letters hop;
empty letters sag; overspent = the word slumps and dims, OVER BY in ink; tap a letter = what that slice was; hold =
every stripe named. No red.
