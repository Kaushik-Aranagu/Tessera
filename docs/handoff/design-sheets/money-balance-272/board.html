The balance.

One hairline, balanced on a point. On the left sits what came in this month; on the right, where it went, each kind of spending as a word set at the size of its amount. The line tilts toward whichever side is heavier, so you can see at a glance whether you are spending more than you earn, and the biggest word on the right is where most of it went. Every payment drops onto the right and the line dips; pay drops onto the left. A dashed line is last month.

What it does

The tilt is the answerLevel when in and out match. It leans left while more has come in than gone out, and right the moment you spend more than you earn. You read it before you read any number.
Word size is weightEach word's size follows its amount (by area), so the biggest word is where most of the money went. The exact amount sits under it.
Payments drop inA payment falls onto the right as a small word, lands on its kind, and the kind swells to take it in; the line dips and settles on a spring.
Pay drops on the leftSalary, a side job, a refund: the word falls onto the left and the line swings back.
Open a wordTap Food: its things hang under the line on threads, Groceries and Eating out, each sized by amount. Tap again to fold them away.
Last month, dashedA dashed line at the angle last month ended on. Under each kind, last month's amount, small.
