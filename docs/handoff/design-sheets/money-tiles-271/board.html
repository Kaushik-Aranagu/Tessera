Tessera / Money / sheet 271

The month, in tiles.

One tile is 10. Everything that came in this month is the grid; every tile still sage is still yours. Each payment flips the next tiles to the colour of where it went, so the grid fills like a calendar of your spending. Every tile belongs to a real payment: tap one and its whole payment lights up; drag along the grid to scrub through the month. Swipe up and the tiles fly into blocks by kind, with last month's size in hollow tiles; swipe down and they go back to time.

Use it

Tap a tile to see its payment. Drag along the grid to scrub the month. Swipe up to sort.

What it does

In adds tilesMoney in drops new sage tiles onto the end of the grid in a ripple. The grid is always exactly what came in this month.
Out flips tilesA payment flips the next sage tiles, one after another, to its kind's colour, with a flash. Coffee flips one; rent flips a hundred and fifteen.
Every tile is a paymentTap a tile: all the tiles of that payment light up and the line under the grid says what it was and when. Drag across to scrub through the month payment by payment.
Sort by kindSwipe up: the tiles fly into blocks, one per kind (Home, Food, Body, Subscriptions, Going out, Japan), with what is left in sage at the end. Swipe down: back to time.
Last month, hollowSorted, each block shows last month as hollow tiles where it went further. Accountability at a glance, nothing coloured good or bad.
The colours are the kindsHome clay, Food the kitchen's ochre, Body the training blue, Subscriptions teal, Going out slate, saving gold, still yours sage (sheet 241).
