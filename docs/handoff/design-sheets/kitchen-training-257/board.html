The chalk slate. The slot is the slate by the rack, where your numbers are written in chalk by hand: the bests, large; today’s sets as tally strokes under the date. Every set you log leaves a chalk handprint somewhere on the slate and a puff of dust that drifts down. A new best gets the old number struck and the new one written bigger, with more dust. Marks fade over the days; on Monday a towel wipes a band clean for the new week. The slate is never empty for long if you train, and it goes grey and quiet if you don’t.

How they work

The same surface rule as the skypiece.Both slots are lifted by the approved cues only: a soft light off the top-left edge, a soft shadow off the bottom-right, a hairline inside the edge. No frames, frost or tint on the surface itself; the window’s steam and the slate’s chalk are contents, not treatments.
The window shares the Weather plates.Outside the pane is the same plate the Weather room would show for this hour and condition, drifting at the same slow rate. Two rooms, one sky. Rain beads on the outside; the kitchen’s steam is on the inside.
Steam is a state.Cooking raises a steam level over 30 s (from the bottom, denser low); it falls over two minutes after the stove is off. Drops form where the steam is dense and run. Tess’s finger writing clears the fog along the stroke (Caveat, 28 pt, revealed 60 ms a letter) and fogs back over 40 s. What she writes: what ran out, the pot’s minutes left, a note for the morning.
Chalk is a state.Each mark has a time; alpha fades with age (demo: 90 s = a week). Bests are written with a two-stroke chalk look (a soft wide stroke under a crisp one, both with grain). A handprint is five short strokes and a palm smudge at a random place and angle. Dust is 30 particles that fall and fade in 2 s. The Monday wipe is a towel band moving across in 1.2 s that clears marks older than a week.
Information in both.Window: tonight’s dish and time, what ran out, the timer. Slate: every best by lift with its date, today’s tally, the streak as weeks ticked. Tap a number to open that lift; tap the window to open Tonight.
Reactions, all under five seconds.Start cooking: steam begins. Ran out: Tess writes. Kettle: a breath. Log a set: handprint and dust. New best: strike, rewrite, dust. New week: wipe. Reduce Motion: states apply at once, no drift or dust.
