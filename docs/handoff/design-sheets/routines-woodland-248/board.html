What came back from the Codex chat

The scene is the keystone."Scene is my biggest takeaway." Each app gets one living scene built from its own data, like Home's mosaic and Notes' word wall. Habits and routines are the first scene: a woodland.
Plants are habits.New habits are small plants; consistent ones become trees. Importance sets a size ceiling (small plant, shrub, small tree, canopy), so a steady small habit can stay a shrub.
Life stages.Sprout, small plant on day 3, medium on day 7, tree on day 28, first fruit on day 35 (one week after the tree stage). Today's completion adds a bloom; growth already earned never shrinks.
Withering and bare.Withering: three days in a row missed, or three missed within seven days. Sparse leaves, some still falling. Bare: eight days in a row missed, no leaves at all. One completion cannot restore a week with too many misses. Replanting starts a new cycle. A planned rest keeps foliage and quiets the plant.
The caretaker.He rests by the lake and walks out to plant a new habit, water a finished one or collect fruit, then walks back. Every reaction is five seconds at most (1.65 out, 1.7 working, 1.65 back). Any effect in the app stays under five seconds.
Layout.Up to 12 to 14 plants show in the wide view, built from one bed to clusters to a rear grove. The center stays a clearing until it fills. Neighbors never share a color family; blue only at the outer edges; background trees stay faint. Every plant is reachable by tap; groves and the index cover the rest (24 routines tested).
Completion.Tapping a routine opens it; swiping or holding completes it, with a short petal bloom. No "Open routine" or "Mark done" buttons.
Rejected in the chat.Big tree icons inside lists, tabs and pills, long lists, a stage legend ("New 3, Mature 2"), a percentage with bars, and cramming a whole grove on one screen. He wants a calm view that moves between dimensions on dials and swipes.

How it was redrawn

Pills and tabs became a dial.All, Body, Mind, Daily life and Index are now doors on the three-place dial (48 to 56 pt slots, neighbor blur up to 1.8 pt, none under Reduce Motion). Moving the dial moves the woodland toward that grove.
The legend became the sentence."Two done, eight to tend, one to begin again." Each big phrase has its own color (green, room plum, wine), so the big-type rule holds. States use the app-wide colors: done green, needs care wine, new and fruit gold, rest slate.
No percentage, no bars.The 77% and its bars are gone. A routine's week is seven short strokes, solid for kept days and a dashed guide for days ahead, the grammar the Habits page already uses.
Tree icons left the lists.The woodland is the only place trees are drawn. Rows are words: name, one state word, one line.
Actions follow Tessera.Swipe right is Done, swipe left is Not now, undo lasts 10 s. The bottom-left word starts something (Plant) and follows the focus inside a grove. Tess sits bottom right. No door rows at the foot.
Soft words for hard states.Withering reads "needs care", bare reads "begin again", rest reads "resting". No "missed" and no red banner. The tree itself carries the state.

Open, with a recommendation

Which groves does Routines use?

The chat used Body, Mind and Daily life. The Routines landing rulings used Mornings, Evenings, Body, Home, Mind and Borrowed from other rooms. My recommendation: groves are Body, Mind and Home, and Borrowed from other rooms is a small grove across the lake. Mornings and Evenings are times of day, so they stay as filters on Today rather than groves.

Does withering fit the no-shame rule?

He asked for withering and bare trees himself, and his ask wins over the older "nothing is owed" ruling. My recommendation: keep his thresholds, keep the words soft, and let a planned rest and Small days protect a tree from withering. That last part is not yet decided with him.
