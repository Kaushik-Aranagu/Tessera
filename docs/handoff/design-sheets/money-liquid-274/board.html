Tessera / Money / sheet 274

The balance, in liquid.

A needle-thin beam on a point, and two bodies of liquid resting on it. Left, in sage: what came in this month. Right: what went out, one body in which every kind keeps its own colour. The beam leans toward the heavier side. Every payment is a droplet that leaves the left, arcs over the point and joins the right, and the beam swings. Hold the right body and it pulls apart into its kinds; let go and they flow back together.

What it does

The lean is the answerLevel when in and out are equal. It leans left while more has come in than gone out, and tips right the moment you spend more than you earn.
A payment is a dropletIt pinches off the left body, arcs over the point and lands in the right body in its kind's colour. The beam swings and settles on a spring.
Pay pours inSalary, a side job, a refund: liquid falls into the left body and the beam swings back.
Pull it apartHold the right body: it separates into one drop per kind (Home, Japan, Food, Going out, Body, Subscriptions), each sized by amount and named. Let go and they flow back.
Last month, dashedA dashed beam at the angle last month ended on. Under the ends, in and out; under the point, still yours.
Nothing to read firstTwo colours of liquid and a lean. The numbers are there, small, for when you want them.
