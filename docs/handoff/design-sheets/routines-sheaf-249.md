# 249 Routines: the Sheaf and the routine page (v1, Oct 25; not yet reviewed by Kaushik)

Board: https://claude.ai/artifact/JYEiJ5Jk9jWN9f4GLbdFac (file `routines-sheaf-249/board.html`). The 248 board is
republished at https://claude.ai/artifact/KxUP3btRNCLLRenstEySuJ (the old link belongs to another account).
Builds on 248 and its Oct 25 verdicts: Sheaf for the list behind a door; the routine page keeps the whole life, but
leads with the routine itself (steps, details), and trends get a new form; groves Body, Mind, Home plus Across the lake;
withering with soft words, protected by a planned rest or the Small way.

## The Sheaf (behind a door, and "All routines")
- Top: back word, title 38 pt plum, the dimension line (By grove, By state, By time), a hairline. Sideways on the line
  turns it.
- Groups: By grove = Body, Mind, Home, Across the lake. By state = Needs care (care, begin again), To tend (to tend,
  new), Fruit, Done today (done, resting). By time = Morning (before noon), Afternoon (before 5 pm), Evening, Any time.
- A closed stack (76 pt): name 28 pt 600 ink with its count in Outfit 300 14 pt ink2; under it one stand line, up to
  three phrases in role colors ("Two to tend, one needs care, one to begin again"); at the right, the edge: one 1.5 pt
  hairline per routine (up to 12), 3 pt apart, each 3 pt shorter than the one above, in that routine's state color.
- Open stack: name turns plum, the edge hides, the sheets fan into 44 pt rows (name 18 pt 500; right side the state
  word 12 pt in its color and the time in Outfit 300 12 pt ink2). The other stacks close up to 52 pt (name 20 pt, edge
  only, no stand line).
- Pull: dragging a closed stack's header fans its sheets live (150 pt of pull = fully open), blur easing from 1.8 pt
  to none. Let go past 50 pt and it opens; otherwise it settles back. Pull up 30 pt on an open stack to close it. Tap
  toggles. Sheets fan with a 28 ms stagger; everything springs in 0.5 s.
- Regroup: every sheet is one persistent view, so changing the dimension moves sheets to their new stacks (0.5 s).
- Rows: tap opens the routine; swipe right = Done (strike, 7-petal bloom, then it moves to Done today); swipe left =
  Not now; Undo 10 s. Bottom word: Plant (Collect or Begin again when the last touched sheet has fruit or is bare).
- Reduce Motion: no stagger, no blur, no petals; stacks swap in place.

## A routine
- Fixed block on every sibling page: back word (its grove), name 38 pt ink, then the whole life on one hairline
  (ticks at sprout, day 3, 7, 28, 35; marker in the stage color; only the current stage is named: "Fruit, day 36").
  Wine marker and word when it needs care, slate when bare.
- Dimension line: Steps, How it's going, About. It opens on Steps.
- Steps, a run of steps: "5 steps, about 9 min left" (13 pt ink2); the current step 24 pt 600 plum with its how-to
  (15 pt ink2), minutes in Outfit with "min" at 10 pt; the others 19 pt 500 rows of 44 pt. Tap or swipe right checks
  (strike 260 ms, 0.6 s wait, drops under a slate "Done" label). Swipe left = Not now (moves to the end). Bottom word:
  Start, Next step, Do it again. Finishing all steps gives the petal bloom and "<name>, done. Sixth evening running."
- Steps, one thing: one sentence in plum 26 pt ("A full glass before coffee."), its how-to (16 pt ink2), and when it
  usually happens. Swipe right or the bottom word Done checks it; it turns green and reads "Done today at 7:02 am."
- How it's going: the count in Outfit 200 64 pt with its words at 22 pt ("26 evenings kept in four weeks"), one Tess
  sentence (Newsreader italic 19 pt), then the TIME RIBBON: 28 days left to right; top to bottom is the time of day
  (the routine's own range, 30 min margin). Each kept day is a 3 pt plum stroke starting at its start time, as long as
  it took (3 pt per minute, at least 5 pt). A planned rest is a slate tick at the foot. A miss draws nothing. The usual
  time is a dashed hairline. Labels: "four weeks ago", "usual 8:10 pm, dashed", "today". No axes, grids or numbers.
- Pull apart: drag the ribbon down (or tap) and it splits into one ribbon per step (54 pt each), so skipped steps
  show as gaps; a one-thing routine splits by weekday (Mon to Sun, 36 pt each). One Tess line says what the split
  shows ("Hip opener is the step that drops first"). Pull up to join it again.
- About: rows of 44 pt, slate small-caps label left, value right: When, Days, Length, Grove (link), Comes from (link
  to the source room, or "Made here"), Planted (date and day), Grows to (size ceiling), Who sees it.
- Colors per page: plum, the state color of the routine, slate, ink. Green appears only for done.

## Open (with recommendations)
- About: keep these eight rows; When, Days and Grows to edit in place with a wheel, so there is no edit page.
- The ribbon replaces the seven strokes on the routine page only; rows elsewhere carry one state word, no strokes.
- Nested routines: one level only; a step can be a routine, shown with its own steps named in its how-to line, and
  opens that routine on tap.
- Nothing here is approved yet.

## Round two (Oct 25, after his notes; board v2, same link; v1 kept as `routines-sheaf-249/board-v1.html`)
His notes on v1: the horizontal menu needs a better form; rows inside a section look too much like the next
section's title; drop the hairline edges on collapsed sections; better spacing inside a section; keep "sections, only
one open, pull up another", but better transitions and closed sections shown differently.
These replace the Sheaf section above:
- Sort: no dimension line. A sentence under the title, "Sorted by grove" (17 pt ink2), where the last word is a roll
  word: plum 600, its neighbors faint above and below (scale .72, 40%, 0.8 pt blur). Tap moves to the next; drag up
  or down turns it. Values: grove, how they stand, time of day. The routine page uses the same roll word (20 pt) for
  Steps, How it's going and About, placed under the life line.
- Open section, always one, at the top (186): name in Outfit 200 40 pt plum with its count in Outfit 300 15 pt ink2;
  the stand line (13 pt, up to three colored phrases) at +48; rows start 18 pt below it.
- Rows: 52 pt. Line 1 the name, Figtree 500 19 pt ink (ink2 when done or resting); line 2 the state word 12.5 pt in
  its color, then the time in Outfit 300 12.5 pt ink2. Two faces and weights keep rows apart from section names.
- Waiting sections: fixed at the foot, ending at 790, 44 pt each: name Outfit 300 24 pt ink2, and at the right its
  most pressing phrase in its color ("two to tend"). A small-caps ink3 "Pull one up" sits 20 pt above them. No edges.
- Fold and deal (0.55 s): the open rows fold into their name (bottom row first, 18 ms apart; scaleY .4, blur 2 pt,
  fade) as it sinks to the foot; the new name rises and grows 24 to 40 pt; its rows deal out from under it, 35 ms
  apart after 90 ms, blur clearing. Pulling a waiting name up follows the finger and dims the open rows; past 60 pt it
  takes over, short of that it settles. Tap does it in one move.
- Regroup: routines keep identity and fly to their new sections; those landing in a waiting section fold into it.
- Reduce Motion: no stagger, blur or petals; sections swap in place.

## Round three (Oct 25, after his notes; board v3, same link; v2 kept as `routines-sheaf-249/board-v2.html`)
His notes on v2: drop "Sorted by", the selection alone is enough; rows leave too much empty space, use the right
side or change the layout; give the waiting sections a fixed part of the screen that scrolls when they don't fit,
and work out how scrolling and pulling live together; decide what happens when the open section's list overflows,
how you see there is more, and how swiping to complete fits with scrolling.
These replace the matching parts of round two:
- Sort: the roll word alone ("By grove", "By how they stand", "By time of day"), 15 pt, right-aligned on the back-word
  row at the top right; neighbors faint above and below.
- Regions, fixed: the open section's name at 134 (Outfit 200 40 pt plum + count; stand line under it); its list
  region 214 to 673; a hairline at 694; the shelf at 708. The foot word and Tess stay below.
- Rows (54 pt), two-sided: left = name (Figtree 500 19 pt) over what it is (13 pt ink2: "5 steps, about 9 min",
  "one thing, 30 min", "from Kitchen"); right = time (Outfit 300 18 pt, am/pm at 10 pt) over the state word (12 pt,
  its color). Done and resting rows turn name and time ink2. "any time" in Figtree 14 pt ink2 when there is no time.
- Overflow: the list scrolls inside its region; the name and stand line never move. The region height (459 pt) is
  8.5 rows, so the last visible row is always cut in half. That is the only "more" signal: no counter, no fade.
- Shelf: the waiting sections in one row (Outfit 300 22 pt ink2, the most pressing phrase under each in its color),
  30 pt apart, scrolling sideways when they don't fit; a name cut at the right edge shows there are more.
- Gestures, by axis (the first 8 pt of movement locks it): on the shelf, sideways browses and up pulls a name open
  (it follows the finger, the open rows dim and blur; past 50 pt it opens); a still tap opens. On rows, vertical
  scrolls the list, right past 56 pt is Done, left is Not now, a still tap opens the routine. Undo 10 s.
- Fold and deal through the shelf: the old rows sink into the shelf bottom first (18 ms apart, scaleY .5, blur);
  its name drops onto the shelf; the new name lifts and grows 22 to 40 pt; its rows rise one after another (35 ms
  apart after 120 ms). Opening a section resets its scroll to the top.

## Round four (Oct 25, after his notes; board v4, same link; v3 kept as `routines-sheaf-249/board-v3.html`)
His notes on v3: a little less spacing; no horizontal shelf, keep the waiting sections vertical, with a sideways swipe
to move through them and pull up to bring one into focus; tapping a phrase in the stand line ("one needs care")
should filter the list to just those.
These replace the matching parts of round three:
- Rows 48 pt: name Figtree 500 18 pt over what it is (12.5 pt); time Outfit 300 17 pt over the state word (12 pt).
- Regions: open name at 128 (Outfit 200 38 pt), list 196 to 652 (9.5 rows, so the last row is always cut), hairline
  at 660, shelf at 670.
- Shelf, vertical: three 40 pt slots; each waiting section is its name (Outfit 300 21 pt ink2) left and its most
  pressing phrase right, in its color. More than three: a sideways swipe on the shelf turns to the next three (the
  names slide out with the finger and the next three slide in); ink3 11 pt "Swipe across for two more" sits right
  above the hairline. Up pulls a name open (past 50 pt); a still tap opens.
- Stand line filter: each phrase under the open name is a filter. Tap "one needs care" and only those routines stay
  (the rest sink into the shelf); the chosen phrase goes 600 and the others drop to 45%. Tap it again to show all.
  Changing section or sort clears it.
- By how they stand now has six sections: To tend, Needs care, Begin again, Fruit, Done today, Resting.

## Verdict (Kaushik, Oct 25): round four of the Sheaf works. APPROVED as the list behind a door. The routine page continues in sheet 250.
