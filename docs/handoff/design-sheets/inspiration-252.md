# 252 Inspiration gallery (Oct 25; not reviewed)

Board: https://claude.ai/artifact/VdZzZGr35NRtzkSRoyXjqm (file `inspiration-252/board.html`).
His brief: no app in mind; general ideas of cool effects, ways of putting text together, UI element effects, to see
what inspires, relate things to each other and keep building. Nothing needs to be right first time.

Tiles, all live:
- Words: Breathing weight (variable weight wave); Letter rain (a sentence assembles from falling letters); Liquid fill
  (an outlined word fills like a glass); Ripple (letters ripple away from the finger); Odometer (Outfit digits roll
  only as far as needed); Lamp (the finger is a lamp over dark text); Depth stack (one word at three depths tilting
  with the finger); Text on a path (a sentence circling forever); Handwriting (a line writes itself in one stroke).
- Moves: Fold (one row folds down to show what is under it); Elastic rows (rows stretch and snap like rubber);
  Strike that drips; Accordion (rows unfold hinged on the one above); Dust off (rub dust off a panel to read it);
  Pull apart (kept from 251); Undo pendulum (ten seconds as a pendulum slowing to a stop); Shuffle to sort (rows
  lift, slide and drop so the eye can follow).
- Textures: Paper grain; Heat shimmer (the running thing wavers like hot air); Hairline web (hold a thing and
  threads reach everything related); Embers (instead of confetti; petals, leaves, sparks or snow by room); Time of
  day (the ground takes the hour's color).
