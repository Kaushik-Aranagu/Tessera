Tessera / Money / sheet 269

In, and out.

Money's whole story is what comes in and where it goes. Read it left to right: income pours in from a pipe; this month's money sits in the jar on the shelf; every payment runs out along a glass tube and drops into the vessel for its kind of spending, where it stays for the month in bands for each thing. Saving drops into a honey pot. A faint line on every vessel marks last month, so you can see at a glance what grew and what didn't. Nothing is coloured good or bad.

What it does

In pours inPay, a refund, a side job: water gushes from the pipe into the jar, bubbles rise, the tag counts up the month's "in".
Out runs outA payment opens the tap; a slug of water runs along the glass tube to its kind and drops into that vessel, which glows as it fills. The name and amount float up and fade.
Kinds, and the things insideHome, Food, Body, Subscriptions, Going out: each vessel holds this month in bands, one per thing (Rent, Electricity, Phone). Tap it: the bands are named.
Saving becomes honeySaving runs down the same tube but drops into the honey pot, turning gold as it falls.
Last month, faintlyA dashed hairline on every vessel: where last month ended. Accountability without judgement; amounts never turn red or green.
What is leftThe jar on the shelf is what is still in hand: in minus out. Tap it for the month's in and out.
