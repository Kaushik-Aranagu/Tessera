# 251 Keystones for the other apps: brainstorm round 1 (Oct 25; not reviewed)

Board: https://claude.ai/artifact/7YWsi9KqwgSUkGQprBBV5S (file `keystones-251/board.html`). Linked prototype of
everything so far: https://claude.ai/artifact/1dH25PYRWRd3TU7LAacNHc (`prototype/`).

His brief (Oct 25): every landing gets a centerpiece like Home's mosaic, Notes' word wall and Routines' woodland:
fun, sophisticated, expressive, made of the app's own material, alive. His correction mid-round: it does NOT have to
lead into the app or need taps; life, fun and expression come first, showing something about the app's data.

Rules carried from his Codex chat: built from real data and scales; reacts to what he does and lives on its own;
every reaction 3 to 5 s; Reduce Motion gets a still frame; no bars, gauges, axes, percentages; pixels only on Home.

Sketches (each live in the 354 x 160 slot, with event buttons):
- Training: Strata (weeks as rock layers, sessions as split-colored lenses); Plate towers (a tower per lift, plates
  sized by weight, gold for a best, drop with bounce); Iron filings (each lift a magnet, its pull = recent training,
  filings swing on a logged set, pulls fade by themselves).
- Kitchen: The loom (places as woven bands, things as threads aging pine to ochre to wine, a shuttle weaves
  groceries in); Steam (each dish you can cook tonight is a ribbon of steam, broken where something is missing);
  Pull apart (tonight's dish in big letters pulls apart into its ingredients, missing ones in wine).
- Lists: Receipt strips (each list a hanging paper strip as long as what is left; checking tears a piece off);
  Origami (each check folds the sheet; the last one turns it into a paper boat that sails off); Knots (lists as
  threads between pegs, a knot per thing, sagging with weight, tightening as things are checked).
- Weather: Horizon (the next twelve hours as one window, distance is time); Window numbers (the temperature with
  the live weather seen through its letters); Wind field (strands drifting with the wind, warm amber to cool slate,
  turning to rain streaks).
- Money: Reservoirs (pools as basins, pay pours in, spends drip into a sediment, moves run through channels, a due
  bill is a closed gate); Engraving (a banknote rosette from the month, each kind of spending a family of curves).

First recommendations: Plate towers (Training), The loom (Kitchen), Receipt strips (Lists), Horizon (Weather),
Reservoirs (Money). Waiting for his picks and his own ideas.

## Verdict on round one (Kaushik, Oct 25)
None of the fourteen clicked. They read as charts in costume. The one keeper is **Pull apart**, not as a keystone
but as an OPENING MOVE: tap a name and its letters come apart to open the screen behind it. Use it wherever opening
a section feels fun, probably reserved for one app so it stays special. Round two follows below.

## Round two (Oct 25; board v2, same link; v1 kept as `keystones-251/board-v1.html`)
Ten living scenes, two per app, each with ambient life and 2 to 3 events: Training: The forge (lifts as iron bars,
a smith hammers on a set, quenches on a best, rust when neglected), Night sky (sessions as stars by lift, bests
bright, a shooting star on a best, dawn on training days). Kitchen: Fridge poetry (magnet letters spell what is in
the house; a word drops off when it runs out, new letters fly in with groceries), Lids (pots as dishes you can make;
lids rattle when ready, sit ajar when something is missing, flame under tonight's). Lists: Dominoes (a run per list,
checking tips one; a finished run lies flat with dust), Abacus (a rod per list, beads slide right with a click).
Weather: The yard (grass, a washing line, a puddle, all from the real weather), Storm glass (crystals grow when a
front is coming). Money: Candles (a candle per bill burning down to its due day; paying blows it out), The printer
(a receipt printer prints every movement; the pile is the month). Plus Pull apart as an opening move on three names.
Mid-round he widened the brief: not per app; a general gallery of effects, text treatments and UI moves to spark
ideas. That is sheet 252.

## Verdicts after round two and the gallery (Kaushik, Oct 25)
- KITCHEN: Pull apart is the theme of the whole Kitchen app. Section names as large text; tapping one opens it in
  place (or close to it) through the pull-apart transition. He also said "something about refrigerator, something
  that could help" for Kitchen: keep Fridge poetry as an open idea, not picked. Continued in sheet 253.
- WEATHER: the whole page carries the weather, like the Apple weather wallpaper: the sun where it is in the sky and
  how bright; the moon in its real phase along its rise-to-set arc; clouds when cloudy; thunderstorms with random
  lightning; rain, fog, snow. The mosaic slot shows plain information (temperature, numbers, small details, played
  with typographically). The rest of the page: weather details and care nudges (things to do because of the weather).
  Continued in sheet 253.
- Everything else on 251 and 252: not picked (nothing else sparked).
