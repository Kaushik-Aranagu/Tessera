What it does

The line is your positionWhat is still yours, from the open on payday to today. Every payment is a tick down; income is a tick up. The line draws itself when the page opens.
The dashed line is the forecastFrom today at your current pace, with the bills you already know about placed on their dates as the drops they will make. The close on the 31st is the number that matters: what you end the month with.
Last month behindFaint, the same chart for last month, so you see whether you are running above or below it without reading a number.
Volume, by whereBars under the chart: what went out each day, stacked in the colours of where it went. A heavy day is a tall bar; a quiet week is flat.
Scrub any dayDrag along the chart: a hairline follows your finger and reads that day, past or future ("the 22nd: 1,090 after Electricity").
Two small tickersJAPAN (savings) and PORTFOLIO as sparklines in the corner, each with its figure for the month. Tap to open. The portfolio moves slowly through the day.
A payment ticksThe line drops at today with a short flash, a bar grows, the forecast re-draws, the close updates. Under a second.
No red, no greenStill yours in sage, forecast in ink, the kinds in their own colours. Up and down are directions on the chart, not judgements.
