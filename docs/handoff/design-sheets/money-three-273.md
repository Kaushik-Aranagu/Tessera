# 273 Money: three ways (Oct 25; superseded by 274 the same hour)

bundles), liquid (still yours a sage body; payments as droplets merging into kind bodies), two lines (cumulative in vs
out across the month, gap = still yours, hatch when out crosses). His note while it was being built: "the idea is there
with balance, but the execution and the means should be rethought." The liquid was the cleanest material and unused
elsewhere, so 274 puts the balance in liquid. Two lines kept as a candidate inner page (In / Out over the month).
