# 275 Money: five ways to feel in against out (Oct 25; for his pick)

## His words (on 274, the liquid balance)
"No, the word thing was so much better than this. But it's the literal use of a balance itself that's throwing me off.
Maybe show that balance in other ways: something floating or drowning in water, a tug of war between things... We
could also use colour, like a battery, how low or high we are, positive or negative. Think of all such options."
So: keep type (272) as the material; drop the literal beam; find the metaphor.

## The five (one board, same month and buttons)
1. Afloat. Still yours floats on a waterline; income words hang above as balloons on strings; kinds hang below as
   weights into the water. Spending sinks it; overspent, it is under the water ("UNDER BY", greyed).
2. Tug of war. One rope; income words lean back on the left, kinds on the right; the knot drifts to the stronger side
   past a dashed centre mark; IN / OUT under the ends; STILL YOURS / OVER BY above the knot.
3. Rising water. Income as one huge thin numeral; spending rises over it as water in kind-coloured layers; above the
   water is still yours; overspent, the number is fully under and the water reaches the top.
4. Charge. OCTOBER as seven cells, filled sage left to right by the share still yours; drains from the right with a
   flicker on the cell being drained; outlined when empty; "% LEFT". No red (sheet 241 rule kept; the charge is the
   warning). Kinds as a small line below.
5. Push. IN and OUT as two heavy words sharing one line, widths by amount; OUT clipped into stripes by kind; a hairline
   divider; overspent, OUT has pushed IN past the middle.

## Notes
- Colour as an indicator (his battery idea) is in 4; the sheet 241 rule "no amount coloured good or bad" holds by
  using fill level and grey, not red/green. If he wants a warmer warning, that is a rule change to raise with him.
