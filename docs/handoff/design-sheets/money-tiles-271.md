# 271 Money keystone: the month in tiles (v1, Oct 25; not yet reviewed)

Board: https://claude.ai/artifact (money-tiles-271/board.html; link in the session handoff). Renders in `money-tiles-271/renders/`.

## How we got here (his words, Oct 25)
- Soul of Money: "the amount of money getting in and the amount getting out... accountability... how much is coming in,
  how much is going out into various things, savings or expenses, and expenses into categories."
- Rejected on the way: the jar (269 v1), the in/out flow with a pipe, tube and vessels (269 v2), the town (270): "too
  cliche"; then the rule: "something creative like the wall of words or mosaic or weather... functionally cool...
  not trying to paint pictures or create a scene. Software features, software cool things." 269 and 270 are kept in the
  repo, unreviewed and set aside.

## The idea
One tile is 10. The grid is exactly what came in this month; sage tiles are still yours. Each payment flips the next
sage tiles to its kind's colour, in a run with a flash, so the grid fills like a calendar of the month. A blinking
outline (a caret) sits on the first tile still yours. A slow light passes over the sage tiles.
- Every tile is a payment: tap one and every tile of that payment lights (others dim); the line under the slot says
  what, how much, when, where. Drag across the grid to scrub through the month payment by payment.
- In: new sage tiles drop in at the end in a ripple (a side job, a refund, pay).
- Swipe up: the tiles fly (staggered, 1.1 s) into blocks by kind, whole columns each: Home, Food, Body, Subscriptions,
  Going out, Japan (saving), then still yours. Hollow tiles show where last month went further. Labels under each
  block: kind, this month, "last N". Swipe down: back to time order.
- Under the grid in time order: IN, OUT, STILL YOURS (Outfit numerals). No legend.
- Colours (sheet 241): Home clay, Food kitchen ochre, Body training blue, Subscriptions teal, Going out slate, saving
  gold, still yours sage; light and dark tokens. Nothing coloured good or bad.

## Open
