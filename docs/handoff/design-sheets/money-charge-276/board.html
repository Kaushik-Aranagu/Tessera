Tessera / Money / sheet 276

The month, charged.

The month's name is the battery. Its lit part is what is still yours; the part that has drained is striped in the colours of where it went, so one word holds in, out and where. Under it, a small mark shows how much of the month is left: if the light runs behind the mark, you are spending faster than the month is passing, and it says when the money would run out. The letters react: they flicker as they drain, hop when pay recharges them, sag when they are empty.

Use it

What it does

Lit is still yoursThe word's light, in sage, is the share of this month's money you still have. It glows a little and breathes.
Drained is where it wentThe drained letters are not empty: they are striped in the colours of where the money went (Home clay, Japan gold, Food ochre, Going out slate...), widest where most went.
The month is the yardstickA mark under the word is how much of the month is left. Light behind the mark: spending faster than the month. It says when the money would run out at this pace.
A payment drainsThe edge of the light slides back with a flicker, the letter it hits jolts, a new stripe appears in its kind's colour and its name floats up.
Pay rechargesA sweep of light runs left to right and the letters hop up one by one as they fill.
Empty letters sagA letter with no light left drops a little. Spend past what came in and the whole word slumps and dims, "over by" in ink. No red anywhere.
Tap a letter, hold the wordTap: what that slice of the month was. Hold: every stripe is named with its amount.
Every month is a different batteryMay has three cells, September nine. Same rules, different word.
