### 1.0 The woodland (248)
- **What it is:** the Routines keystone (see 00 Read me first). Plants are habits. Consistent habits grow into trees, new ones are small plants, several species and sizes. It is a living scene built from the Routines data, in the Home mosaic's slot, and it is also how you move around Routines.
- **Life stages:** sprout day 0, small plant day 3, medium plant day 7, tree day 28, first fruit day 35. Flowers = done today. Fruit = a completed week, collectable. A nest on trees at 30 days. Importance caps size (small plant 32, shrub 46, small tree 68, canopy tree 104 prototype units), so a steady small habit can stay a shrub.
- **Withering:** 3 days in a row missed, or 3 missed within 7 days = sparse canopy, leaves falling. 8 in a row = bare, no leaves. One completion can't restore a bad week. A planned rest keeps the foliage. Replanting starts a new cycle. UI words stay soft: "needs care", "begin again", "resting"; never "missed", "dead" or red banners.
- **Caretaker:** sits by the lake; walks out to plant, water a finished habit or collect fruit, then back. Whole reaction ≤ 5 s (1.65 out, 1.7 working, 1.65 back).
- **Scene layout:** lake in the central clearing (quiet silver-jade, reflective wavelets), middle empty until the forest fills, 12-14 plants in the wide view, every plant tappable, none hides another, neighbours never share a color family, blue only at the outer edges, tree shape and color independent, background trees faint, the scene fades into the page color in Paper and Black. Slow clouds, birds visiting new trees, butterflies, evening fireflies.
- **Landing (402 x 874):** plum title; one Tess sentence of three big colored phrases ("Three done, eight to tend, three to give a little care.": green, plum, wine); the woodland in the mosaic slot (402 x 182); one caption line names a tapped tree and its stage, a second tap opens it; the 3-place dial (Today, Body, Mind, Home, Fruit, Across the lake) with the focused door's three most urgent routines beside it; turning the dial pans the woodland to that grove; foot word "Plant", Tess at right.

## 3. Measure and body weight

**Measure** is the engine's one units system (Core). It is a set of rules, not a room.
- Settings > Units: "Weight, kg/lb", "Distance, km/mi", "Food, grams/cups" (189). Changing a unit rewrites every number shown and **never** a stored value. A sample line shows the effect: "Bench 82.5 kg is 182 lb." Temperature follows the phone, and money currency is set per pool.
- **Unit rule (Oct 24):** every measured number shows its unit at about half the numeral's size, minimum 10 pt, in Figtree 400, joined by a no-break space. Core splits number and unit.
- **Unit turn:** tap or swipe the unit word to cycle that quantity's units. The digits roll and the unit word swaps (138; current rule: no fades, so it clips).
- The hung fraction is one role smaller, in ink2.
- After a change by hand, the old value shows as "was 80" in ink3 for 2 s. Never on money.

**Body weight page** (body-weight-page 29; behavior stands, look old). Reached from Training (the body link) and from Review.
- Title "Body weight". Then a context line: "78.1 yesterday, 0.3 kg less than last week" (the rate only after 7 days of entries).
- The hero is the **trend** (one decimal, Outfit, unit at half size), never the raw weight.
- A fine trend line from the first entry to today, with each entry as a small mark. There are no axes, goal line or BMI. An out-of-range entry is pinned at the edge with its value, never red.
- The change line: "1.2 kg less since Sep 3", "0.8 kg more", "The same as on Sep 3". Never "lost", "gained", "good" or praise. There is no color for direction.
- **Logging:**
  - "bw 82.4" or "weighed 82.4" commits right away.
  - A bare "82.4" (30-250 kg, nothing running) waits as a ghost, "Body weight 82.4 kg", until tapped. Tess asks "82.4 kg as today's weight?" with Log / Not now.
  - The trend step is capped at 2 kg.
  - If the entry is more than 3 kg off the trend, it notes "4.2 above trend".
  - A second entry the same day replaces the first.
  - Mid-workout, a bare number is never a weigh-in: it goes through the live-workout guard.
- Copy: "Body weight logged. 82.4 kg." Undo: "Body weight taken back."
- Empty: "No weigh-ins yet." in ink2, with no hero and no line.
- **Privacy:** body data never reaches widgets, backups beyond the local store, or logs. Off the phone, Tess and Claude lines follow the lab/strict policy.
- Old colors to drop: wet cobalt and sage. Use the Training blue accent and go green only for a logged moment (unconfirmed).
