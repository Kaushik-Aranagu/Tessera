# New on your phone: release f8bb516e

Release 10 is the next build to install. Everything below is new since 4757aef, the build on your phone now (release 3).

## Training and rest
- **Your rest weekday** now shapes the week. The first launch sets Sunday, and the Training week marks it as rest, today and ahead. Past weeks keep the rest day they had, even if you change it later. It applies to a rolling plan, not fixed days.
  Try: open Training and look at Sunday in the week.
- **Say it to Tess** in the Ask bar: "rest day friday" or "make friday my rest day". She says "Friday, rest day." and it is a setting, never a task called "Rest day". Saying the same day again changes nothing and has no UNDO.
  Try: type "rest day friday", then UNDO.
- **A planned workout beats rest.** If a session is set for today, Now and Home no longer say "Rest day" beside the START key.
- **Set receipts** drop the e1RM and say "best" when it is one: "Bench press 82.5 × 8, logged, best." On the workout screen: "New best: 85 × 5." and "Set 3 logged. 80 × 8." With no set number it reads "Set, logged."
  Try: log a heavy set from the Ask bar.
- **A workout finished with no sets** no longer shows as a session in the Day log.

## Review and Home
- **Review's foot line** ("Review", "Notes") now sits above Tess's band, not beside the soul.
- **Weekday names** in Review are always English, whatever the phone's language.
- **Exercise trends** count calendar days, so a clock-change night no longer adds or drops a day in "N days".

## Plans and routines
- **Tess's line for a routine change** now appears in her band beside the soul, with UNDO, like every other page. Examples: "Legs B, dropped." (UNDO says "Legs B, back on the plan."), "Push A, taken back."
  Try: swipe a routine left on the Routines page, then UNDO.
- **UNDO on "made"** takes back both steps and closes the empty new routine if you are on it.
- **A swipe no longer opens the row** when you lift your finger.
- **The routine editor** leaves room at the bottom so a row never rests under Tess's band.

## Private things
- **Opening a note from a link** opens it in the note editor, as from Notes. A private note stays on a page that shows only "Private note", also in "Linked from" and in Contents.
  Try: open any note from a link.
- **A workout's notes** read "Note kept private" on its object page.
- **A task with money** loses the amount: "pay rent 2100 fri" is saved as "Pay rent" for Fri. A medicine reminder is saved as "Private task" and Tess says "Task added for Fri." (her exact line for the rent task is not checked on a phone yet).
- **A Groceries or list line** keeps the thing and drops a price ("milk $4" is milk). A line that is only money, a secret or your weight becomes a Private note ("Private note, kept"). A medicine line reads "Private item, added to Errands".
- **A bare number near your weight**, like "is 82.4 good?", is now treated as your body, not saved as a task. Asked as a question, it stays on this phone and Tess says so in her own short line (exact words not checked yet; a question with a secret word says it is in your private notes). A lift number is still a lift when a set is in focus.
- **Times in task lines** drop the "at": "Call mom, tomorrow 6:30 pm".

## Quietly safer
Walls only: Claude calls no longer follow redirects, Home's done-workout and Plan my day wording drop any title that looks private, and Tess will not keep your weight as a memory.

## Not on screen yet
- The first-launch "Sunday, rest day." receipt (with UNDO) is written but has no screen. You see its effect in the week.
- Home's checks on Tess's done-workout lines, the task story ("planned" versus "moved"), and the buy rhythm line "You buy it about every N days." are merged but nothing shows them yet.
