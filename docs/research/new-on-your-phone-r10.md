# New on your phone: release f8bb516e

Release 10 is the next build to install. Everything below is new compared with e149e66, the build on your phone now (release 8). Checked against the code that differs between the two; nothing here was run on a simulator.

## Training and rest
- **Your rest weekday** now shapes the week. The first launch sets Sunday, and the Training week marks it as rest, today and ahead. Past weeks keep the rest day they had, even if you change it later. It applies to a rolling plan, not fixed days.
  Try: open Training and look at Sunday in the week.
- **Say it to Tess** in the Ask bar: "rest day friday" or "make friday my rest day". She says "Friday, rest day." and it is a setting, never a task called "Rest day". Saying the same day again changes nothing and has no UNDO.
  Try: type "rest day friday", then UNDO.
- **A planned workout beats rest.** If a session is set for today, Now and Home no longer say "Rest day" beside the START key.
- **A workout finished with no sets** no longer shows as a session in the Day log.

## Review
- **Weekday names** in Review are always English, whatever the phone's language.

## Lists and tasks
- **A list line said back** puts the thing first: "Milk, added to Errands". A private line reads "Private item, added to Errands".
- **A task with money** loses the amount: "pay rent 2100 fri" is saved as "Pay rent" for Fri, and Tess says "Pay rent, Fri." If the saved title is still private (a medicine reminder, say), she says only "Task added for Fri."
- **A number beside a lift name stays a lift.** "weighed squat at 140 kg" is a bar, not your weight. A morning weigh-in like "82 kg this morning" is still your weight, even if an exercise called "Good Morning" exists.

## Quietly safer
Walls only: Claude calls refuse every redirect. Home's done-workout wording drops any title that looks private. Your weight, money or a long number beside set notation like 5x5 is still walled.

## Not on screen yet
- The first-launch "Sunday, rest day." receipt (with UNDO) is written but has no screen. You see its effect in the week.
- A new set of checks on Tess's lines is merged but nothing shows it.
