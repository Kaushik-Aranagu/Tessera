## Team appendix: the full list

What changes when the next build goes in, compared with build 7b321d2 (installed Oct 16, 9:57 am PT).
Trimmed to the pin a36b161 on Oct 16 (Pacific). Every line below is in that build.

### Home
- **Glass tabs:** hold the tab row and a glass lens forms under your finger. The pages shrink side by side, and letting go opens the one under it. A tap still works.
- **Home lines:** one plain line per thing ("Push A, paused at bench", "Tacos done"), nothing cut short, and the day word follows your 4 am day.
- **Review** is your week on one page: a band each for training, tasks done and cooking, then the week's lines. Tap a day to read just that day; tap again for the week.

**Training and the live workout**
- **START** wakes the page, the rest of the plan sinks away, and the live workout grows out of the first exercise.
- **The live page** has a context line on top ("Push A, set 1 of 4"), the name never cut off, and LOG on the key. Last time is said once, and skipping rest reads "Rest skipped. Set 3 is up."
- **Training's Tess line** stays quiet when today's target is the same as last time.
- **Routines:** make one from a writing line, hold and drag to reorder, swipe left to drop. Switch between ROLLS ON and FIXED DAYS, and give each routine its days.
- **Bests** stay on the day you set them, even after a later session beats them.
- **Apple Health** now gets workouts you finish early, too.
- **Trips:** on your Oct 26 trip, each workout, task and set stays on the day it had where you were, and times Tess reads with no zone are taken in yours.

**Tess and the Ask bar**
- **Your typing** sits one size under Tess's voice, in the Ask bar, Notes and paste.
- **Tess working:** when she waits on Claude she fades and splits into twelve faint lights, then gathers and says Done. You'll see it first on the key check in Review, under Claude.
- **Holding Tess** with the mic off now settles her back to rest instead of leaving her listening.
- **The tab lens** no longer gets stuck after you leave one of Tess's questions unanswered.
- **"pay rent fri"** makes a task again, and "pull-ups" is known as an exercise.
- **Undo** after an empty workout is wrapped up brings it back.

**Notes**
- **Secret words** (password, PIN, code, wifi) make a private note. The Day log just says "Private note".

**Kitchen**
- **Groceries** from a recipe keep their pack size (" · 15 oz"), two can sizes stay two rows, and salt, oil and pepper count as staples. "For serving" lines stay on the recipe. Prep words like "diced" drop off, and the aisles use US words.
- **Pasted recipes** leave out any line that looks private, and it's never saved.
- **The cook clock** isn't in this build yet.

**Review: Claude and looks**
- **The Claude cap** reads $50 a month (it was $10).
- **Looks:** Porcelain, Plein, Celadon and Lacquer are recut, each its own material. Plein is flat warm paper now. Obsidian is unchanged.

**Siri**
- **Add a Task** refuses your Claude key, and "door code" with a number becomes a private note.

**Privacy**
- **Your Claude key** typed or pasted into a note is refused. Tess says it goes under Claude instead.
- **Secret words** stay private notes on the phone. They never reach Claude, Tess's memory or the miss log.
- **Money and body facts** ("$212 at Costco", "182.4 this morning", "knee hurts") never go to Claude, even with "Tess asks Claude" on. Tess still reads them on the phone.

**Your data.** Catalog v9 only adds fields; nothing you have is changed. Save a copy first (Review > Backups, SAVE A COPY). If you need to go back, the rollback build is 7b321d2, installed over the top. Don't restore a copy saved on the new build onto 7b321d2. This build won't open a copy from a newer Tessera; it asks you to update first.
