## Part 2. Ruling 5:40 stamp G94 plus 3:43 PM, REPLACED by the one rule of 4:20 PM PT (1): the quantity rule

There are two different places a leading number is read on master, and the ruling reaches only one of them. Rows are split accordingly.

**The one rule, ruled 4:20 PM PT (1), clarified 4:25 PM PT** (replaces the G94 wording and the 3:43 PM fold-in; decisions.md lines 951, 955): a first line starting with a number or fraction is an AMOUNT when the number is followed by a unit word at any length ("2 cups flour"), or when the line is exactly two words with a lowercase second word ("2 eggs"); "2 Eggs" and "5 Spice" are amounts too. Longer lines ("3 eggs benedict", "7 Up Cake") are TITLES. Hyphen rule (4:25 PM PT): a hyphenated first line is an exempt title only when the hyphen joins a number to a letter word ("30-Minute Dal", "5-Spice", "5-Ingredient Chili"). A digit range ("2-3 eggs") and a mixed number with a unit word or pack size ("1 14.5 oz can") are AMOUNTS. This is the old Q5 (a to c) answered; (4) repeats that "3 eggs benedict" is a title.

### 2.1 What exists on master

**Paste title rule (what G94 and 3:43 PM change).** `RecipeParser.classify` (`TesseraCore/Sources/TesseraCore/Kitchen/RecipeParser.swift:41-70`): line index 0 is the title when `!startsWithAmount(line)` and `!isSentence(line)` (`:53`). `startsWithAmount` (`:171-175`) is true when the first character is a number (`clean.first?.isNumber`) or the line opens "a/an" plus pinch, handful, bunch, clove, can, tin, slice, sprig.
