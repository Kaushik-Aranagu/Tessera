## 1. The rulings, as testable sentences

| Ruling | Test sentence |
|---|---|
| 9:17 PM G257 | Bare words never mask. "Dr" or "rent" alone stay raw as a row and as a list name. A row or list masks only by the one existing check: a title plus a name ("Dr Patel"), or an amount or sign for money. "Dr Pepper" stays raw. |
| 9:17 PM G258, 9:18 PM items 1 and 6 | Groceries Done, Clear the basket and Lists Reset (and cook Done, Finish workout, Finish task) are named-action-only for VoiceOver: double tap speaks the hold hint, the named action completes, UNDO covers it 10 s. Keys that start something (Start shopping, timer start, Cook) keep double tap. Voice Control "Tap Done" and Switch Control activate the Finish action directly, never a dead control. |
| 9:17 PM G250 | One shared Tess line with UNDO on every full-screen layer (Groceries with items in the basket, Lists), one app-wide UNDO slot. |
| 9:17 PM G249 | His own touches keep their haptic in Low Power and in quiet hours. Only Tess-initiated landings follow quiet hours (23:00 to 07:00, fixed). Weights: check and uncheck light, Done and Clear success, Reset medium. |
| 9:17 PM G247 | In Low Power the zoom into a screen is a 150 ms crossfade (D5: Low Power = Reduce Motion everywhere, 150 ms crossfade, no drag spring). |
