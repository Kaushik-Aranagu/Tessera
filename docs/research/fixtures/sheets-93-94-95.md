## 4. Contradictions: between the three sheets, and with earlier decisions

1. **Sheet 93 vs page-turn PG07/PG08.** Sheet 93's four pages hold 3 rows each. PG07 says small neighbours share a page (at most two groups, 6 rows or fewer), so pages 1 and 2 (3 + 3) would merge, and pages 3 and 4 as well, giving 2 pages, not 4. Either Settings pages are authored (not run through the page build, SP03) or the merge rule needs a "named page" exception.
2. **Sheet 93 vs settings-80 and claude-settings.** Sheet 93 puts only "Tess asks Claude" on page 1 and drops everything else Claude: the spend meter and month line, key line with TEST, REPLACE and REMOVE, "Misses go to Claude", WHAT WAS SENT, receipts, the walls line, the Backups prose line (D2). No page holds them. Sheet 93's "stays off until the key is there" also reads against `missesGoToClaude ?? true` ("typing the key opts in", Oct 15).
3. **Value words.** Sheet 93 says plain "On"/"Off" and no "Enabled/Disabled"; settings-80 and settings-eyes use "yes"/"no" ("Tess asks Claude: yes", "Tess has eyes: no"); claude-settings cycles "Claude" and "only this phone". Sheet 93 also drops "Tess has eyes" and "Misses go to Claude".
4. **"A row never wraps" vs AX sizes.** Sheet 93 says the value shortens and the name never wraps; settings-80 says from the first accessibility size the count drops under the name and nothing truncates. SP30 follows settings-80; Design to confirm.
5. **Sheet 94 vs the Lists screens already built (sheets 9 and 10).** Master's shelf has Hero/Title/Body tiers, capitals state words ("3 OF 7", "REUSABLE") and running-first order; sheet 94 has uniform Body 21/500 rows, a count at the right, groups, Groceries first, and no "reusable" word on the shelf. Four decisions at once (tiers, state word, order, reusable) would be replaced.
6. **Sheet 94 vs R1.** "A section of more than six continues on the next page under the same word" does not say how it splits; R1 says nearest even break (7 as 4+3). The shelf text says the same for groups; the list-page text does not. Treated as R1 (LV21); confirm.
7. **Sheet 95 vs the 12:35 PM Oct 17 decision and `heroText`.** The decision keeps the "Title, done" shape ("Private workout, done"; "Workout, done" when empty); sheet 95 makes the hero the title alone and moves "done" into the strike. Master tests `V9WalkLowsTests.swift:28-43` pin "Push Day A, done", "Workout, done" and the no-break glue, and would change.
8. **Sheet 95 vs Tess's `done` haptic.** Sheet 95 says one `.success` haptic; master plays a three-tap Core Haptics pattern (`DonePattern`) through Tess. Sheet 95 also says "nothing else buzzes", which drops the pattern or changes what `onGathered` fires.
9. **Sheet 95 vs Tess's count line.** Sheet 95: "Private workout, done." (thing first, comma); master: "Private workout done, 5 of 5 exercises." (`doneLine(in:)`, QA #781/#787). Two forms for one moment.
