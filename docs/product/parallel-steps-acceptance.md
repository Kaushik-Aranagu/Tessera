## Open, defaults taken

1. **Does the 10% check need a total line?** The backlog says the clock lands within 10% of the written total "when there is one, else the parser shows the sum and marks nothing parallel". D1 and D2 as pasted carry no total line, yet the fixture's lane column gives them lanes. Default taken: cue words alone open lanes; the 10% check runs only when a total line exists (PS10).
2. **The join, not the guest, decides the next step.** Default taken (PS1, PS2): a cue-less step starts when all live tracks have ended, never at a short guest's end.
3. **Third candidate waits for the earliest end.** Default taken (PS7).
4. **Fixed-width waits host nothing.** Default taken (PS9).
5. **The key row** reads the clock's length less minutes elapsed (join based). Sheet 69's "33 MIN LEFT" and "25 MIN LEFT" stay one-lane numbers until lanes ship; with lanes D1 reads 31 at minute 12.
6. **A held host stretched by "5 more minutes"** shifts every later step by 5 and restarts only its own timer; a guest already running is untouched (default taken).
7. **Which track is current** is the one last touched; with neither touched, the host's (default taken).
