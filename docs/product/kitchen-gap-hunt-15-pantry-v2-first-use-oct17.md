## What master is today (the baseline for "first use")

Pantry v2 is not built on master. There is no stored `state`, `stateAt`, `lastBoughtAt` or `usualSkippedAt` field (SystemCatalog.swift:280-284 gives Ingredient only `aisle` and `isStaple`), no `pantryBackfilled` key, no `PantryBook`, no backfill, no "out of" rule in TessGrammar+Kitchen.swift, no CAN COOK word (grep finds none in Features or Core), and the catalog is still version 9 (SystemCatalog.swift:350). Pantry today is the app-side `PantryLedger` (PantryLedger.swift:10-136), which derives state live from shop clears and finished cooks. So a person who updates today to a v2 build gets: catalog bump, new fields, one backfill from that same derivation, then the derivation is deleted (spec line 45). Every row below about v2 is spec-level unless it names master code.

## Part C: first week, in order (what a person meets)

1. Update and first launch: backfill runs (G199, G201, no copy before it G200). Pantry shows last week's OUT and LOW from history, private lines as "Private item" (G198 for the sticky ones). A fresh phone sees "Nothing yet".
2. First open of Pantry: Tess says the last cook line (G212); no hint that a phrase exists (G208).
3. First phrase: `Milk, out. On Groceries.`, 4 s; a private word reads "Private item, out." (G213 for the log).
4. First Finish shop: everything checked is `in`; if nothing is OUT or LOW the page is bare (G207). Recipes' CAN COOK un-greys on one state (G209).
5. First cook: unknown lines become low or out (G210); no way back to unknown (G211).
6. Share or save: the shared export must carry none of it (G196, G197); the iCloud folder copy needs a ruling (G206).
7. Reinstall or go back: restore resets the marker and loses told states (G205).
