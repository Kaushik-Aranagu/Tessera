A walled thing reads "Private item", "Private recipe" or "Private note". Tess's lines are cobalt in her band; screen words are ink. UNDO is 4 s for a new capture, 10 s for a change or a remove, and a flat 10 s under VoiceOver. No row, fixture or log holds a key or key-shaped text.
