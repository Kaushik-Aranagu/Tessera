# Kitchen first week: acceptance rows for gaps FG1 to FG14 (K86)

*Kitchen lead, board K86, 2026-10-17. Docs only, no code. One row per gap in section 9a of `kitchen-first-week-script.md`, in order: K86.1 is FG1 through K86.14 for FG14. Each row gives Given / When / Then, the exact copy, the owner and the first-week step it fixes. Where a ruling exists the row applies the latest dated one and names it. Where none exists the line is marked "proposed, Product to rule" and the script's default stays in force until it is ruled. Two gaps are wording conflicts (FG5 and FG11); FG11 could not be settled from the rulings and sits under "Unsure" at the end, with no line chosen.*

**House copy.** Receipts name the thing first and use "on" ("Milk, on Groceries."). No "at" before a clock time. Clocks are lowercase ("8:30 pm"). Digits for counts. US English. A walled thing reads "Private item", "Private recipe" or "Private note". Tess's lines are cobalt in her band; screen words are ink. UNDO is 4 s for a new capture, 10 s for a change or a remove, and a flat 10 s under VoiceOver. No row, fixture or log holds a key or key-shaped text.

**Owners.** Taken from the owner tables in `kitchen-gap-hunt-2.md` (Triage): Core owns engine behavior and receipt wording that comes from the engine (the G6 to G8 and G11 pattern); Builder B owns the Kitchen screens, Home lines, Lists and Pantry (the G9 and G10 pattern, "Core, then Builder B"). Builder A owns widgets and the training week (`kitchen-gap-hunt-3.md`, `kitchen-gap-hunt-4.md`), and no gap here lands on Builder A.
